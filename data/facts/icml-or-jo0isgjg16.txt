Implementation Details In this section, we provide a detailed description of the XR-1 framework, focusing on the architecture and training of the dual-branch VQ-V AE. Dual-Branch VQ-V AE To achieve a unified latent representation, we introduce a dual-branch VQ-V AE consisting of two complementary encoders, a vision encoder and a motion encoder, that map their respective modalities into a common discrete codebook. Vision Branch Encoder.We adopt SigLIP (Zhai et al., 2023) as the backbone vision encoder, comprising approximately 400M parameters. During training, all parameters in both the SigLIP backbone and the dynamic module remain fully trainable.
