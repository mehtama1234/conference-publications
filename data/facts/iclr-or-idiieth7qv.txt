It assigns a vector space to each cell and a linear restriction map from each higher-dimensional cell to each of its faces, reflecting the hierarchical structure of the complex. In such models, the sheaf structure enables a principled generalization of message-passing architectures by allowing node features to propagate through edge-level transformations governed by linear restriction maps.
