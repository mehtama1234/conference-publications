Driving-World Model To endow the VLM with long-horizon spatial perception and future-state anticipation, we train the model to predict latent semantic features F= [f 0, f1, f2, f3, f4] for consecutive future frames in the BEV space. To achieve human-like efficiency in predictive modeling, we introduce Qworld as a set of learnable queries to the model. This enables the parallel inference of motion states across multiple future time steps within a single forward pass. Unified Training Strategy To ensure the model better understands trajectory generation, we encode trajectory waypoints intoaction tokensbased on their pixel-space coordinates in the BEV grid. This encoding allows us to compute a Cross-Entropy (CE) loss between predicted and ground- truth tokens, as both are represented in the same tokenized space.
