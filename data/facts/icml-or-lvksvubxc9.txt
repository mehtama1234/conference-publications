Moreover, LoRA-FA simplifies LoRA by freez- ing A and training only B, cutting trainable parameters by half while maintaining competitive performance. Moreover, beyond param- eter discrepancy, the variance induced by sampling stochas- ticity also contributes to the training error, yet such methods fail to account for it in their initialization strategies. In this work, we propose a data-aware LoRA initialization method grounded in asymptotic analysis.
