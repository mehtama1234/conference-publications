All architectures considered in this work are parallelizable through associa- tive scan algorithms (Blelloch, 1989; Martin & Cundy, 2018; Smith et al., 2023), enabling efficient computation of hidden states across the entire sequence. The complete block described by Equation (25) constitutes a sub-layer SubLayer(·) that is integrated into the broader backbone architecture. The architecture interleaves recurrent layers with MLPs, skip connections, and normalization. Normalization and skip-connection layers are applied point- wise and following a pre-norm scheme, i.e., y=υ⊙x+ SubLayer(Norm(x)), where υ∈R m is a vector of learnable parameters, ini- tialized to unity. This nonlinear encoding and decoding design ensures that single-layer recurrent architectures are not limited by in- sufficient point-wise nonlinear computation capacity, but rather by the recurrent mechanism itself.
