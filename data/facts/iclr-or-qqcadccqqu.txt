Across 11 LVLMs (both proprietary and open source), we find that even state-of-the-art models struggle with multi-step cartographic reasoning. Overall accuracy remains essentially un- changed in the contextual setting, indicating that retrieval and disambiguation are not the primary bottlenecks; the core difficulty lies in cartographic reasoning itself. In FRIEDA, we design questions to assess core map-reading competence while mirroring realistic document use, where a reader may need to navigate a document to locate the relevant map(s).
