Task-Vector Guided Importance Estimation A critical limitation of attention-based importance metrics (e.g., H2O, EMLoC) is their susceptibility to “attention sinks”—tokens with high attention scores but low semantic value (e.g., punctuation). We propose that true semantic importance is defined not by generic attention, but byalign- ment with the task transformation direction. We compute the semantic centroids for the Question and Answer representations at layerlas: shallow layers process local, high-frequency features (syntax, edges), while deep layers process global semantics.
