Building on these insights, we introduce SlotGCG, a pioneering approach that represents the first systematic exploration of positional vulnerabilities in adversarial token insertion slots. By identi- fying and exploiting these vulnerable positions, our method launches targeted optimization-based attacks that significantly enhance jailbreaking effectiveness. Our method offers a universal position- discovery mechanism that is independent of specific attack strategies and can be easily integrated into existing optimization-based frameworks with just a single inference step.
