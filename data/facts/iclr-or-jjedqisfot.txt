To address this issue, recent methods such as Linear attention (Katharopou- los et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2024b) approximate or eliminate the softmax operation to explicitly compute the entireN 2 attention matrix, thus reducing memory usage and achieving linear-time complexity.
