Inspired by previous works (Zhang et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Alayrac et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023), we freeze the original pre-trained parameters ΘD of the cross-attention layer ψD to preserve generalized capabilities and introduce a parallel, trainable copy Θl.
