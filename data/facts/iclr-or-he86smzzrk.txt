Our approach also relies on object-centric representa- tion points, but rather than predicting them via learned networks, we utilize VLMs to perform stable and robust object segmentation, followed by two heuristic strategies for anchor selection: we structure the action plan based on a one-shot, fine-labeled demon- stration, then adapt it using language-grounded object anchors and motion optimization techniques. For instance, rather than mim- icking the exact poses or insignificant diversities involved in pouring water, our approach focuses on capturing and re-instantiating the relative spatial relationship between the cup and bottle, empha- sizing coordination rather than absolute motion.
