We conducted a unified analysis of initialization and aggregation noise of our method, comparing with state-of-the-art heterogeneous LoRA-based FFT methods. For aggregation, local model modules from smaller ranks are zero-padded, and the resulting global LoRA module is subsequently truncated for client LoRA module initialization. While this aims for optimal aggregation, FLoRA then applies this update to the client’s target module, and the trainable LoRA modules for the next round are typically initialized from scratch, thereby incurring significant initialization noise.
