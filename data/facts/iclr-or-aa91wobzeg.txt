Different from existing work (Romano et al., 2020a; Zhou & Sesia, 2024) which directly extracts groups from the raw input featureX, our approach encodesXinto a latent rep- resentationZvia a mappingZ=f(X), and learns unfair groups characterized by the low group coverage based onZ.
