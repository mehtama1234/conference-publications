To identify hidden Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs), various causal discovery methods have been developed, including constraint-based (Spirtes & Glymour, 1991; Spirtes, 1995) and score-based (Cooper & Herskovits, 1992; Chickering, 2002) approaches. However, these methods typically identify only up to an equivalence class, failing to distinguish causes from effects without additional assumptions. Functional Causal Models (FCMs) are a class of Structural Causal Models (SCMs) that impose con- straints on causal mechanisms.
