Unlike IDM-K, which jointly encodes entire past and future trajectories, our method simply rearranges the (observation, action) sequences by shifting the observation window forward byτsteps. Our pipeline first curates long-form gameplay uploads with permissive li- censes, retrieves them at 20 Hz, and converts the frames intoScreenevents so they can be fed through the same tokenizer used for human demonstrations. Building on this, we train the Generalist-IDM using the InternVL3-1B (Zhu et al., 2025) architecture with theNEP-τobjective.
