Combined with a splatting-based renderer and a topology-control strategy, our method can be optimized in about 20 minutes to achieve accurate surface reconstruction while substantially improving mesh quality. Despite these advances, volumetric methods inevitably rely on a meshing step that can produce overly dense meshes and accumulate errors. To address the shortcomings that volumetric methods rely on error-prone meshing while surface-based methods lack sufficient 3D con- text and have a single-layer receptive field, we bridge the gap by differentiably converting surfaces into a pseudo-volumetric representa- tion.
