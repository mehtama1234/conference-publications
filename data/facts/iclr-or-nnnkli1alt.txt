Foundational results on hyperbolic embeddings (eg, Poincaré embeddings) show strong benefits for symbolic data with latent trees (Nickel & Kiela, 2017), and early hyperbolic neural architectures extend core layers and classifiers to the Poincaré model for language tasks (Ganea et al., 2018).
