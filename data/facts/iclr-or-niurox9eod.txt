A key training detail is the target LLM layer k to read activations from and the decoder LLM layer ℓ to write activations to. we read from the middle layers because they contain the most semantically-rich representations (Ghandeharioun et al., 2024b) and we write to the 0th layer because we want to provide our decoder with as many steps for processing the activation as possible.
