Abstract Learning-based whole-body controllers have be- come a key driver for humanoid robots, yet most existing approaches require robot-specific train- ing. In this paper, we study the problem of cross- embodiment humanoid control and show that a single policy can robustly generalize across a wide range of humanoid robot designs with one-time training.
