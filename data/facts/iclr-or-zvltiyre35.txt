Our approach.To address the challenges of MCES, we proposeNeural Graduated Assignment (NGA) – a novel neuralized optimization framework designed to approximate MCES solutions in polynomial time. Inspired by annealing mechanism in statistical physics (Gold & Rangarajan, 1996), our approach seeks to find an assignment matrix that identifies node correspondences between the two graphs, such that the number of preserved edges – i.e., edges that exist between matched node pairs in both graphs – is maximized.
