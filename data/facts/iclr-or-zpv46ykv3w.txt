We introduce theSpatial-Acoustic Geometry Encoder (SAGE), a geometry-aware audio encoder that aligns binaural acoustic features with 3D spatial structure using panoramic depth images and simulated room-impulse responses at training time, while requiring only audio at inference. We introduceSAGE, the first geometry-aware encoder that jointly models acoustic signals and scene structure. The key idea is to inject geometric cues through an auxiliary binaural RIR prediction task, providing privileged supervision that regularizes the audio encoder.SAGEcomprises two jointly optimized modules:(i) a binaural audio encoder for perceptual tasks, and(ii) an RIR prediction module that fuses depth features with audio representations. The overall training objective is a weighted sum of cross-entropy losses for event classification (Lcls), distance prediction (Ldis), and DoA estimation (Ldoa), where the learnable weight coefficients (αi) balance each task’s contribution. Given the ground-truth RIR R, reconstruction is supervised by a geometric loss that combines an ℓ1 term with an Energy Decay Curve (EDC) loss, Lgeo =∥R− ¯R∥1 +λL EDC(R, ¯R)(3) where LEDC measures the mismatch between predicted and reference decay curves computed via Schroeder’s backward integration algorithm Schroeder (1965). The overall training objective combines perceptual and geometric terms, L=η 1Lbinaural +η 2Lgeo.
