Overall, normalization, layer-wise attention, and node-wise attention are critical for capturing complex dependencies and realizing the full performance of HISTOGRAPH. 6 CONCLUSION We introduced HISTOGRAPH, a two-stage attention-based pooling layer that learns from historical activations to produce stronger graph-level representations. layer- wise attention captures the evolution of each node’s trajectory across depths, node-wise self-attention models spatial interactions at readout, and signed layer-wise normalization balances contributions across layers to preserve discriminative signals and stabilize training. Our method is primarily methodological, but we encourage responsible use to avoid potential misuse in applications that could impact privacy or enable harm.
