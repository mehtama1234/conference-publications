This paper provides the first mechanistic expla- nation for a long-standing and unresolved failure case where training with flash attention in low-precision settings leads to catastrophic loss explosion. We systematically modify the algorithm—disabling tiling, selectively replacing flash attention with a standard implementation, and performing key computations in high precision—to narrow down the potential causes of instability.
