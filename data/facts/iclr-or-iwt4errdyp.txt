We propose SceneStreamer, a unified autoregressive framework for continuous scenario generation that represents the entire scene as a sequence of tokens, in- cluding traffic light signals, agent states, and motion vectors, and generates them step by step with a transformer model. SceneStreamer generates these tokens step- by-step on top of static map tokens, allowing flexible and fine-grained simulation. To avoid confusion with the concurrent work (Yang et al., 2025), we refer to our method as SceneStreamer throughout this paper. Following this idea, we developSceneStreamer, a unified transformer that sees the whole history and rolls out the scenario step-by-step, enabling fine-grained, closed-loop generation and smoother downstream simulation integration.
