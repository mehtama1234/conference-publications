While re- cent advances in continual parameter-efficient tuning (PET) of pretrained models show promise, they typically rely on multiple training epochs and explicit task cues, limiting their effectiveness in GCL scenarios. Moreover, existing methods often lack targeted design and fail to address two fundamental challenges in con- tinual PET: Inspired by the fruit fly’s hierarchical memory system characterized by sparse expansion and modular ensembles, we propose FlyPrompt, a brain-inspired framework that decomposes GCL into two subproblems:expert routingandexpert competence improvement. Such methods introduce task-specific prompt experts to adapt PTMs incrementally, and typically rely on clear task cues and sufficient gradient updates to allocate and train expert modules (Wang et al., 2024a; 2022b).
