EXPERIMENTALSETUP We evaluate our method in simulation to assess whether modeling spectral structure improves imitation learning under clean supervision. For each task and chunk length K, we determine the coarse– fine frequency split using a data-driven heuristic. We compute the DCT of demonstrated action chunks, average squared coefficients across demonstrations and action di- mensions to obtain an empirical power spectrum, and select the smallest index whose cumulative energy exceeds 90%. Across both benchmarks, this typically assigns the lowest ∼30% of frequency coefficients to the coarse component.
