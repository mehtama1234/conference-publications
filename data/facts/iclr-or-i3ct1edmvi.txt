However, compressing the semantics of an instance into a single point makes it challenging to faithfully encode two semantic structures at once:hierarchy(is-arelations in a concept family) andcompositionality(conjunction across distinct concept families). These observations have motivated the development of hyperbolic embeddings (Nickel & Kiela, 2017) and hyperbolic entailment cones, which encode partial orders via inclusion (Ganea et al., 2018a). Classical approaches express composition via logical conjunction or additive operations (e.g., Boolean algebra, bag-of-words, and vector addition in word2vec) (Hinton et al., 1986; Mikolov et al., 2013; Vendrov et al., 2016), but these struggle to encode semantic hierarchy efficiently.
