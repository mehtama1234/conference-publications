Attention Mechanisms and Sinks.The self-attention mechanism is fundamental to the Transformer architec- ture (Vaswani et al., 2017), and research analyzes its mecha- nisms, from head-level syntactic roles and inactivity to layer- wise linguistic properties and overall depth efficiency (Clark et al., 2019; Skean et al., 2025; Csord ´as et al., 2025; Sandoval-Segura et al., 2025). Interpretations of its function vary, from preventing representation over- mixing (Barbero et al., 2025) to being an emergent property of information compression (Queipo-de-Llano et al., 2025), while its emergence during pre-training has been empirically linked to the constraints of softmax normalization (Gu et al., 2025). Proposed solutions range from post-hoc, training- free calibration (Yu et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025) to archi- tectural modifications like gated attention designed to be sink-free (Qiu et al., 2025). Finally, we briefly revisit the self-attention mech- anism to formally introduce the concept of attention sink.
