In this work, we propose ENS, a novel ensemble framework that enhances the detectability and robustness of logits-based unbiased watermarks while strictly preserving their unbiasedness. To address these challenges, we propose ENS, anensembleframework that composes multiple unbiased watermark instances with independent keys to amplify the detection signal while provably preserving the LM distribution.
