Zero-shot Embodied Task Planning with VLMs.VLMs (OpenAI, 2023; Team et al., 2025; Ahn et al., 2022) have gained significant attention in robotic task planning (Niu et al., 2024; Yue et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024) due to their powerful capabilities in processing multimodal inputs, such as images and language instructions. To overcome this gap, we propose theGraph-then-Planstrategy, which first generates task-specific scene graphs as an intermediate structured representation before high-level planning.
