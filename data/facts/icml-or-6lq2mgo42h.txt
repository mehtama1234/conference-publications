To avoid the high cost and suboptimal learning that can result from training these coupled skills together, we introduce the “Action Pre-training, Pointcloud Fine-tuning” (APPF) paradigm. We first pre-train the expert on large batches of pure trajectory data (up to a batch size of 31,824) to master motion following, and then fine-tune it with point cloud data to learn refinement. Now we detail the architecture of our proposed model and its training process: Model Architecture.Our action expert’s architecture is inspired by the design of 3D Diffusion Policy (Ze et al., 2024). Each component of CA is processed by a dedicated encoder (MLPs for St and Pg, and a PointNet-based encoder for Opcd) to produce a final conditioning feature vectorf A t . We then create a noisy action ak t by adding k steps of Gaussian noise according to the noise schedule¯αk: The noise prediction network ϵθ is trained to predict the added noise ϵ based on the noisy action ak t , the diffusion step k, and the conditioning feature f A t . During pre-training, the point cloud feature within f A t is masked out, training the diffusion model to follow trajectories.
