In this paper, we propose a Bayesian reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm, EUBRL, which leverages epistemic guidance to achieve principled exploration. This guidance adaptively reduces per-step regret arising from estimation errors. An algorithm is said to be minimax-optimal (Lattimore & Hutter, 2012; Dann & Brunskill, 2015) if its bounds match the corresponding lower bound up to logarithmic factors.
