More recent deep learning-based methods, such as ImplicitV ol [27] and RapidV ol [7], lever- age implicit, NeRF-like neural representations for higher fidelity reconstructions. However, fully implicit methods are computationally expensive, while explicit voxel-based methods suffer from memory overhead and resolution con- straints. Moreover, state-of-the-art methods often assume light-based ray accumulation, which is a fundamental mis- match with ultrasound physics, where wave propagation dictates image formation [1, 16].
