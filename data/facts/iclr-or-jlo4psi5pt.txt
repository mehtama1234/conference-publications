Despite their impressive capabilities, these models often suffer significant performance degradation when deployed in real- world environments where the test-time data distribution diverges from their pre-training data distribution (Agarwal et al., 2021; Menon et al., 2024). To address this challenge, test-time adaptation (TTA) has emerged as a powerful paradigm, allowing pre-trained models to adapt to different target domains using only a stream of unlabeled test samples (Liang et al., 2025; Dong et al., 2025).
