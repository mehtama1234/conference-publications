How- ever, existing retrieval agents mainly rely on keyword- or embedding-based methods. To address this limitation, we introduceSciNet, the firstScientificNetwork relation-aware dataset for information retrieval agents. Extensive evaluation of three categories of retrieval agents shows that their accuracy on relation-aware tasks often falls below 20%, highlighting a fundamental short- coming of current retrieval paradigms.
