Due to the combinatorial structure of theK-means clustering problem, current relaxation algorithms struggle to balance their constraint feasibility and objective optimality, presenting tremendous challenges in computing the second-order critical points with rigorous guarantees. In this paper, we provide a new formulation of theK-means problem as a smooth unconstrained optimization over a submanifold and characterize its Riemannian structures to allow it to be solved using a second-order cubic-regularized Riemannian Newton algorithm. Our numerical experiments show that the proposed method converges significantly faster than the state-of-the-art first-order nonnegative low-rank factorization method, while achieving similarly optimal statistical accuracy.
