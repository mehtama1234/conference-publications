In this work, we introduce the first unified physics-based HOI framework that leverages Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to enable long- horizon interactions with diverse object types — including static, dynamic, and articulated objects. To support our methodology, we present Interplay, a novel dataset with thousands of long-horizon static and dynamic interaction plans. Experimental results demonstrate that our method achieves promising quantita- tive performance and generates natural, human-like motions across a diverse range of HOI tasks.
