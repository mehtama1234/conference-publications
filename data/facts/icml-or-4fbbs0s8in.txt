Here, the image and flow losses provide the primary supervision, while the deformation regularizers and the heterogeneity constraint serve as auxiliary guidance. Following common practice, all methods first recon- struct the initial object shape at the first timestamp using 3DGS, and then simulate the deformation with their cor- responding dynamics modules.
