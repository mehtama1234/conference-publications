This motivates an Identify-then-Measure formulation, reframing VTG from black-box numeric regression into a structured grounding process where explicit evidence selection guides evidence-conditioned boundary refinement. To operationalize this intuition, we propose Foresee-to- Ground (F2G), a framework that translates the Identify-then- Measure routine into a verifiable grounding pipeline. The LLM then identifies the most rele- vant hypothesis and measures precise temporal boundaries through evidence-conditioned refinement. Motivated by the human tendency to perceive videos as coherent events rather than a flat visual stream, this module decomposes untrimmed videos into a compact set of candidate segments. These candidates are extracted from boundary-aware representations learned via predictive objectives, which encourage the temporal module to capture coherent event dynamics and transition cues. By binding each boundary prediction to a specific evidence ID, F2G shifts grounding from uncon- strained timestamp regression over a flattened visual-token stream to verifiable, citation-based inference. We propose Evidence-Driven Reasoning, which aug- ments the LLM input with citable evidence units and implements Identify-then-Measure through joint span- ID and timestamp supervision. The perception module extracts boundary-sensitive temporal features U, proposes Top-K candidate event segments, and aggregates segment-local visual evidence via a Span Evidence Encoder (SEE), form- ing the video-wide evidence pool SK(V) . Conditioned on the instruction, video tokens, and SK(V) , the Video-LLM first cites one evidence ID z and then generates the final boundariesTand answerAunder the cited hypothesis.
