Measuring Agents in Production

Problem. Despite widespread deployment of LLM-based agents in production across industries, we lack systematic understanding of what technical approaches make these deployments successful.
Approach. Conduct 20 in-depth case studies with agent developers and survey 86 practitioners across 26 domains to understand why organizations build agents, how they build them, how they evaluate, and what challenges they face.
Contribution. Document that 68% of production agents execute at most 10 steps, 70% rely on prompting instead of weight tuning, 74% use primarily human evaluation, and reliability remains the top development challenge.
