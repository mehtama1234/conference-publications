Generative modeling

The central problem is controlling generation across media, structure, and constraints. The field is moving beyond "sample something plausible" toward choosing the order, state space, guidance signal, safety boundary, and compute path that make a generated object useful.

Problem

Generation is no longer one format. It includes images, speech, 3D scenes, molecules, videos, code-like sequences, policies, and optimization candidates, each with different validity and latency requirements.

Approaches

The papers use diffusion, flows, autoregression, masked generation, insertion processes, latent structures, watermarking, safety guidance, reward adaptation, mode-preserving composition, and acceleration.

Connecting read

The shared move is to make generation conditional on purpose: preserve modes, obey constraints, align to rewards, protect safety, reduce steps, and keep long outputs coherent.

READ

The Big Picture

Generative modeling is no longer a single recipe for drawing images or predicting text. It is a family of ways to construct objects under uncertainty. The object might be a video, a 3D Gaussian scene, a continuous image token stream, a speech conversation, a synthesis pathway, a robot plan, a molecular transition path, or a set of Pareto-optimal design candidates. The central design question is what process should build the object. Should it denoise all variables gradually, predict left to right, unmask tokens adaptively, insert pieces in a learned order, flow probability mass through a Wasserstein scheme, or optimize candidates against a reward?

The hard part is that every generation process brings a failure mode. Autoregressive video drifts over long horizons. Diffusion guidance can push samples off manifold. Masked models can commit uncertain tokens too early. Self-training can amplify degradation. Safety guidance can destroy distributional fidelity. Minority classes can lose capacity to majority modes. Fast samplers can break exactness. The papers on this page are best read as control mechanisms around these failure modes: choose the state space, choose the order, choose the guidance, choose the safety signal, and choose the compute path.

STRUCTURE

What Has To Be Designed

Generation Order

Autoregressive, masked, block, insertion, any-order, and diffusion processes differ mainly in what is known when the next piece is generated.

State Space

Continuous tokens, 3D Gaussians, geometry feature maps, graphical latents, speech tokens, synthesis pathways, and denoising spacetime all define what the model can move through.

Guidance

Alignment directions, reward models, safety energies, concept-removal scores, Langevin initialization, and RL fine-tuning all steer generation, but each can distort the base distribution.

Mode Coverage

Good generators must avoid collapsing local modes, minority classes, synthesis pathways, or long-horizon alternatives into one plausible average.

Efficiency

Few-step distillation, constant-memory attention, retraining-free acceleration, local parallel decoding, and streaming video generation determine whether a method can actually run.

Accountability

Watermarks, safety guidance, perceptual reward models, editing benchmarks, and distribution-preserving constraints make generation inspectable and deployable.

CLUSTERS

The Main Research Moves

1. Generation order is becoming a learnable policy

Several papers question the default assumption that generation must proceed left to right or through a fixed denoising schedule. Unifying Masked Diffusion Models puts masked diffusion, autoregression, and block diffusion in one framework and learns context-dependent token order. Learning Unmasking Policies formalizes diffusion language model unmasking as an MDP trained from token confidence. Any-Order GPT evaluates masked diffusion models as any-order autoregressive models inside GPT-style architectures. Insertion Process learns where, what, and when to insert using permutation-based variational inference. LPD uses arbitrary ordering and position query tokens to sharply reduce image generation steps. The deeper point is that order is not a formatting decision; it is an inference strategy.

2. Diffusion and flows are being unified as transport mechanisms

A second cluster tries to reduce the fragmentation between diffusion, flow matching, bridges, and stochastic interpolants. RealUID distills diffusion, flow, bridge, and stochastic-interpolant matching models under a single inverse-distillation framework. GWF treats broad generative algorithms as parametric JKO schemes over distribution divergences. DFM Bounds extends diffusion flow matching convergence to 2-Wasserstein distance under integrability and weak log-concavity assumptions. RFM shows noise- and gradient-expectation diffusion-policy objectives are instances of one Stein-control estimator class. Diffusion Spacetime derives geodesics over denoising distributions, making the path through diffusion time part of the geometry rather than a bookkeeping variable.

3. Guidance is useful only when it preserves the distribution enough

Guided generation sits between control and distortion. SGF recasts safety guidance as energy-based negative guidance away from unsafe samples. Concept Removal Guidance estimates unwanted concept presence from noise predictions and calibrates negative guidance during denoising. AGSM integrates contrastive text-image alignment directions directly into the score-matching objective. UniPercept trains a perceptual multimodal reward model that can guide text-to-image generation. GLASS Flows combines ODE efficiency with stochastic evolution for inference-time reward alignment. DivIn steers diffusion and flow models away from mode collapse by sampling initial noise from a guidance potential. The key tension is that steering has to move samples toward a goal without breaking the model's learned world.

4. Long-form generation has drift and memory problems

Long outputs expose compounding errors. Stable Video Infinity trains diffusion transformers on self-generated error trajectories so they learn to correct autoregressive drift over ultra-long videos. Flex-Forcing unifies bidirectional and autoregressive video diffusion through flexible chunking over time and denoising steps. SANA-Video uses Linear DiT and constant-memory state for high-resolution minute-long video. MotionStream distills a bidirectional motion-controlled teacher into a causal streaming student with sub-second latency. LiveMoments restores selected video frames using the original high-quality key photo as structural and textural guidance. Long-form generation is not just "more frames"; it is maintaining identity, motion, and recoverability under accumulated approximation.

5. 3D and geometry generation require decodable structure

The 3D papers treat generation as producing a structure that can be rendered, edited, or reconstructed, not just viewed. FlashWorld combines multi-view diffusion, direct 3D Gaussian generation, and distillation to generate scenes much faster. VIST3A aligns generated video latents so they decode into consistent Gaussian splats or pointmaps. Generative Human Geometry Distribution encodes human geometry distributions as feature maps over SMPL domains. NextStep-1 predicts continuous image tokens with a flow-matching head instead of suffering vector-quantization loss. Structured Flow Autoencoders add graphical latent structure to continuous normalizing flows. These papers make the latent object more structured so downstream geometry is not an accident.

6. Mode preservation matters more than average plausibility

A generator that averages incompatible futures can look smooth and still be wrong. CDGS avoids averaging incompatible local modes in long-horizon robot plans, panoramas, and videos. Capacity Manipulation identifies majority-class capacity monopolization as a cause of minority-class degradation in diffusion models. Divide-and-Denoise coordinates multiple pretrained diffusion models through a fair division game over noisy-sample regions. Path-dependent DAI extends amortized sampler learning beyond Markov state assumptions to improve exploration and convergence in GFlowNets. Reinforced SMC uses off-policy SMC data, tempering, and replay to stabilize neural samplers. Across these papers, diversity is not aesthetic variety; it is preserving valid alternatives.

7. Domain constraints turn generation into optimization

Some generated objects are judged by objective functions rather than human perception. Efficient Diffusion via Landing enforces equality and inequality constraints on nonconvex feasible sets without costly projections. PCD treats offline multi-objective optimization as conditional sampling over desired Pareto tradeoffs. NEXCO makes adaptive solution expansion the intrinsic unmasking process of a masked diffusion solver for combinatorial optimization. ReaSyn refines full synthesis pathways through insertion, deletion, and substitution operations. Rex uses numerical inversion for near-machine-precision reconstruction, image editing, and Boltzmann sampling. The model is useful because it searches a constrained space, not because it imitates a dataset in the abstract.

8. Generation needs provenance, safety, and evaluation hooks

Deployment-oriented generation needs mechanisms for accountability. Spherical Watermark designs distribution-preserving watermarks by reconstructing Gaussian noise from codes through spherical mapping and rotation. EditVerse provides instruction-based video editing data and a benchmark, making editing quality measurable across diverse requests. DiffusionNFT uses positive and negative generations to optimize diffusion models without likelihood estimation. Neon studies self-training collapse and then reverses the degraded update direction to improve image generation. Error Propagation in Quantized Diffusion Models tracks post-training quantization degradation as errors accumulating through the denoising trajectory. These papers make generation easier to audit, improve, and accelerate without pretending the output alone is enough evidence.
