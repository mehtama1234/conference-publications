FIRST PRINCIPLE

A Paper Entry Should Preserve The Claim

The problem with a weak paper summary is that it compresses away the thing that makes the method mathematically meaningful. Saying "better benchmark", "new agent", "efficient training", or "robust generation" does not say what changed, what stayed fixed, what evidence identifies the claim, or what failure remains possible.

The template below forces one discipline: before describing the approach, name the object being controlled. The object may be a trace, distribution, feasible set, graph, spectrum, coupling, state transition, verifier, ambiguity set, sample path, or artifact. Once the object is named, the method can be explained as a particular update, projection, sampler, estimator, routing rule, representation, or verifier acting on that object.

TEMPLATE

The Nine Slots Every Paper Needs

SlotQuestionGood answer shapeWeak answer to avoid
1. Big-picture problemWhat real confusion or failure is the paper trying to remove?State the hidden variable or decision that surface performance cannot identify."It improves performance" without saying which failure mattered.
2. State spaceWhat space does the method act in?Prompt traces, parameters, tokens, graphs, coordinates, actions, distributions, markets, proofs, or physical fields.Only naming the model family.
3. Mathematical objectWhat object makes the claim precise?A verifier, feasible set, posterior, coupling, spectrum, transition law, tail event, equivalence class, or sufficient statistic.A vague phrase like robustness, alignment, or intelligence.
4. Preserved quantityWhat must remain true while the system changes?Input dependence, safety margin, likelihood ratio, spectral signal, molecular validity, route feasibility, legal provenance, or domain evidence.Assuming all useful behavior survives the update.
5. Allowed movementWhat is the system allowed to change?Policy weights inside a trust region, samples along a transport path, data mixture weights, token budget allocation, graph edges under CI evidence.Describing optimization without constraints.
6. Why the method worksWhy does this object make the hard problem easier?It removes irrelevant symmetry, preserves sufficient state, reduces variance, prices compute, exposes tail mass, separates signal from noise, or prevents overclaiming.Listing method components without causal explanation.
7. Formula conditionWhat compact condition would make the idea true?An inequality, estimator, constraint, decomposition, conservation rule, threshold, or objective that states the working regime.A decorative equation unrelated to the paper's claim.
8. Evidence checkWhat observation would make the claim credible?Ablations, calibration, confidence intervals, verifier outcomes, counterfactual swaps, route validity, stress tests, or spectral stability.Only reporting one aggregate leaderboard number.
9. Cross-paper roleWhich other papers solve the same mathematical problem elsewhere?Connect by shared object: trace, measurement, feasible set, transport, spectrum, game, state, or identifier.Connecting by surface topic alone.
WORKED FORMS

How The Template Changes By Theme

Agents

Trace As Controlled Evidence

Problem. A final answer can be right for the wrong reason, or long because the policy learned a template.

Object. A trajectory of states, actions, observations, tool calls, and verifier outputs.

Why it works. The trace exposes whether information from the input still controls the action path. RAGEN-2, h1, Skill-Pro, CyberGym, ParetoPO, and OMAC differ in implementation, but each treats reasoning as a controlled process rather than a static answer.

trace T is useful when I(T; input | task) is high and cost(T) is justified by outcome gain

Evidence check. Perturb the input, measure trace dependence, tool-call marginal value, verifier agreement, and whether reusable skills preserve correctness outside their source episode.

Evaluation

Benchmark As Instrument

Problem. A score can confuse ability with item choice, judge error, shortcut behavior, or sampling noise.

Object. A measurement channel from hidden capability to observed score.

Why it works. ATLAS, FRABench, UFEval, WebDevJudge, CounselBench, MADQA, and domain-specific suites improve evaluation by matching the observation channel to the capability claim.

observed score = capability signal + item noise + judge bias + shortcut artifact

Evidence check. Report uncertainty, component scores, judge calibration, artifact-native checks, ranking reversals, and domain-expert disagreement.

Safety

Risk As Boundary And Tail Mass

Problem. Average behavior can be safe while rare or pressured behavior crosses the line.

Object. A harmful event set, feasible action boundary, or adversarial game.

Why it works. CSPO, Rare Event Analysis, SandboxEscapeBench, Jailbreak Foundry, AdvGame, FlowGuard, and watermarking papers make the forbidden region explicit instead of assuming random tests find it.

risk = P(Y in E_bad) or maximize utility subject to margin_to_violation(s,a) >= 0

Evidence check. Estimate tail probabilities, boundary distance, adaptive attack success, false positives, robustness to paraphrase or editing, and utility loss.

Data / Systems

Data As Parameter Movement

Problem. Examples are useful only relative to current model state, training time, hardware limits, and target distribution.

Object. A marginal update induced by data under compute and memory constraints.

Why it works. Midtraining Bridges, DiReCT, Sequential Data Values, OPUS, Common Corpus, ThinKV, LiftQuant, RePAIR, and sparse/IO-aware systems all ask what information survives the path from data to deployed function.

value(x at time t) = target_gain(Delta theta_x,t) - interference - compute_cost - provenance_cost

Evidence check. Measure timing sensitivity, gradient alignment, cache sufficiency, compression error, provenance, data duplication, and real hardware behavior.

Physical / Generative

Generation As Native-State Sampling

Problem. A generated artifact can look plausible while violating the state space where the object actually lives.

Object. Coordinates modulo symmetry, transport paths, molecular routes, PDE fields, world states, or action manifolds.

Why it works. Quotient-Space Diffusion, Protein Autoregressive Modeling, FALCON, IRNO, TideGS, FlashWorld, HDFlow, and molecule-design papers choose the representation before optimizing the sample.

sample x in native space S / equivalence, subject to physical or chemical constraints C(x)

Evidence check. Test invariance, route validity, field residuals, temporal consistency, rare-mode coverage, and verifier projections in the native artifact.

Theory / Optimization

Theorem As A Budget Statement

Problem. A method may work only under hidden budgets: rank, curvature, precision, sample size, regret time, or spectral separation.

Object. The budget that controls movement or evidence.

Why it works. Adam Degeneracy, LoRA theory, Alignment-Sensitive Minimax Rates, Single-Head Attention, Transformer Circuits, Language Generation in the Limit, and finite-certification papers explain when a recipe is allowed to succeed.

claim holds only when signal, samples, precision, rank, or time exceed the relevant threshold

Evidence check. State assumptions, show threshold behavior, separate expressivity from learnability, and test whether deployment preserves the mathematical regime.

Causality / Multimodal

Claim As Identified Evidence

Problem. Correlation, fluent explanation, or fused modality output can overstate what the evidence distinguishes.

Object. An identified effect, equivalence class, causal route, modality contribution, or sufficient latent state.

Why it works. Local Covariate Selection, DiCoLa, Distributional Equivalence, CausalGame, PRISM, Mind-Omni, VISUALSWAP, MINT, and DLMR all restrict claims to what interventions, swaps, graph assumptions, or measurement channels can support.

claim is valid only over the identified set: all worlds consistent with observed evidence and assumptions agree on target

Evidence check. Report adjustment validity, equivalence classes, intervention response, modality swaps, memory ablations, expert labels, and remaining unidentifiable alternatives.

STYLE RULE

Write The Object Before The Method

The atlas should avoid paper prose that starts with a method name and then tries to infer why it matters. The better order is: problem, object, invariant, allowed movement, method, evidence, boundary, connection. This keeps the writing conceptual without becoming vague.

The result should let a reader compare papers across conferences by the object being controlled. A benchmark paper and a causal-discovery paper may both be about measurement validity. A diffusion paper and a safety paper may both be about tail mass. A tokenizer paper and a video-compression paper may both be about sufficient information under a fixed budget. The template makes those shared problems visible.
