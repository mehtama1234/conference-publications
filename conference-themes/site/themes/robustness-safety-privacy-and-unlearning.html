Theme 02 - ICLR 2026: 27 / ICML 2026: 77

Robustness, safety, privacy, and unlearning, mapped

The central problem is control under stress: models and agent systems are useful only if they keep their promises when attacked, pressured, fine-tuned, queried at scale, or asked to forget data.

Problem

Capability creates new failure surfaces. A model can leak private data, hide unsafe behavior, pass a weak safety check, generate dangerous artifacts, or look harmless until the interaction becomes multi-turn or tool-enabled.

Approaches

The papers build certified deletion notions, watermarking and provenance methods, sandbox and cyber evaluations, pressure tests, activation-level monitors, robust reward methods, and defenses against malicious fine-tuning.

Connecting read

The field is moving from abstract safety slogans to concrete boundary tests: what can the system do, what can it reveal, what can it hide, and what can be proven after deployment.
