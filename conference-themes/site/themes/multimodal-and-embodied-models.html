Theme 01 - ICLR 2026: 41 / ICML 2026: 74

Multimodal and embodied models, mapped

The central problem is that intelligence in the world is not text-shaped. A useful system must bind words to images, video, sound, camera geometry, objects, motion, memory, and action.

Problem

The old division between perception and reasoning is breaking down. A model that can describe an image but cannot preserve spatial layout, temporal continuity, or object identity will fail as soon as the task depends on the world changing over time.

Approaches

The papers attack this by adding camera and depth structure, building scene graphs, compressing video without losing the important tokens, training vision-language-action systems, learning world models from human or robot video, and creating verifiers for visual outcomes.

Connecting read

The connecting idea is grounding: a model has to carry enough structure from the world into its internal state that later reasoning is about the same world, not just about plausible language.
