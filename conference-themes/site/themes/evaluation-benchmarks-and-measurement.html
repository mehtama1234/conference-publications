Evaluation, benchmarks, and measurement, mapped

The central problem is knowing what a model actually did, not what its answer sounded like.

Problem

Static leaderboards are too weak for systems that browse, write code, operate tools, generate images, reason over long traces, or behave differently under rare pressure. A score without a verifier and failure explanation is often not actionable.

Approaches

The papers use executable checks, visual verifiers, human-calibrated judges, rare-event estimators, code and security tasks, subjective scoring protocols, and benchmark designs that expose hidden failure modes.

Connecting read

This theme is the measurement layer of modern AI: make tasks real enough, verifiers sharp enough, and diagnostics honest enough that progress cannot hide behind fluent output.
