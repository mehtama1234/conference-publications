FIRST PRINCIPLE

A Subtheme Is Not A Topic; It Is A Burden Of Proof

A subtheme is useful only when it says what kind of claim the papers must justify. A reasoning subtheme must prove the middle work changes with the task. A compression subtheme must prove the shortened state still keeps the facts needed later. A benchmark subtheme must prove the score measures the real target. A causal subtheme must prove the action claim is forced by the evidence, or state the remaining possible stories.

This ledger makes that burden explicit. It also gives the next expansion rule: when adding detail to any paper, write the row's obligation as a concrete testable condition, then show whether the paper's evidence supports it.

NEXT USE

How This Drives Paper-Level Expansion

Before adding a paper

Choose its subtheme row and inherit the proof burden. The paper card should explain what must stay true and how the method supplies evidence for it.

Before comparing papers

Compare whether papers satisfy the same burden through different objects, or whether they only share vocabulary while proving different claims.

Before trusting a result

Use the evidence-check column to decide whether the result supports the real claim or only reports a surface improvement.
