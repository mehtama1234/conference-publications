Research lane

Safety and evaluation as measurement design

This lane asks what kind of evidence should count when models become deployed systems. ICML and ICLR 2026 are not treating evaluation as a scoreboard after the real research is done. They treat measurement as a design choice that shapes what models learn, what failures remain invisible, and what kinds of systems the field is willing to trust.

FIRST PRINCIPLE

Evaluation Is a Control Surface

A benchmark is not a neutral mirror. It tells researchers what to optimize, tells companies what to ship, and tells users what to trust. If the benchmark is static, models can memorize its style. If the score is a single number, models can improve the number while hiding the failure. If the judge is an LLM, the evaluation inherits that judge's blind spots. If rare harms are not sampled, they disappear from development even though they return at deployment scale.

The safety and evaluation papers are therefore about measurement design. They ask what object should be measured: final answer, action trace, executable outcome, internal activation, clinical span, legal source choice, visual evidence re-check, policy recovery path, tail probability, uncertainty interval, or human-verifiable consequence. The deeper argument is that modern AI systems need measurements that see process, pressure, uncertainty, and context rather than only final response quality.

SUBTHEME 01

Benchmark Validity

The central problem is whether the score is measuring the ability it claims to measure.

A modern benchmark can fail even when every example has a correct answer. It can reward shortcuts, collapse several skills into one number, hide score variance, or test an ability that matters only inside the benchmark's artificial format. The strongest papers in this subtheme make the benchmark itself the object of scientific scrutiny: what is the construct, what evidence supports it, how stable is the ranking, and what failures are invisible to the score?

ICLR 2026

FRABench / UFEval

Treats fine-grained multimodal evaluation as an aspect taxonomy problem. Instead of asking whether a judge prefers output A or B overall, it asks whether evaluation criteria can transfer across text, image understanding, image generation, and interleaved generation.

ICML 2026

Micro-Benchmarking Reliability

Asks how small a benchmark can be before pairwise model rankings become unreliable. The concept is not benchmark compression alone; it is confidence in the ordering decision created by a reduced test set.

ICML 2026

ATLAS

Recasts benchmark use as psychometric ability estimation. Item-response theory and Fisher-information item selection replace fixed test administration, allowing fewer questions while estimating latent model ability more directly than raw accuracy.

ICML 2026

Prescriptive Scaling

Turns evaluation into a compute-to-capability frontier. Instead of reporting one model's score, it estimates high-quantile downstream capability boundaries as a function of pretraining FLOPs under current post-training practice.

ICML 2026

Beyond First-order Asymptotics

Studies the fluctuation behavior of sequential tests beyond first-order expected stopping time. The benchmark lesson is that evaluation procedures have variance and stopping-time uncertainty, not only asymptotic optimality claims.

ICML 2026

HypoSpace

Evaluates LLMs as samplers over enumerable scientific hypothesis spaces, measuring validity, uniqueness, and recovery. The score asks whether a model explores a structured set of possible explanations rather than producing one plausible answer.

ICML 2026

Finite Test Certification

Characterizes when finite stochastic tests can certify task-family performance and when worst-case impossibility remains. It makes certification limits explicit instead of treating benchmark pass rates as general guarantees.

ICML 2026

Language Generation in the Limit

Separates formal learnability from practical sample complexity for language generation. The evaluation point is that a task can be possible in principle while still hiding requirements so large that ordinary benchmark success gives little deployment assurance.

SUBTHEME 02

Judge Reliability

A judge is a measurement instrument, not ground truth.

The field increasingly uses LLMs, multimodal models, expert panels, and hybrid evaluators to score open-ended outputs. That creates a second-order evaluation problem: how do we know the judge is reliable? These papers focus on calibration, aspect generalization, disagreement, rationale quality, and situations where judges are especially likely to overrate plausible but unsafe answers.

ICLR 2026

WebDevJudge

Evaluates LLM, multimodal, and agentic judges on web implementation comparisons. The web page is a difficult object because correctness includes layout, interaction, responsiveness, and functional behavior that source-only judging can miss.

ICLR 2026

CounselBench

Uses mental-health professionals to evaluate open-ended counseling responses. The paper exposes a core judge failure: models and LLM judges can reward comforting language while missing unsafe clinical advice or boundary violations.

ICML 2026

Pressure Reveals Character

Validates an LLM judge against human annotations while evaluating multi-turn pressure scenarios. The key measurement problem is not only whether a model refuses bad behavior, but whether scoring rationales remain faithful under honesty, safety, and manipulation pressure.

ICML 2026

AGREE

Shows that overall aesthetic judgments can hide conflicts between underlying attributes. It treats subjective scoring as multidimensional measurement rather than one scalar preference.

ICML 2026

HAMC

Uses hyperbolic uncertainty for multi-view clustering, letting high-confidence views guide unreliable views. The evaluation idea is asymmetric trust: corrupted sources should not be forced into equal agreement with reliable ones.

ICML 2026

MADQA

Uses human-authored PDF question answering and Classical Test Theory to expose gaps among human, agent, and oracle performance. The benchmark distinguishes what is answerable in principle from what current agents can actually recover.

ICLR 2026

EigenBench

Builds a label-free benchmark for subjective model values by validating model-ensemble consensus against human judgments. It asks how to evaluate preference-like constructs when objective ground truth is unavailable.

ICLR 2026

Omni-Reward

Builds reward benchmarks and data across text, image, video, audio, and 3D with free-form preferences. It treats reward modeling as a cross-modal judging problem where the evaluator must understand both the artifact and the preference language.

SUBTHEME 03

High-Stakes Evaluation

High-stakes tasks require measuring the constraints around the answer.

In medicine, law, education, finance, and compliance-heavy workflows, a useful answer can still be unacceptable if it violates role boundaries, source rules, privacy constraints, or professional standards. The common move in this subtheme is to evaluate behavior inside the pressure of the domain rather than treating the domain as a source of prompts.

ICML 2026

Copyright-Bench

Evaluates whether agents select lawful sources in realistic commercial workflows. Its important variable is pressure: user preferences and time constraints can push models away from compliant behavior even when the rule is known.

ICML 2026

Seizure-Semiology-Suite

Tests medical temporal-report faithfulness in seizure videos. The measurement target is not generic video understanding but laterality, symptom sequence, temporal localization, and clinically meaningful reporting.

ICML 2026

DR Tulu

Evaluates long-form research-agent answers across science, healthcare, and general benchmarks. Its focus is attributed research behavior: whether a model can produce sustained, sourced, domain-sensitive work rather than a short answer.

ICML 2026

M-CBE

Studies the design space between accuracy and interpretability using linear and symbolic expert functions. In high-stakes decisions, a correct prediction may be insufficient if the reason cannot be understood or governed.

ICML 2026

Falling Trees

Learns constrained sets of sparse interpretable models for high-stakes risk prioritization. It treats governance as a Rashomon-set problem: there may be many accurate models, but only some preserve monotone, inspectable risk structure.

ICML 2026

Accuracy Auctions

Replaces average accuracy with social welfare based on users' private values for correct predictions. It asks what evaluation should optimize when different users have different stakes in model correctness.

ICML 2026

TRECA

Links treatment-responder abstention to CVaR and learns low-regret causal classification rules. The high-stakes choice is not only who will respond, but when the model should abstain because the tail risk is too large.

ICML 2026

Robust Contextual Optimization with Missing Covariates

Builds distributionally robust decision sets directly from partially observed covariates. In high-stakes settings, imputing a complete record can hide uncertainty; this approach evaluates decisions against ambiguity created by missing information itself.

SUBTHEME 04

Adversarial Safety

Safety measurement has to assume the model is being pressured, attacked, or bypassed.

Static refusal tests are too weak for deployed systems. Attacks can appear through hidden text channels, multimodal inconsistencies, fine-tuning access, linguistic triggers, or internal harmful features that are not visible in the prompt. The papers here move safety evaluation from checking a final answer toward monitoring internal state, attack diversity, and strategic interaction.

ICML 2026

Jailbreak Foundry

Turns jailbreak papers into executable modules in a shared harness. Its contribution is not just another attack list; it is infrastructure for making red-team evaluation reproducible and extensible.

ICML 2026

AdvGame

Frames attacker and defender models as a non-zero-sum game. Instead of testing a frozen defense against a frozen attack set, it co-evolves both sides so safety training faces adaptive pressure.

ICML 2026

Activation Oracles

Uses internal activations to detect malign propensities absent from visible text. The measurement object is hidden computation, not surface content.

ICML 2026

FlowGuard

Detects multimodal jailbreaks by tracking redundancy, synergy, and modality dominance between unimodal and fused predictions. It treats abnormal cross-modal information flow as the safety signal.

ICML 2026

Robust Harmful Features

Finds safety-relevant attention heads and activation features that remain informative under jailbreaks. The idea is to monitor robust internal features when visible refusals can be manipulated.

ICLR 2026

Invisible Safety Threat

Shows that malicious fine-tuning can hide harmful prompts and responses inside benign cover text. The failure is a monitoring failure: surface classifiers see safe stegotext while decoded interactions remain unsafe.

ICML 2026

Trojan-Speak

Treats fine-tuning access as a safety attack surface. It shows adversarial fine-tuning can preserve reasoning capability while bypassing content classifiers, and that activation probes can reveal harmful internal representations.

ICML 2026

Stable-GFN

Uses GFlowNet training to find diverse effective red-team attacks. Pairwise comparison, noisy-reward masking, and fluency stabilization turn attack generation into a distributional search problem instead of a single best jailbreak.

ICML 2026

Consistent Adversarial Attacks

Separates label-preserving vulnerability from finite-data or expressivity artifacts. The paper asks whether adversarial vulnerability remains a real property when the perturbation preserves the underlying label.

ICML 2026

SecFid

Frames prompt-injection defense as a frontier between security and fidelity. The evaluation separates executing injected text, ignoring it, and faithfully processing benign spans, so defenses are judged by their deployment cost as well as blocked attacks.

ICLR 2026

SGF

Recasts safety guidance for diffusion and flow models as energy-based negative guidance against unsafe samples. MMD potentials and barrier analysis identify the early denoising window where intervention most changes the final sample.

ICML 2026

SVGT

Studies stable inference-time value steering, reducing harmful scores while preserving fluency. Its safety object is a controllable generation-time direction, not a retrained policy.

ICLR 2026

TRACE Effort

Detects implicit reward hacking by truncating reasoning traces and measuring how verifier success changes with effort removed. The safety question is whether a model learned the task or learned to spend reasoning tokens in ways that satisfy the verifier without real progress.

ICML 2026

Rapid Poison

Shows prompt injection can poison synthetic jailbreak training data for adaptive safety classifiers. The attack surface is the safety-data production loop itself, not only the final deployed model.

ICLR 2026

DeceptionDecoded

Provides image-caption pairs for detecting creator intent, misleading source attribution, and desire inference. It makes misinformation evaluation about intent and framing, not only whether a caption is literally false.

ICML 2026

Monitoring Monitorability

Measures when chain-of-thought traces make agent misbehavior more visible than action-only monitoring. It treats monitor compute and follow-up reasoning as oversight resources that can scale weak supervision.

ICLR 2026

Weak-to-Strong Monitoring

Red-teams monitors for covert tool-calling and computer-use agents by varying agent awareness, monitor awareness, and evasion strategies. The benchmark tests whether weaker monitors can supervise stronger agents with targeted escalation.

ICLR 2026

RedTeamCUA

Builds a hybrid VM and Docker sandbox for indirect prompt injection against computer-use agents. The attack originates in GUI environments, making the environment itself part of the adversary.

ICLR 2026

PLAGUE

Structures multi-turn jailbreaks into primer, planner, and finisher phases. The safety lesson is that context optimization across turns can be a more efficient attack surface than single-shot prompt search.

ICML 2026

Backdoor Self-Awareness

Trains poisoned LLMs to articulate their implanted triggers through inversion-inspired reinforcement learning. It turns hidden backdoor conditions into explicit descriptions that can be inspected and mitigated.

ICML 2026

Assistant Axis

Identifies an activation-space direction that predicts assistant persona drift and uses it to restrict activations to a stable assistant region. Persona stability becomes a jailbreak defense surface.

ICML 2026

Performative Misalignment

Reframes alignment-faking evaluations as potentially confounded by sycophancy toward AI researchers. The paper warns that current probes may not mechanistically distinguish scheming from pressure to please the evaluator.

ICLR 2026

LLM Deception on Benign Prompts

Measures hidden-objective bias and belief-output inconsistency without explicitly inducing deception. The evaluation target is self-initiated deception under ordinary prompts, not only adversarial scenarios.

ICLR 2026

Steering the Herd

Models an information-mediating planner that shapes sequential agents' Bayesian belief updates and decisions. It shows that a system can shift welfare without lying, cherry-picking, or hiding information.

ICML 2026

Obfuscation Atlas

Separates honest policies, obfuscated activations, and detector-evading obfuscated policies under RLVR. It treats deception evaluation as a three-way diagnostic problem: whether the model is honest, internally hiding, or actively hiding from the detector.

ICLR 2026

Persistent Homology LLM Adversarial Influence

Uses persistent homology to identify topological compression in LLM activations under prompt injection and backdoor fine-tuning. It adds a nonlinear geometry signal for adversarial influence that may not appear in ordinary accuracy or refusal metrics.

ICLR 2026

LVLMs-Saliency

Combines attention and gradients to diagnose hallucination onset when saliency shifts away from current visual evidence and toward prior outputs. It makes multimodal safety monitoring about where the model is looking while it answers.

ICLR 2026

WIMHF

Finds harmful preference examples through learned feature structure and shows targeted relabeling can improve safety without broadly degrading general performance. The safety lever is data curation, not only post-hoc refusal tuning.

SUBTHEME 05

Uncertainty, Calibration, and Tail Risk

Deployment risk often lives in probability mass the benchmark barely samples.

Many harmful behaviors are rare per query but common at platform scale. Other failures come from overconfident wrong answers, reward models that are uncertain in hidden ways, or calibration procedures that fail under online shift. These papers treat uncertainty as a first-class output of evaluation, not an appendix after the score.

ICML 2026

Rare Event Analysis of LLMs

Defines rare-event analysis for model behavior through event setup, probability estimation, and structure exploration. The key move is to estimate tail probabilities with error analysis instead of waiting for rare harms to appear in ordinary samples.

ICML 2026

Tail Risks in LM Outputs

Uses importance sampling from unsafe model variants to estimate low-probability harmful-output rates. It is a deployment-scale answer to events around one in ten thousand queries.

ICML 2026

Know More Know Clearer

Aligns subjective certainty with objective accuracy. The paper targets the practical failure where a model's confidence presentation diverges from the evidence supporting its answer.

ICML 2026

UP-OCP

Reduces online conformal prediction to universal portfolio selection. The purpose is finite-time miscoverage control without fragile manual tuning under sequential distribution shift.

ICML 2026

BNRM

Uses sparse non-negative latent factors and Bayesian reward modeling to reduce reward over-optimization. It treats reward uncertainty and factor interpretability as defenses against reward hacking.

ICML 2026

Binary RAR

Uses a binary retrieval-augmented factuality reward: a response is rewarded only if it contains no contradictions with retrieved evidence. The design avoids the generic-claim reward hacking that can appear with continuous factuality scores.

ICML 2026

Reward Redistribution for CVaR MDPs

Reformulates static CVaR reinforcement learning with dense per-step rewards and a contracting Bellman operator. Tail-risk safety becomes trainable through dynamic programming rather than a sparse terminal penalty.

ICML 2026

Loss-Aware OT-DRO

Learns optimal-transport ambiguity sets through bilevel hypergradient optimization. The robustness object is not a generic ball around the data; it is an ambiguity set shaped by the downstream loss that actually matters.

ICML 2026

Bulk-Calibrated Credal Sets

Separates high-mass bulk contamination from tail bounds so robust objectives remain finite under Huber-style uncertainty. It makes uncertainty sets less brittle by distinguishing ordinary ambiguity from pathological tails.

ICML 2026

ROCP

Constructs conformal prediction sets to minimize downstream minimax decision risk while maintaining finite-sample coverage. The evaluation target is not only coverage but decision usefulness under uncertainty.

ICML 2026

CreDRO

Learns credal prediction sets from distributionally robust model disagreement rather than random seed variance. It improves OOD detection and medical selective classification by modeling train-test relaxation directly.

ICLR 2026

CRC

Builds less conservative robust decision sets by optimizing prediction-set construction under decision robustness constraints. Coverage alone is not enough; the set must support the downstream decision.

ICML 2026

Conformal Policy Control

Uses safe reference-policy data to regulate how far optimized policies may deviate under finite-sample risk guarantees. It turns user-declared risk tolerance into a calibrated exploration control.

ICML 2026

Distributional IRL

Recovers reward distributions and distribution-aware policies through stochastic dominance violation minimization. Safety enters through risk measures over imitation behavior rather than a single inferred reward.

ICML 2026

Ambiguity-Averse MDPs

Treats uncertain transitions as random variables and characterizes which risk measures preserve dynamic-programming decomposition. The safety question is how to plan when epistemic uncertainty itself has to be evaluated through a compatible risk measure.

ICML 2026

Robust Causal Discovery with Power-Laws

Extracts spectral features from noisy time series before causal discovery so the model compares more stable trajectories than raw observations. It is an evaluation method for causal claims under noise and non-stationarity.

ICML 2026

Value of Variance

Penalizes self-contradiction, peer conflict, and low-confidence outputs in multi-agent debate. It treats disagreement structure as a calibration signal rather than only counting whether the final debate answer is correct.

SUBTHEME 06

Executable Code, Security, and Agent Evaluation

For agents and code systems, evidence should often be executable.

Some capabilities cannot be graded by reading the model's explanation. A security agent should produce an exploit or patch evidence. A compiler optimizer should preserve semantics and improve runtime. A UI or code-edit agent should act in context. The papers in this subtheme turn evaluation into an environment with state, actions, tools, and verifiers, even when the final product is a research benchmark rather than a deployed system.

ICLR 2026

CyberGym

Validates generated proof-of-concept tests against pre-patch and post-patch code versions. The score is not whether the model describes a vulnerability; it is whether executable evidence distinguishes vulnerable from fixed code.

ICML 2026

SandboxEscapeBench

Measures whether an agent can escape nested VM/container sandboxes and reach protected host evidence. The benchmark makes the security boundary itself the object under test.

ICML 2026

CVE-Factory

Builds LiveCVEBench and more than a thousand executable training environments for code-security agents. It makes security evaluation live enough to avoid relying only on stale examples.

ICML 2026

CIRBench

Combines compiler verifier checks, equivalence checking, and runtime measurement for LLVM IR optimization. The correct answer must preserve semantics and produce useful performance change.

ICML 2026

DRPBench

Benchmarks data-race prediction over concurrent programs. The object is program semantics across possible interleavings, not local code style.

ICML 2026

TokSuite

Releases fourteen matched pretrained models that differ only in tokenizer choice. It turns tokenizer effects into a controlled evaluation variable instead of an uncontrolled difference between model families.

ICML 2026

Harnessing Non-Adversarial Robustness

Studies robustness to semantically neutral prompt changes rather than only adversarial strings. It identifies perturbation-induced module bias and gives per-example and population-level certification framings for random prompt perturbations.

ICML 2026

Neural Concept Verifier

Restricts decisions to selected concept evidence to reduce shortcut behavior on complex image tasks. The verifier makes the model justify prediction through approved concepts rather than any predictive feature it can exploit.

ICML 2026

CE-Graph

Models agent workflows through failure signatures and edits workflow graphs using counterexample pools. The evaluation signal is not just task success; it is which counterexamples reveal where a math, code, or QA workflow breaks.

ICML 2026

PaperBanana

Benchmarks methodology-diagram generation for AI scientists through specialized visual and image-generation agents. It turns research workflow automation into an evaluable artifact: whether a system can produce publication-ready conceptual diagrams.

ICML 2026

WZ-LLM

Trains a specialized prover through Lean-verified bootstrapping and evaluates combinatorial identity proof generation. The important property is kernel-verified correctness, not whether the proof text sounds plausible.

ICML 2026

BrokenMath

Uses expert-reviewed false competition-math statements to test theorem-proving sycophancy. The benchmark asks whether models produce convincing flawed proofs when the premise itself is wrong.

ICML 2026

Weak-Strong Verification

Uses calibrated weak verifier scores to accept, reject, or defer reasoning outputs to stronger costly verification. It turns verification into a budgeted triage problem rather than an all-or-nothing check.

ICLR 2026

VERINA

Evaluates code, specification, and proof generation on manually curated Lean tasks. It exposes a gap between writing correct code and producing formal proof artifacts that certify the code.

ICLR 2026

EditBench

Evaluates real-world instructed code edits with highlighted spans, cursor context, and natural editing requests. It measures the practical contract of a coding assistant: preserve surrounding code while making the requested local change.

ICML 2026

FPS

Evaluates constructive problem solving where the output must satisfy an explicit formal or procedural target. The benchmark belongs here because success is tied to checkable construction, not fluent explanation.

ICML 2026

INDUCTION

Tests whether models can infer finite logical structures from examples rather than pattern-match familiar tasks. It is an evaluation of rule formation: the model must recover the hidden generative structure well enough to generalize.

ICLR 2026

CRV

Detects reasoning errors by classifying structural fingerprints in computational attribution graphs. It treats chain-of-thought verification as a graph problem over internal computation rather than as a surface-text judging problem.

ICML 2026

2-SAT Robustness

Uses parameterized 2-CNF families to test reasoning-model brittleness under controlled implication-graph structure. The point is to create logical perturbations where failure can be localized to a formal property rather than an anecdotal prompt.

ICML 2026

MEnvAgent

Automates executable software-engineering environments across ten programming languages using planning, execution, and verification agents. It treats environment construction as part of evaluation infrastructure, because a code benchmark is only useful if tasks can actually run.

ICML 2026

TerminalTraj

Curates Docker images and verified terminal trajectories across multiple domains. The benchmark object is the command-line process: observations, commands, environment state, and verification, not a single static answer.

ICML 2026

AlgoVeri

Holds classical-algorithm contracts fixed across Dafny, Verus, and Lean to expose proof-tool capability gaps. It evaluates whether a system can satisfy the same semantic specification when the verification language changes.

ICML 2026

Bitween

Combines regression, symbolic search, and agent proposals to discover randomized self-reductions with verification-backed evidence. It is an example of evaluation where the generated object is a reusable reduction that can be checked beyond one test case.

ICLR 2026

RefineStat

Refines probabilistic programs by enforcing semantic constraints and locally resampling priors or likelihoods when diagnostic checks fail. It treats statistical code as an executable artifact whose reliability can be repaired through targeted verification feedback.

ICLR 2026

SparseRL

Uses hierarchical rewards for sparse CUDA generation: compilation, correctness, and then runtime speed. It is a clean executable benchmark because code-like text gets no credit unless it builds, computes correctly, and improves the intended workload.

ICLR 2026

SwingArena

Pairs patch-generating submitters with test-writing reviewers over real GitHub issues and CI pipelines. The benchmark measures the software-development loop, where retrieval, tests, review, and CI expose failures a one-shot patch score would miss.

ICLR 2026

ADP

Unifies tool-use and browsing trajectories across agent datasets and reports gains across coding, browsing, tool-use, and research tasks. It is evaluation infrastructure: the point is to make heterogeneous action traces comparable instead of leaving each benchmark in its own format.

SUBTHEME 07

Multimodal Grounding

Multimodal evaluation asks whether the model uses the evidence channel it claims to use.

A vision-language model can answer from priors, text context, or earlier conversational assumptions without actually grounding in the current image or video. Multimodal evaluation therefore needs interventions that separate seeing from guessing: swapped images, situated egocentric views, perceptual attributes, emotion dimensions, visual outcome verification, and medical temporal reports.

ICLR 2026

OmniVerifier

Builds a universal visual verification benchmark and verifier across generation, editing, and visual reasoning tasks. It treats verification as reusable infrastructure for visual outcomes.

ICML 2026

VISUALSWAP

Swaps visually similar but semantically different images to test whether VLMs genuinely re-check visual evidence. It directly attacks conversational inertia.

ICML 2026

UniPercept

Defines perceptual image understanding across aesthetics, quality, structure, and texture. It broadens image evaluation beyond object recognition into perceptual judgment.

ICML 2026

SAW-Bench

Evaluates observer-centric spatial reasoning from smart-glasses videos. The key measurement target is situated awareness from the viewer's physical perspective.

ICML 2026

EEmo-Logic

Builds fine-grained image-evoked emotion assessment across five dimensions. It treats affective perception as structured reasoning rather than a single sentiment label.

ICML 2026

FeatJND

Allocates token-wise dynamic quantization step sizes by just-noticeable feature distortion. It treats quantization as perceptual feature preservation under a fixed noise budget, not just bit reduction.

ICML 2026

FLIP2

Expands protein fitness evaluation to enzymes, protein-protein interactions, light-sensitive proteins, and engineering-relevant splits. The benchmark tests whether protein models survive realistic biological distribution shifts.

ICLR 2026

PhyWorldBench

Evaluates whether text-to-video systems obey physical realism rather than only visual plausibility. It treats generated video as a simulated world whose motion, contact, and causality can be judged against physical expectations.

ICML 2026

RealPDEBench

Benchmarks physical-system modeling with real-world PDE data and paired simulation structure. The evaluation target is not a generic prediction score but whether a model preserves the behavior of a governed dynamical process.

ICLR 2026

TabStruct

Measures whether generated tabular data preserves structural relationships, not only marginal statistics. It makes table quality about constraints, dependencies, and usable synthetic structure rather than surface resemblance.

ICML 2026

TTSDS2

Builds objective text-to-speech evaluation around validated degradation and similarity signals. The broader point is that audio generation needs measurement scales tied to what listeners and downstream systems can actually perceive.

ICML 2026

DOUBT

Separates object recognition from relational reasoning before scoring multimodal hallucination trustworthiness. Its von Mises-Fisher trust metric is a way to avoid unstable small-sample semantic-entropy estimates.

ICML 2026

Geometric Flow Grounding

Uses manifold-flow projection residuals as zero-shot signals for deepfake video detection. The safety signal is whether a video follows learned geometric dynamics rather than merely whether frames look plausible.

ICML 2026

DGS-Net

Fine-tunes CLIP for AI-generated image detection while preserving transferable priors through distillation-guided gradient decomposition. It targets cross-generator generalization rather than detector success on one generator family.

ICML 2026

Causal Route Gating

Decomposes LVLM attention heads into visual and text routes, then suppresses prior-dominant text paths. The mitigation is training-free: one forward pass and one gradient estimate identify causal routes that drive hallucination.

ICML 2026

Real-World Unsupervised Models

Tests cross-environment sensory generalization, including Mandarin-to-English and infant-headcam-to-adult-object transfer. It treats environment statistics as the source of representation robustness rather than a nuisance variable.

ICML 2026

DISCO

Regularizes deep models toward causal conditional independence from observed shortcut bias variables. The robustness target is not only distribution-shift performance but removal of a measurable shortcut path.

ICML 2026

Hallucination Rate-Distortion

Frames factual hallucination as optimal lossy sparse membership testing under limited capacity. The key claim is that high-confidence non-facts can arise even with perfect data and optimal training.

ICML 2026

VJA

Shows image editing models can receive malicious instructions through visual marks, arrows, and inputs. It treats visual prompting itself as a jailbreak channel and uses training-free reasoning over visual intent for defense.

ICML 2026

AdLift

Lifts bounded 2D adversarial perturbations into safeguard Gaussians to protect neural 3D assets from instruction-driven editing. The safety property must generalize across rendered views, not only a single image.

ICLR 2026

SGRS / LocoRE

Reduces LVLM hallucination by filtering low-saliency candidate tokens and reinforcing local coherence during decoding. The method attacks hallucination at generation time by changing which visual evidence survives token selection.

ICML 2026

OPL / G-OPL

Removes facial attributes from video anomaly representations while preserving pose and motion cues. It treats privacy as orthogonal sensitive-subspace removal rather than wholesale feature deletion.

ICML 2026

PACT

Distills constraint gradients into diffusion policies to reduce physical safety violations without demonstrations or rewards. It evaluates whether post-training can add physical constraints while preserving manipulation success.

ICLR 2026

Veritas

Builds HydraFake and pattern-aware multimodal reasoning for deepfake detection across generators, forgery types, and domains. It measures whether a detector generalizes beyond one artifact family instead of overfitting visible generator fingerprints.

ICML 2026

VideoKR

Uses expert-annotated questions to test knowledge-intensive video understanding. The benchmark is designed to reduce textual shortcut success: the model must connect visual evidence with external knowledge rather than answer from language priors alone.

ICML 2026

Visual Attribution Streaming

Approximates exhaustive causal attribution for thinking VLMs without repeated backward passes or perturbation sweeps. It evaluates whether intermediate reasoning is grounded in the visual evidence that actually drives the answer.

ICML 2026

MoCA

Separates perception failure from reasoning failure in multimodal agents and routes reinforcement signals to the failing component. It makes evaluation diagnostic: a bad answer should say whether the system looked badly, thought badly, or coordinated the two badly.

ICML 2026

TEDBench / MiAE

Builds a non-redundant protein-fold benchmark from TED and Foldseek-clustered AlphaFold structures. The measurement concern is leakage: a protein model can look strong if the benchmark contains structural near-duplicates of training examples.

ICML 2026

LIMSSR

Reformulates incomplete multimodal action-quality assessment as conditional sequence-to-score reasoning. The evaluation problem is missing evidence: the model must combine prompt-guided imputation with uncertainty rather than pretending all modalities are available.

ICLR 2026

ImageDoctor

Scores generated images across plausibility, semantic alignment, aesthetics, and overall quality while localizing flaws with heatmaps. It turns text-to-image evaluation from one scalar preference into a grounded diagnosis of what is wrong and where.

SUBTHEME 08

Robotics and Embodied Evaluation

Embodied models have to be measured through memory, state, and action over time.

Robotics and agent evaluation are hard because the model's answer changes the state it will later observe. Success depends on temporal memory, spatial memory, procedural memory, user coordination, and the ability to rank policies without always running expensive real-world rollouts. These papers define evaluation around trajectories and state evolution rather than isolated perception.

ICML 2026

RoboMME

Benchmarks memory-augmented VLA variants across temporal, spatial, object, and procedural memory. Its main claim is that memory representation effectiveness is task-dependent.

ICML 2026

dWorldEval

Ranks robot policies by coupling future observation generation with progress-as-text success indicators. It evaluates action-centric world models as policy-assessment tools.

ICML 2026

tau2-bench

Tests conversational agents in a shared Telecom Dec-POMDP where user and AI both use tools. The benchmark separates reasoning failures from communication and user-guidance failures.

ICML 2026

MemoryBench

Benchmarks service-time LLM memory through accumulated simulated user feedback. The object is not recall of a static context window but continual learning from interaction history.

ICML 2026

VenusBench-Mobile

Shows mobile GUI agents remain brittle under environment variation, with failures dominated by perception and memory. The evaluation object is not app success alone but which mobile-agent capability fails first.

ICML 2026

Vision2Web

Evaluates coding agents from static UI-to-code translation to full-stack website development with visual verification and VLM judging. It measures whether an agent can produce an interface that matches intent in structure, appearance, and behavior, not only whether code was emitted.

ICLR 2026

AstaBench

Compares 57 agents across 22 classes while controlling tool access, costs, interfaces, and baselines. It treats agent evaluation as confounded unless tooling and operating conditions are measured alongside scores.

ICLR 2026

Gaia2

Evaluates agents in asynchronous environments that change independently under time pressure and noisy events. The key shift is from static task completion to acting in a world that can move while the agent is still reasoning.

ICML 2026

OpenApps

Tests app agents under interface and content variation, showing that apparent competence can depend on a memorized surface. It measures whether control transfers across equivalent but visually different app states.

ICML 2026

PIPE

Evaluates agents under rewritten interfaces, making robustness to UI transformation part of the benchmark. The task is not merely to click the right place once; it is to preserve intent when the presentation changes.

ICML 2026

CausalGame

Tests AI scientist agents in controlled games involving confounding, selection bias, noisy measurements, data collection, inference, and explanation. It exposes whether an agent can design evidence rather than only narrate a causal story after seeing data.

ICML 2026

PhotoAgent

Introduces UGC-Edit and evaluates multi-step photo editing with memory, visual feedback, tree search, and learned aesthetic rewards. The benchmark turns visual taste into an iterative control problem where each edit changes the next observation.

ICLR 2026

Rational Agents

Benchmarks decision-oriented dialogue in games such as Collaborative Battleship and Guess Who? against human exploration and action behavior. It measures whether agents gather information rationally before acting, not only whether the final guess is right.

ICML 2026

RoTS

Evaluates GUI agents on executable recovery cases where their own policy choices create errors. The important capability is repair after a bad action: observing the changed state, diagnosing the mistake, and returning to a valid path.

ICML 2026

MiniAppBench

Evaluates generated HTML mini-applications across user intention, static interface quality, and dynamic behavior. It treats software generation as a runnable artifact benchmark where visual structure and interaction both matter.

ICML 2026

RAGEN-2

Identifies template-collapse reasoning in agentic RL by measuring mutual information between inputs and generated reasoning. The failure is subtle: the trace can look like reasoning while becoming input-agnostic boilerplate.

ICML 2026

Unsupervised Partner Design

Generates training partners on the fly and selects them by learnability for robust ad-hoc teamwork. The evaluation target is adaptation to unseen partners, not success with a fixed scripted collaborator.

ICML 2026

SCALE

Controls vision-language-action inference without external verifiers or repeated rollouts by using the model's own output distribution as a test-time signal. It evaluates whether a policy can self-regulate action confidence cheaply during deployment.

ICML 2026

Skill-Pro

Turns episodic agent traces into reusable executable skills with activation, execution, and termination conditions. Evaluation moves from one solved episode to whether experience becomes a reliable procedural memory.

ICML 2026

MASPOB

Uses bandit search, topology priors, and coordinate ascent to tune prompts in fixed multi-agent workflows under small evaluation budgets. It treats workflow design itself as an object to optimize and measure.

ICML 2026

MAP

Studies production agents through practitioner interviews and deployed case studies. Its value is measurement realism: it documents what teams actually track when agents run in products, not only what academic benchmarks report.

ICLR 2026

MC-Search

Provides long, verified stepwise chains for multimodal retrieval-augmented agents, including subquestions, retrieval modalities, evidence, and intermediate answers. It evaluates whether a system can search and assemble evidence over many steps.

ICLR 2026

SimuHome

Evaluates smart-home agents in a Matter-based simulator with device APIs, environmental variables, and scheduling constraints. The task is grounded in time and state: the agent must coordinate devices under changing household conditions.

ICLR 2026

AgentFlow

Coordinates planner, executor, verifier, and generator modules through evolving memory during live tool use. It belongs in this lane because the benchmark object is a modular agent loop whose intermediate checks shape the final outcome.

ICLR 2026

BIRD-INTERACT

Couples databases, knowledge bases, user simulators, CRUD tasks, and executable tests for dynamic text-to-SQL interaction. It evaluates whether an agent can maintain database intent across multi-turn state changes rather than produce one query.

ICLR 2026

WoW!

Benchmarks whether world models improve embodied task success rather than only visual quality. It asks whether learned futures help an agent act, which is a stronger deployment claim than producing plausible rollout videos.

SUBTHEME 09

Privacy, Unlearning, and Formal Guarantees

Some safety claims need guarantees, not only empirical rates.

Privacy, unlearning, robustness, and fairness often require stronger evidence than average-case benchmark performance. The common mathematical move is to define the property as a constraint over distributions, mechanisms, output configurations, or deletion events, then prove or verify when the system satisfies it.

ICLR 2026

Gaussian Certified Unlearning

Introduces epsilon-Gaussian certifiability and studies deletion through one Newton step plus calibrated Gaussian noise. The core object is a prediction-level guarantee after removing training data.

ICML 2026

High-Dimensional Gaussian Mechanism

Proves when Gaussian additive noise is asymptotically optimal for differential privacy and when other radial mechanisms may improve finite-regime utility.

ICML 2026

OC-space

Frames robustness and fairness verification for tree ensembles as search over output configurations. It unifies multiple property checks through one verification space.

ICML 2026

CSPO

Evaluates safe RL recovery through time-to-safety, violation frequency, and reward preservation. The safety property is dynamic behavior near a constraint boundary, not final return alone.

ICML 2026

Catch-22

Frames watermarking as a trilemma among robustness to edits, stealth from keyless observers, and reliable verification. Its information-theoretic analysis shows why provenance signals can become distinguishable as they become robust.

ICLR 2026

Spherical Watermark

Embeds watermark bits into diffusion latent Gaussian noise while preserving visual quality and the latent prior. The mathematical object is a distribution-preserving transformation of latent noise for media provenance.

ICLR 2026

Gaussian Certified Unlearning in High Dimensions

Defines unlearning privacy as a hypothesis-testing problem and introduces Gaussian certifiability for high-dimensional regimes. The deletion claim is measured by indistinguishability from a model that never saw the removed data.

ICML 2026

SlaClip

Uses slack already induced by DP-SGD clipping to adapt thresholds without extra private gradient-statistic queries. It keeps the same privacy accountant while improving utility through better clipping control.

ICML 2026

Biased Generalization

Shows that diffusion test loss can improve while generated samples become unusually close to training data. It challenges test-loss early stopping as a privacy-safe metric for generative models.

ICML 2026

GEM

Uses contrastive velocity matching to erase unwanted concepts in Rectified Flow models. It treats generative unlearning as trajectory shaping, bridging teacher-guided erasure with flow-matching geometry.

ICML 2026

Concept Removal Guidance

Estimates unwanted concept presence from diffusion noise predictions and adaptively calibrates negative guidance during denoising. It suppresses concepts without fine-tuning or external classifiers while preserving benign fidelity.

ICML 2026

OCE

Uses layer-wise orthogonal transformations for fast diffusion concept erasure while preserving non-target generation geometry. It treats erasure as a geometry-preserving edit rather than broad destructive fine-tuning.

ICLR 2026

Efficient Resource-Constrained Training of Transformers via Subspace Optimization

Frames on-device transformer training as a privacy and resource problem, adapting only a constrained subspace rather than moving all learning to centralized infrastructure. It connects privacy to energy, memory, and where adaptation physically happens.

ICLR 2026

Common Corpus

Assembles permissively licensed multilingual and code pretraining data with provenance and filtering details. It belongs in this lane because data legality and provenance become safety properties before the model is ever trained.

ICML 2026

FlatLand

Separates client-specific and shared parameters for privacy-preserving heterogeneous federated graph learning. The method treats client variation as geometry, not noise to average away.

ICML 2026

SmartFed

Routes LoRA adapter capacity by input semantics and device resource budgets for private edge LLM adaptation. It links privacy to deployment constraints: compute and communication budgets shape what can be privately adapted.

ICML 2026

PRISM

Adds intrinsic tangent-space privacy noise to low-rank updates so LoRA privacy does not depend on an arbitrary factorization. The guarantee is made gauge-invariant, matching the geometry of the parameterization.

ICML 2026

IHM

Improves private ordinary least squares through Gaussian sketching and Hessian mixing. The paper clarifies when private regression can get better utility without weakening the privacy guarantee.

ICML 2026

Secret Memory Lower Bounds

Proves user-level differentially private streaming algorithms need substantially more space for distinct-elements and related analytics. It is a reminder that privacy guarantees can impose unavoidable memory costs.

ICML 2026

Empirical Privacy Protection Benchmark

Audits privacy risk for adapted LLMs across pretraining overlaps, IID adaptation data, and OOD adaptation data. It shows vulnerability can rise when adaptation data is close to pretraining data even without direct overlap.

ICML 2026

Exact RL Unlearning

Defines reinforcement-learning deletion as indistinguishability from never seeing a user's data. The paper targets exact removal cheaper than full retraining, extending unlearning from supervised prediction into interaction data.

ICML 2026

LM Memorization Capacity

Separates unintended memorization from useful information about the data-generating process. By estimating bits stored per parameter when generalization is removed, it connects memorization capacity to membership-inference risk.

ICLR 2026

Hubble

Releases standard and perturbed LLM pairs with known sensitive-text insertions for controlled memorization research. It makes memorization measurable because the sensitive insertions and exposure frequencies are known.

ICLR 2026

PetaGAIL++

Generates useful synthetic mobility trajectories with imitation learning and differential privacy. Sensitivity-aware noise adjusts privacy protection by sample risk while preserving mobility utility.

ICLR 2026

Semantic Watermark Fingerprints

Uses statistical watermarks conditioned on broad semantic domains instead of brittle fixed trigger keys. The provenance goal is ownership verification after fine-tuning, quantization, and filtering.

ICLR 2026

Ellipse Signatures

Uses high-dimensional ellipse constraints in logprob outputs as model-specific provenance signatures. Verification can operate from output logprobs without full model weights, making the signature closer to symmetric-key authentication.

ICLR 2026

WGM Domain Discovery

Provides missing-mass guarantees for differentially private discovery when users hold subsets from an unknown item domain. Private selection is treated as a discovery problem under unknown support.

ICML 2026

GoodDiffusion

Conditions diffusion bridge generation on valid per-input signatures, then shows universal authorization signatures are vulnerable to gradient-based surrogate recovery. Provenance mechanisms themselves become attack surfaces.

ICML 2026

FedPissa

Selectively aggregates LoRA components and decorrelates subspaces for federated personalization. It reduces heterogeneous client interference without the redundancy of dual-LoRA designs.

SUBTHEME 10

Scaling, Transfer, and Measurement Economics

Evaluation also has a budget and a transfer problem.

Large model evaluation is expensive. Human labels are expensive. New tasks arrive faster than full benchmark suites can be built. These papers ask how much evidence is needed, how public benchmarks transfer to new tasks, how subjective constructs should be measured, and how to estimate capability frontiers without exhaustively testing every model on every task.

ICML 2026

Hedging on the Frontier

Uses public benchmark dominance relations as side information for few-sample new-task learning. It treats benchmark tradeoffs as geometry that can guide model selection under sparse evidence.

ICML 2026

VALUEFLOW

Uses repeated rankings and Plackett-Luce aggregation to estimate value intensity. It avoids direct scalar ratings for subjective constructs where people may not share a stable numeric scale.

ICML 2026

Ranking Time Series

Replaces noisy absolute time-series labels with pairwise comparisons and ideal-point modeling. The measurement insight is that relative judgments can be more reliable than unstable direct labels.

ICML 2026

Anytime Trees

Replaces fixed-sample Hoeffding split tests with sequentially valid inference under adaptive stopping. It makes tree-split evaluation compatible with anytime decisions instead of pretending sample sizes were fixed in advance.

ICML 2026

Pre / Mid / RL Reasoning

Uses synthetic parseable reasoning tasks to isolate pretraining, midtraining, and RL contributions. The benchmark is causal by design: it asks which training phase actually produced the observed reasoning behavior.

ICML 2026

BFTS

Combines calibrated tabular prediction with Thompson-sampling style decision making and evaluates policy value under bandit feedback. It moves evaluation from prediction accuracy to whether uncertainty leads to better sequential decisions.

ICML 2026

FOCUS / RePAIR

Decomposes repetition failure into loop-entry risk and loop-persistence risk, then targets escape mass around plausible alternatives. It makes degeneration a token-level diagnostic problem rather than a vague complaint about bad generation.

ICML 2026

Benchmarking at the Edge of Comprehension

Uses adversarial generation and local human verification for tasks where full human solution may be hard. It reframes evaluation around bounded verifiability: humans may not solve the whole problem, but they can check critical claims.

ICML 2026

Consequence-Based Utility

Scores hard math solutions by downstream consequences when direct verification is unavailable. Evaluation becomes a question of whether an output improves related verifiable work.

ICML 2026

Surgery

Uses attention-sink divergence regularization to reduce harmful pattern learning during fine-tuning. It is measurement economics at the internal level: a cheap mechanistic signal stands in for expensive post-hoc safety discovery.

ICML 2026

Buffer-and-Reinforce

Protects refusal behavior during fine-tuning-as-a-service adaptation with BufferLoRA and ReinforceLoRA. The safety object is the gradient path during user adaptation, not only the final adapted model.

ICML 2026

Linguistic Nepotism

Finds multilingual RAG systems can prefer English or query-language citations even when relevance is held constant. The measurement issue is citation bias: retrieval quality can lose to language preference.

ICML 2026

Token Overcharging

Shows fixed per-token pricing creates incentives for strategic overcharging and that transparent generation constraints can hide profitable tokenization heuristics. It treats token accounting as an auditable governance problem.

ICML 2026

Data Market Pricing

Studies stability in non-rival data markets where reusable datasets are sold by strategic sellers. The evaluation object is not model accuracy but whether a pricing rule creates a stable market for data access.

ICML 2026

Bayesian Truthful Valuation

Combines semivalues with hidden-validation data valuation to reward truthful knowledge sharing. It asks how collaborative data evaluation can be incentive-compatible instead of merely accurate after the fact.

ICML 2026

Fair OT

Requires optimal-transport group-pair matching probabilities to satisfy target fairness levels. The paper makes fairness a transport constraint with explicit cost tradeoffs rather than an after-the-fact metric.

ICLR 2026

SafeDPO

Reformulates safety alignment as a closed-form direct preference objective with a lightweight helpfulness-safety control parameter. It exposes the tradeoff as a tunable training object rather than a separate reward-model stack.

ICML 2026

Fair Posthoc Control

Learns representations that allow fairness-accuracy tradeoff adjustment after training without retraining. Fairness becomes a controllable post-processing frontier, not a fixed one-time constraint.

ICML 2026

Adaptive Bias

Shows LLMs can develop novel artificial-group biases through adaptive exploration even when groups have no inherent differences. It treats exploration itself as a source of emergent unfairness.

ICML 2026

Self-Soupervision

Improves corrupted-distribution robustness by adapting self-supervised models on unlabeled shifted data and merging parameter soups. The evaluation object is robustness gained without labels in the shifted environment.

ICML 2026

Rashomon Trust

Shows diverse near-optimal model sets can support reactive robustness while increasing training-data leakage. Trustworthiness is evaluated at the model-set level, where robustness and privacy can pull in opposite directions.

ICLR 2026

Divergent Causal Interventions

Shows common activation interventions can push representations away from natural model distributions. It adds natural-distribution constraints so interpretation interventions stay faithful rather than creating artificial internal states.

ICLR 2026

CorreGen

Formulates noisy cross-view alignment as hidden-correspondence inference solved by EM. Robust clustering comes from down-weighting unalignable samples while retaining class-level cross-view structure.

ICML 2026

Constrained Transformers

Uses primal-dual training to make intermediate Transformer representations descent-like. The robustness claim is that optimization-shaped layers improve perturbation robustness and OOD generalization.

ICML 2026

C2R

Prioritizes smallest-margin adversaries during dataset distillation and widens decision boundaries with contrastive robustness loss. The distilled set is judged by whether it preserves robust boundary cases.

ICLR 2026

Capacity Manipulation

Identifies majority-class capacity monopolization as a driver of minority-class degradation in diffusion models. Low-rank capacity reservation gives minority knowledge protected representational room.

ICML 2026

GFD-EMVC

Repairs distorted evolutionary multi-view classification landscapes caused by noisy boundary labels. The evaluation issue is that noisy labels can corrupt the fitness landscape used to select robust classifiers.

ICML 2026

Noisy Sample Compression

Characterizes when sample-compressible distribution families remain learnable under l2 and total-variation perturbations. It asks which structure survives when the examples themselves are noisy.

ICML 2026

Sharp IO Analysis

Learns nonlinear input-output maps under full-history adversaries when attack times are sparse. The paper gives matching bounds that show how memory length and sparse attacks shape robust system identification.

ICML 2026

GRAM

Uses gradient-routed auxiliary modules so selected dual-use capabilities can be ablated at inference time. It treats access control as a pretraining design problem rather than only a deployment policy.

ICML 2026

RLVepsR

Analyzes reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards under noisy verifiers and shows regimes where models learn, stall, or anti-learn. It makes verifier quality a phase-transition variable rather than a background assumption.

ICML 2026

LM Geometry Diagnostics

Uses a controlled family of OLMo-style models to separate representation-geometry metrics from downstream task performance. It warns that elegant internal metrics need behavioral validation before they become evaluation claims.

ICML 2026

CV Relative Instability

Shows that individually stable learners can still produce unstable model comparisons under cross-validation. It shifts attention from estimator stability to the reliability of the comparative claim researchers actually report.

ICML 2026

LLM Adaptability Limits

Finds that task-definition familiarity predicts LLM annotation performance better than simple text memorization metrics. The evaluation lesson is that adaptation depends on alignment with the task definition, not just exposure to similar words.

ICLR 2026

Train-before-Test

Fine-tunes each model before evaluation to estimate model potential rather than frozen benchmark skill. It asks whether rankings should measure present capability, reachable capability under adaptation, or both.

ICLR 2026

LLM DNA

Reconstructs model lineages from functional behavior across hundreds of LLMs. The evaluation object is model provenance: which systems are related, copied, distilled, or independently developed when public documentation is incomplete.

ICLR 2026

LLMs Get Lost

Shows strong models lose substantial performance in simulated multi-turn generation tasks. The paper makes conversation length and turn structure part of evaluation instead of assuming single-turn scores transfer to extended interaction.

ICLR 2026

CALIPER

Bridges drift detection and adaptation by measuring retraining readiness across domains, learners, and detectors. It treats evaluation as an operational trigger: not only whether performance drifted, but whether the system is ready to adapt.

ICML 2026

MIRA

Enables Bayesian model comparison without direct marginal-likelihood computation, supporting posterior validation and uncertainty estimates when evidence is expensive or unavailable. It belongs in measurement economics because it asks how to compare models when the usual evidence calculation is the bottleneck.

ICML 2026

Thinking in Flow

Adds a dissipative Neural ODE thought state to stabilize long-horizon autoregressive reasoning under distractions. It evaluates reasoning as a dynamical system whose internal trajectory can drift, not just a sequence whose final token is right or wrong.

ICML 2026

SMoE Discontinuities

Softly incorporates experts near routing boundaries to reduce discontinuities in sparse mixture-of-experts models. The robustness target is local smoothness: small input changes should not create abrupt expert-routing failures.

ICML 2026

Suppress and Diversify

Identifies robust-feature decay across network layers and refines robust computational pathways. It treats robustness as a pathway-selection problem inside the model rather than only an output-level score.

ICML 2026

DDG-SFDA Regression

Uses density-guided pseudo-label refinement for source-free regression adaptation under distribution shift. The evaluation issue is continuous-label robustness when the source data is unavailable and shifted target examples must carry the adaptation signal.

ICML 2026

SCIQL

Balances task reward and explicit behavior style under distribution shift with gated advantage-weighted regression. It evaluates alignment as reward-seeking under changing data, where style constraints can fail even when task performance holds.

ICLR 2026

MNPO

Extends duality-gap measurement to heterogeneous non-transitive preferences. It makes preference optimization evaluable when there is no single stable ranking that all users or models agree on.

ICLR 2026

Provable NAM Explanations

Uses additivity in neural additive models to reduce sufficient-explanation search to logarithmically many verification queries. The paper treats explanation as a property that can be verified efficiently, not just visually inspected.

ICLR 2026

HERO

Combines sparse correctness checkers with dense reward-model scores for reasoning post-training. It is a hybrid verifier design: exact checks provide hard anchors while learned rewards supply shaping where exact verification is too sparse.

ICLR 2026

P-GenRM

Converts preference signals into adaptive personas and scoring rubrics for personalized reward evaluation. It treats reward modeling as user-conditioned measurement rather than assuming one universal preference function.

ICLR 2026

AdAEM

Automatically generates value-probing questions to distinguish LLM inclinations beyond static HHH-style benchmarks. The evaluation adapts to the model being tested, searching for questions that reveal value differences instead of relying on a fixed questionnaire.

ICLR 2026

HSD

Improves speculative decoding acceptance while preserving target-model distribution fidelity. It is a systems-side verification paper: faster inference is only acceptable if the accepted sequence still follows the intended distribution.
