ICML and ICLR differ by mathematical habit

This page compares the conferences through the objects their papers make explicit. ICML often turns a method into a controllable variable. ICLR often turns a behavior into an environment, interface, or representation where the behavior can be observed.

FIRST PRINCIPLE

The Difference Is Where Each Conference Puts The Missing Variable

Both conferences are moving away from isolated prediction. The difference is where they usually place the hidden variable that explains behavior. ICML tends to put it inside the learning or inference mechanism: curvature, constraint distance, hidden-state norm, tail probability, denoising error, spectral span, low-rank update, or verifier signal. ICLR tends to put it around the model: agent world, public data recipe, benchmark environment, multimodal interface, quotient sample space, retrieval setting, or executable task.

That distinction is useful because it explains why similar papers feel different. ICML often asks: what variable can we measure and control so the method becomes reliable? ICLR often asks: what system form must exist before the behavior is meaningful? The shared end state is the same: an AI system whose state, evidence, constraints, and failure boundaries are visible.

ICML habit: expose control variable c. ICLR habit: build observation system E. Both ask whether c or E identifies the target.

DIFFERENCE 01

Reasoning: Control Signal Versus Process Substrate

ICML habit

Make the reasoning signal measurable

Think of a dashboard light that should turn on only when the engine needs attention. A reasoning model also needs a readable signal: is it still working toward the answer, repeating itself, guessing, or spending extra steps usefully? RAGEN-2, The Tell-Tale Norm, h1, and Thinking in Flow each try to turn some hidden part of the work into something that can be watched.

Why it works. The final answer can look polished even when the work that produced it was weak. The method works only if something inside the process changes when the reasoning is improving or breaking. What must stay the same is the link between the signal and the quality of the work. What may change is how much time, guidance, or correction the model gets after that signal is read. Name used later: a control variable or statistic.

read the work signal, then decide whether to spend, steer, or stop
ICLR habit

Save the work so it can be checked

It is easier to judge a lab result when the notebook, test setup, and raw observations are still available. OpenThoughts publishes reasoning recipes. Verifying Chain-of-Thought checks whether steps actually depend on earlier steps. TRACE Effort asks whether a short-looking answer hides shallow work. LongWriter-Zero and ThinkV keep long outputs and memory behavior visible.

Why it works. You cannot inspect a process that disappears as soon as the answer is printed. These papers make the intermediate work into an artifact that can be tested. What must stay the same is the connection between the saved work and the answer it supports. What may change is the checker: a graph, a long-writing task, a memory test, or a verifier. Name used later: process evidence.

keep the work record so a checker can test answer and path together
DIFFERENCE 02

Agents: Boundary Control Versus World Construction

ICML habit

Define what the agent must not cross

Picture a map with open rooms, locked rooms, toll roads, and a destination. An agent is not finished just because it reaches the destination; it also matters whether it entered a locked room or spent too much along the way. SandboxEscapeBench, Copyright-Bench, DRPBench, and ParetoPO make those limits explicit.

Why it works. A text answer can hide a bad route. These methods judge the whole path: what the agent touched, what it changed, what it paid, and whether it broke a rule. What must stay the same is the boundary between allowed and forbidden behavior. What may change is the task, tool, website, codebase, or cost budget. Name used later: a reachable set, constraint, or tradeoff frontier.

success means reach the goal while staying inside the allowed path
ICLR habit

Create worlds where agent behavior can be seen

A worker should be tested in a workshop, not only asked what they would do. CyberGym runs exploits. GAIA2 changes the environment while the agent acts. OpenApps changes interface state. ASTABench, MedAgentGym, and SwingArena put agents inside workflows where actions have consequences.

Why it works. The important part is the next thing that happens after an action. If a click opens the wrong screen, a patch breaks a test, or a medical step changes the case, the environment records it. What must stay the same is the action-to-consequence link. What may change is the domain: security, medicine, apps, science, or code review. Name used later: an environment transition.

action changes world; changed world reveals whether the action was right
DIFFERENCE 03

Safety: Tail Geometry Versus Pressure Harness

ICML habit

Say exactly what safety means

A bridge is not safe because the average crossing feels comfortable. The question is whether it fails under the rare heavy truck, storm, or cracked support. Rare Event Analysis, Tail Risks, CSPO, VALUEFLOW, and Catch-22 each choose the part of behavior where harm would show up.

Why it works. Average scores can hide the cases that matter most. These methods work by naming the danger precisely enough to measure it: how often it happens, how close the system gets to it, or whether a detector can tell safe from unsafe. What must stay the same is the safety question. What may change is the way the question is measured. Names used later: tail probability, boundary distance, or hypothesis test.

safety claim = measured answer to a specific danger question
ICLR habit

Create pressure that reveals the weak spot

A lock cannot be judged by trying one easy key. Someone has to try many keys, adapt after each failure, and look for the shape of the weakness. Jailbreak environments, malicious-finetuning studies, counsel benchmarks, deepfake detectors, and CyberGym-like tasks create that pressure.

Why it works. Failure is invisible if the test never goes near it. These papers build the attacker, benchmark, or generator that pushes the system toward risky behavior. What must stay the same is the rule being protected. What may change is the pressure source: prompts, finetuning, generated media, browser actions, or security tasks. Name used later: an adversarial distribution.

measure risk under the pressure that can actually expose it
DIFFERENCE 04

Generation: Path Control Versus Native Sample Space

ICML habit

Control the route, not only the final sample

Bread is not explained only by the final loaf; kneading, resting, heat, and timing all change it. DMPO, denoising-error work, HDFlow, FALCON, and SRMC look at how a generated answer, image, or plan is made step by step.

Why it works. Small mistakes can build up while the sample is being formed. A method can improve the result by changing the route: reward useful moves, avoid unsafe moves, preserve rare cases, or stop numerical drift. What must stay the same is the final target. What may change is each intermediate move that gets there. Name used later: a path through a probability distribution.

control each making step because step errors shape the final result
ICLR habit

Choose the space where the thing is easiest to learn

If the goal is to learn chairs, camera angle should not count as a new kind of chair. Quotient-Space Diffusion removes that kind of irrelevant change. FlashWorld, TRACE, VibeVoice, LatentLM, and video work choose spaces that match what is being generated: worlds, objects, audio, or long media.

Why it works. A generator learns whatever differences the space makes visible. If the space mixes the real thing with distractions, the model wastes effort. What must stay the same is the object being represented. What may change is viewpoint, coordinate system, file format, or output interface. Name used later: a sample space with nuisance variation removed.

learn the object in the space where irrelevant changes are ignored
DIFFERENCE 05

Data: Update Timing Versus Public Recipe Infrastructure

ICML habit

Treat data as a timed lesson

A lesson helps only if it arrives while the student can still use it. DiReCT asks which late examples can still move the model. Midtraining Bridges asks when specialized material belongs. LR-decay, annealing, and sequential data-value work ask whether the next example adds something the current model still needs.

Why it works. The same example can be useful early, useless later, or harmful if it crowds out better evidence. What must stay the same is the target behavior. What may change is which examples are shown, when they are shown, and how strongly they count. Name used later: data value conditioned on training state.

an example is valuable when it can still move the model toward the target
ICLR habit

Make the data recipe inspectable

A cookbook is not enough if nobody knows which ingredients were used or where they came from. Common Corpus tracks lawful sources. OpenThoughts publishes reasoning recipes. Retrieval, multimodal, and governance papers keep records of documents, media, filters, and source rules.

Why it works. A dataset is not just a pile of files; it is the result of choices. If those choices are visible, another person can rebuild, audit, or challenge the evidence. What must stay the same is the recipe that produced the data. What may change is the sample drawn from that recipe. Name used later: provenance and reproducible data generation.

data = sources + filters + weights + records, not only final files
DIFFERENCE 06

Systems: Behavioral Approximation Versus Deployable Form

ICML habit

Ask what can be simplified without changing behavior

If a mechanic replaces a metal part with a lighter one, the test is not whether it looks similar; the machine must still finish the same cycle. LiftQuant, TetraJet, FOCUS, RePAIR, and diffusion quantization work ask which cheaper model pieces still preserve the behavior the task needs.

Why it works. Compression is safe only when the cheaper version keeps the important cause-and-effect pattern. What must stay the same is the answer, state change, or generation path that the task reads. What may change is number precision, stored weights, removed pieces, or arithmetic. Name used later: an approximation that preserves the task-relevant function.

simplify the machine, then test the behavior the task actually uses
ICLR habit

Build the form that can run in the real system

A warehouse plan fails if the aisles are too narrow for the carts. A model also has physical limits: memory, chip layout, latency, token budget, and number precision. Mamba-3, FlashVID, InfoTok, TileLang, angle kernels, and low-precision studies shape methods around those limits.

Why it works. A method that is correct on paper can be unusable if it cannot move information fast enough or store the right state. What must stay the same is the computation needed by the task. What may change is the layout, precision, cache, kernel, or sequence model used to execute it. Name used later: a system representation.

the usable model is the idea plus the memory, layout, and timing that make it run
DIFFERENCE 07

Theory: Guarantee Boundary Versus Expressive System Model

ICML habit

State when the recipe is allowed to work

A warranty is useful because it says the conditions: normal use, correct parts, and a known time period. Theory papers do the same for learning methods. Alignment-sensitive rates, clustering-in-transformers work, generation-in-the-limit work, and Jacobian studies each say when the claim is supposed to hold.

Why it works. A claim without conditions sounds bigger than it is. These papers make the boundary visible: what kind of signal exists, how many examples are available, what the architecture can do, and where the promise stops. What must stay the same is the assumption set. What may change is the model, task, or dataset inside that set. Name used later: a guarantee under assumptions.

the claim holds only inside the stated conditions
ICLR habit

Explain what kind of work the system can do

Before trusting a machine, you want to know its basic motion: can it sort, remember, compare, solve, or match shapes? Softmax Transformers Are Turing-Complete, HATSolver, Temporal Superposition, GraphGlue, and Taming Momentum ask what kinds of work their systems can express efficiently.

Why it works. Architecture matters because it makes some operations easy and others hard. What must stay the same is the operation the task needs. What may change is the internal form used to do it: stored memory, attention layers, graph matching, algebraic steps, or low-rank optimizer state. Names used later: computational form or geometric structure.

understand the model by the operations it can carry out reliably
DIFFERENCE 08

Measurement: Estimator Validity Versus Artifact-Native Observation

ICML habit

Ask whether the score measures the right thing

A thermometer is useful only if it touches the right place, has known error, and is not consistently off. ATLAS, reliability micro-benchmarks, rare-event studies, value papers, and watermark work ask what their scores can and cannot prove.

Why it works. A score is evidence, not truth by itself. It can be noisy, biased, too narrow, or blind to alternative explanations. What must stay the same is the thing being claimed. What may change is the test item, sample size, uncertainty estimate, or comparison method. Name used later: an estimator with error.

a score supports a claim only with known uncertainty and coverage
ICLR habit

Look at the artifact where correctness actually lives

A website should be judged by opening it in a browser, not by reading a summary of its code. WebDevJudge scores rendered sites. CyberGym runs exploit artifacts. OpenApps changes real UI state. MINT and visual or scientific benchmarks inspect images, videos, proofs, code, worlds, or workflows directly.

Why it works. A measurement can fail by looking at the wrong surface. These benchmarks keep the real object in the loop, so the test sees the property that decides success or failure. What must stay the same is the connection between the artifact and the task outcome. What may change is the artifact type. Name used later: a valid observation channel.

measure the object in the form where the task is actually decided
SYNTHESIS

How To Read The Conferences Together

ICML Gives Control Knobs

When an ICML paper is strongest, it usually names a variable that can be measured, optimized, bounded, or used for intervention: trace dependence, constraint distance, curvature alignment, spectral span, precision error, tail probability, or distinguishability.

ICLR Gives System Forms

When an ICLR paper is strongest, it usually builds the setting where the behavior becomes real: an agent environment, public recipe, sample space, multimodal interface, executable verifier, retrieval loop, or deployment-shaped benchmark.

The Shared Problem Is Identification Under Action

Both conferences ask whether the system's observed behavior identifies the intended target after the system acts, learns, compresses, samples, or adapts. The methods differ, but the mathematical standard is shared: preserve the right information, remove fake variation, name the constraints, and show what evidence rules out shortcuts.
