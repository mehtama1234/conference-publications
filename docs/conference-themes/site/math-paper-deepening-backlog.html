FIRST PRINCIPLE

Depth Means The Claim Survives The Template

A paper is fully deepened only when a reader can recover the problem, state space, thing being measured or changed, preserved quantity, allowed movement, why the method works, evidence check, failure boundary, and cross-paper role. This backlog marks where that full treatment should go next.

Priority is not just popularity. A high-priority paper family either anchors many other papers, exposes a recurring failure mode, connects ICML and ICLR habits, or supplies a reusable object that appears across several lanes.

Next Iteration Rule

Choose from P0 first

Use the nine-slot template

Every selected row should become a full worked card: problem, state, object, invariant, allowed movement, method reason, equation, evidence, and cross-paper role.

Fold back into lane pages

After a paper gets full treatment, move the strongest explanation into its lane page so readers starting from themes get the same depth.
