READ FIRST

What Counts As Covered

A page is deeply covered when a reader can understand the papers without already knowing the field.

Deep

The page has first-principles framing, paper-specific objects, method logic, proof evidence, failure boundaries, and useful cross-paper synthesis.

Solid

The page explains its direct papers well, but it may still be a single-paper page, a compact cluster, or missing broader neighboring evidence.

Next Pass

The page is valid but should be expanded with clearer paper maps, more concrete examples, or stronger links to related ICML/ICLR work.

AUDIT RESULT

What The End-To-End Goal Now Means

The goal is an ICML / ICLR first-principles paper atlas, not a list of conference papers. A page counts when it teaches the paper cluster as a reusable idea: what problem is real, what concrete object is being changed, what must stay fixed, what may change, why the method works, what evidence would prove it, where it fails, and which nearby papers reveal the same principle from another angle.
