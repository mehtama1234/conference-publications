FIRST PRINCIPLE

A Subtheme Is Not A Topic; It Is A Burden Of Proof

A subtheme is mathematically useful only when it says what kind of claim the papers must justify. A reasoning subtheme must prove the trace is task-conditioned. A compression subtheme must prove the compressed state is sufficient. A benchmark subtheme must prove the score measures the target. A causal subtheme must prove the claimed effect is identified.

This ledger makes that burden explicit. It also gives the next expansion rule: when adding detail to any paper, write the row's obligation as a concrete testable condition, then show whether the paper's evidence supports it.

NEXT USE

How This Drives Paper-Level Expansion

Before adding a paper

Choose its subtheme row and inherit the proof obligation. The paper card should then explain how the method supplies evidence for that obligation.

Before comparing papers

Compare whether papers satisfy the same obligation through different objects, or whether they only share vocabulary while proving different claims.

Before trusting a result

Use the evidence-check column to decide whether the result supports the mathematical claim or only reports a surface improvement.
