FIRST PRINCIPLE

A Method Works When It Removes The Right Difficulty

A good mathematical explanation does not say that a method works because it has a new module. It says which difficulty the module removes. It may remove nuisance symmetry, invalid actions, irrelevant history, variance, noisy measurement, unrecoverable directions, strategic mismatch, or overclaiming beyond the evidence.

The sketches below are reusable. Each one is a compact argument that appears across many papers in different surface forms.

Invariance
Remove fake differences before learning.
Feasibility
Search only where outputs can be valid.
Sufficiency
Compress history without losing decisions.
Measurement
Make scores identify the hidden target.
Tail Sampling
Make rare failures observable.
Spectral Visibility
Use directions recoverable above noise.
Update Geometry
Move in the metric that governs loss.
Transport
Control the path of probability mass.
Identification
Do not claim more than evidence distinguishes.
Verification
Tie claims to checkable artifacts.
Mechanism
Model actors that respond.
Latent Interface
Control through a hidden coordinate.
SKETCH 01

Invariance Reduces The Problem

Assumption. Some transformations change the encoding but not the object: rotation, translation, camera viewpoint, node relabeling, prompt surface form, or coordinate frame.

Move. Train or sample on equivalence classes instead of raw encodings.

if y(x) = y(gx) for g in G, learn on quotient space X / G instead of X

Why It Works

The model no longer spends capacity distinguishing copies of the same object. Sample complexity drops because one example informs every equivalent encoding.

Papers

Quotient-Space Diffusion, Camera-Aware MLLM, GraphGlue, protein generation, transformer clustering circuits, TideGS/RadioGS.

Breaks When

The transformation is not a true nuisance. Orientation may matter near a binding site; viewpoint may matter when camera geometry is part of the task.

SKETCH 02

Feasibility Changes The Search Space

Assumption. Valid outputs live in a smaller set than all high-scoring outputs.

Move. Build constraints into decoding, projection, policy update, coupling, or route construction.

solve maximize reward(x) subject to x in C, not maximize reward(x) then filter invalid x

Why It Works

Optimization pressure cannot spend probability mass outside the allowed set. The method avoids learning attractive but unusable artifacts.

Papers

Pi-net, CSPO, Copyright-Bench, ReaSyn, MORetro*, Fair OT, Robust Contextual Optimization, PACT, HDFlow.

Breaks When

The feasible set is misspecified, too conservative, or checked in the wrong representation.

SKETCH 03

Sufficiency Preserves Decision Information

Assumption. A compressed state can preserve everything needed for the future task.

Move. Replace full history with a state, cache, trace summary, memory, or latent that is sufficient for downstream prediction or action.

P(y | history) = P(y | state); then history can be replaced by state for target y

Why It Works

The system saves compute while keeping the conditional information that determines the next step.

Papers

ThinKV, STAR-KV, EntroKV, DLMR, Skill-Pro, VideoKR, Mamba Markov ICL, RAGEN-2, IRNO.

Breaks When

The summary keeps common evidence but drops rare dependencies, hidden constraints, or long-range information needed later.

SKETCH 04

Measurement Makes A Score Meaningful

Assumption. The target is hidden and only observed through tasks, judges, artifacts, or experts.

Move. Model the measurement channel: item difficulty, judge error, rubric dimensions, uncertainty, and artifact form.

observed score = target signal + noise + judge bias + task artifact

Why It Works

The evaluation can estimate what it actually observes and report uncertainty instead of treating a score as direct capability.

Papers

ATLAS, FRABench, UFEval, JudgeBench, WebDevJudge, CounselBench, MADQA, Seizure-Semiology-Suite.

Breaks When

The channel misses the target, aggregates incompatible dimensions, or rewards shortcuts indistinguishable from ability.

SKETCH 05

Tail Sampling Makes Rare Risk Visible

Assumption. The event that matters has small probability under ordinary sampling but large consequence under deployment.

Move. Sample from a proposal that visits the rare region and correct the estimate back to the deployment distribution.

P(E) = E_Q[1{Y in E} * p(Y) / q(Y)]

Why It Works

Samples are spent where the indicator is informative. Reweighting preserves the deployment claim if proposal support and variance are controlled.

Papers

Rare Event Analysis, Tail Risks, Control Consistency Losses, SandboxEscapeBench, Jailbreak Foundry, FALCON, SRMC.

Breaks When

The harmful event is misspecified, the proposal misses a failure mode, or likelihood weights become too variable.

SKETCH 06

Spectral Visibility Explains Generalization

Assumption. Useful signal appears in directions that can be separated from noise or curvature.

Move. Decompose the representation, gradient, kernel, attention map, or logit matrix into singular or eigen directions.

recover signal when leading direction separates from noise bulk by a stable gap

Why It Works

Finite data estimates strong directions more reliably. Generalization improves when target signal lies in recoverable directions.

Papers

Alignment-Sensitive Minimax Rates, Single-Head Attention, BBP Transitions, Low-Rank LM Logits, SVD Interpretability, DiReCT, LoRA Theory.

Breaks When

The basis is unstable, the signal is nonlinear in a way the spectrum misses, or top directions reflect dataset artifacts.

SKETCH 07

Update Geometry Controls What Can Move

Assumption. The same update vector has different effects depending on metric, curvature, rank, precision, and trust region.

Move. Measure and constrain updates in the geometry the optimizer or deployment system actually uses.

theta_{t+1} = theta_t + Delta theta, with Delta theta constrained by metric M, rank r, curvature H, or KL radius

Why It Works

The method avoids harmful directions while preserving movement that improves the target. It explains why data, adapters, RL, and optimizers can help or damage the same model.

Papers

Adam Degeneracy, DiReCT, LoRA Theory, Trust-Region LLM RL, Beyond Muon, Non-Euclidean Edge of Stability, RAIN-Merging.

Breaks When

The measured geometry omits deployment risks, high-curvature estimates are noisy, or the constraint protects the wrong behavior.

SKETCH 08

Transport Controls The Distribution Path

Assumption. The path taken by probability mass affects final likelihood, safety, diversity, or reward.

Move. Choose a denoiser, bridge, flow, proposal, or reward tilt that moves mass through useful regions.

p_t evolves by vector field, denoising update, bridge, or reward-tilted transition toward p_target

Why It Works

The method controls intermediate distributions rather than hoping final samples pass checks. This matters for modes, tails, constraints, and accumulated numerical error.

Papers

DMPO, FALCON, Safety-Guided Flow, Cauchy Bridges, GWF, MMOT/Schrodinger Bridges, Quantized Diffusion.

Breaks When

The path changes the target distribution, misses support, or accumulates approximation error unseen by endpoint metrics.

SKETCH 09

Identification Prevents Overclaiming

Assumption. Observed evidence may be compatible with multiple worlds, graphs, effects, or mechanisms.

Move. Return the claim that is invariant across all worlds consistent with data and assumptions, or state the equivalence class.

claim c is identified if c(w) is constant for all w in W(data, assumptions)

Why It Works

The method separates evidence from story. It only asserts what the data and assumptions force.

Papers

Distributional Equivalence, Local Covariate Selection, Structure Learning CI, DiCoLa, Unpaired Causal IV, BRCD, Latent Hawkes, CausalGame.

Breaks When

Assumptions are wrong, tests are underpowered, or the method silently chooses one explanation inside an indistinguishable class.

SKETCH 10

Verification Externalizes The Claim

Assumption. A generated answer can be plausible without satisfying the real condition.

Move. Attach the claim to a checkable artifact: execution, formal proof, rendered page, indistinguishability test, or finite-sample certificate.

accept claim only if verifier V(artifact, assumptions, observation channel) passes

Why It Works

The evaluator no longer trusts the generator's narration. It asks a separate procedure to reject invalid artifacts.

Papers

CyberGym, VERINA, WZ-LLM, PaperBench, WebDevJudge, OmniVerifier, Gaussian Certified Unlearning, Finite Test Certification.

Breaks When

The verifier observes the wrong artifact, is too narrow, is noisy in an exploitable direction, or certifies only a proxy condition.

SKETCH 11

Mechanisms Model Response

Assumption. Other actors adapt to the model, metric, market, defense, or decision rule.

Move. Treat the system as a game or mechanism with payoffs, information, constraints, and response dynamics.

choose mechanism M so responses pi_i form acceptable outcomes under incentives and constraints

Why It Works

The method includes strategic response inside the object rather than treating it as external noise.

Papers

AdvGame, OMAC, Accuracy Auctions, Data Market Pricing, Fair Causal Bandits, Constrained Nash, MASPOB, Value of Variance.

Breaks When

Actors have different incentives, information, collusion channels, or adaptation speeds than the mechanism assumes.

SKETCH 12

Latent Interfaces Make Control Local

Assumption. A hidden representation contains a coordinate where the desired intervention is simpler than in raw input space.

Move. Learn or identify a latent variable, token, slot, activation direction, or shared code that supports decoding, transfer, or intervention.

find z = f(x) so target-relevant changes are sparse, stable, or causal in z

Why It Works

Control becomes local when the right hidden variable is exposed. Steering, fusion, brain decoding, and multimodal generation all become easier if the interface preserves the causal variable.

Papers

Steer Like The LLM, The Tell-Tale Norm, MINT, Causal Route Gating, Mind-Omni, PRISM, CompSLOT, LatentLM, UALM.

Breaks When

The latent is entangled, non-transferable, or aligned with a shortcut rather than the intended concept.

BIG PICTURE

How To Use These Sketches

When reading any new paper, first ask which proof sketch it is relying on. If the paper removes nuisance variation, look for an invariance argument. If it prevents invalid outputs, look for a feasible-set argument. If it compresses context, look for a sufficiency argument. If it evaluates models, look for a measurement argument. If it claims safety, look for tail or boundary evidence.

The approach is credible when the evidence tests the exact assumption used by the sketch. Otherwise the paper may still be useful, but the mathematical reason has not been shown.
