Why Exemplars Matter

The shorter atlas cards are useful for coverage. They are not always enough to show why a method works mathematically. These exemplars make the reasoning explicit: what is being estimated, constrained, routed, preserved, or certified, and why that object changes the problem rather than merely changing the implementation.

The shared picture is that modern AI papers are about preserving a claim through a transformation. The transformation may be training, reasoning, compression, sampling, evaluation, intervention, or deployment. The mathematics is the rule that says which transformations are allowed and which evidence proves the preserved claim survived.

EXEMPLAR 01

RAGEN-2

Reasoning Collapse As Lost Dependence

Problem

Agentic RL can increase the amount of reasoning text while the reasoning stops depending on the particular input. The visible trace is longer, but the information carried by the trace is lower.

State Space

The state is an interaction trajectory: prompt, internal reasoning trace, actions, observations, verifier feedback, and final answer.

Object

The mathematical object is conditional dependence between the trace and the task instance. A healthy trace changes when the problem changes; a collapsed trace is a template with weak input dependence.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is task-specific information. Training may change style, length, and policy logits, but it should not erase information from the input that is needed to solve the task.

Allowed Movement

RL may move the policy toward higher reward, but only in ways that keep the trajectory conditioned on the environment and not just on the reward model's preferred surface pattern.

Why It Works

The method works mathematically when it treats reasoning as an information channel, not a transcript. If the channel carries input information, longer or more structured traces can help. If the channel collapses, extra reasoning becomes an optimized artifact with little evidence value.

healthy trace: I(T; X | task) remains high while reward increases; collapse: I(T; X | task) drops

Evidence Check

Perturb the input, hold task format fixed, and measure whether trace structure, action choice, and intermediate claims move with the perturbation. Compare entropy with input-dependence because entropy alone can look healthy while templates persist.

Failure Boundary

The diagnostic can miss collapse if the perturbations do not touch the features the policy actually uses, or if the model preserves shallow input markers while losing the causal reasoning path.

Connection

h1 tries to preserve learning signal across longer horizons; The Tell-Tale Norm turns internal state magnitude into a compute-control signal; ParetoPO asks whether tool use is worth its cost. All are versions of one question: when should a system spend reasoning, and how do we know that spending is still tied to the task?

EXEMPLAR 02

ATLAS

Evaluation As Adaptive Measurement

Problem

A fixed benchmark wastes many items because easy questions do not distinguish strong models and irrelevant questions do not reduce uncertainty about the ability being claimed.

State Space

The state is a posterior over model ability, plus an item bank whose questions have difficulty, discrimination, and noise.

Object

The mathematical object is a measurement instrument: an item-response model that maps hidden ability to observed answers.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is rank or ability information. Evaluation should compress many possible questions into a smaller test without changing the capability claim.

Allowed Movement

The test may adaptively choose the next item based on current posterior uncertainty. It may not choose items that maximize convenience while losing information about the target ability.

Why It Works

Adaptive testing works when each selected item has high Fisher information near the current ability estimate. The benchmark becomes an estimator, not a static checklist.

choose item i maximizing I_i(theta) = E[(d/dtheta log p(y_i | theta))^2] near posterior mass

Evidence Check

Report posterior intervals, item-information gain, agreement with full-test rankings, sensitivity to starting estimates, and cases where adaptive paths choose different items for different model regimes.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when the item-response model is misspecified, when item banks omit important capability dimensions, or when models exploit item-selection patterns.

Connection

FRABench and UFEval factor scores and uncertainty; Micro-Benchmarking Reliability asks when ranks are statistically stable; CounselBench and WebDevJudge change the observation channel. All say that evaluation is measurement design before it is ranking.

EXEMPLAR 03

Rare Event Analysis And Tail Risks

Safety As Tail Probability

Problem

Average benchmark behavior can look safe while rare failures dominate deployment harm. Random sampling often misses the events users most need measured.

State Space

The state is an output, action, or trajectory distribution with a harmful subset defined by policy, security, physical, or social consequences.

Object

The mathematical object is tail probability or tail loss: probability mass inside a harmful event set, sometimes with severity weighting.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is the deployment-distribution estimate. Sampling may be biased toward failures, but the final estimate must still refer back to the real distribution.

Allowed Movement

The evaluator may sample from a proposal distribution that visits rare regions more often, but must correct for the change of measure.

Why It Works

Importance sampling works because it spends samples where the indicator is informative, then reweights by the likelihood ratio. It turns invisible risk into estimable risk if the proposal covers the harmful region without exploding variance.

P(E_bad) = E_Q[1{Y in E_bad} * p(Y) / q(Y)], with Q chosen to cover E_bad

Evidence Check

State the event grammar, report confidence intervals, effective sample size, proposal sensitivity, boundary sensitivity, and examples near the event threshold.

Failure Boundary

The estimate fails if the harmful event set is wrong, if the proposal misses a failure mode, or if likelihood weights have extreme variance.

Connection

CSPO measures distance to violation; SandboxEscapeBench and Jailbreak Foundry apply adaptive pressure; FALCON and SRMC face the same sampling-support problem in molecular and generative settings.

EXEMPLAR 04

Midtraining Bridges

Training As Timed Distribution Movement

Problem

Specialized data can help at one training phase and do little or harm at another. Data value depends on where the model currently is, not only on the dataset's standalone quality.

State Space

The state is the model parameters and optimizer state moving through a sequence of training distributions: broad pretraining, specialized bridging data, and posttraining targets.

Object

The mathematical object is a path through distributions. A data mixture is useful when it moves the model toward the target while preserving plasticity and avoiding interference.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is transferable capability. The model should gain specialized structure without losing the general representation needed for later alignment or task adaptation.

Allowed Movement

The training process may change data mixture weights over time, but the movement should remain inside a region where gradients still produce useful updates.

Why It Works

A bridge distribution works if its gradients are neither too generic nor too late. It supplies intermediate evidence at a time when the model can still reshape representations toward the final task family.

use data D_b at time t when alignment(g_b,t, g_target,t) is high and interference with g_base,t is bounded

Evidence Check

Compare early, middle, and late insertion; measure target gains, general-capability loss, gradient alignment, representation drift, and whether the same data helps under different training states.

Failure Boundary

The bridge fails if it arrives after plasticity has narrowed, if it over-specializes the representation, or if the target distribution was misidentified.

Connection

DiReCT chooses late data by curvature; Sequential Data Values makes marginal data value order-dependent; BLL-Loss and OPUS select examples by optimizer effect. All treat data as movement, not fuel.

EXEMPLAR 05

Quotient-Space Diffusion

Generation Modulo Symmetry

Problem

Molecular coordinates contain irrelevant degrees of freedom. Rotating or translating the molecule changes coordinates but not molecular identity.

State Space

The state is a molecular configuration, but the meaningful space is not raw Euclidean coordinates. It is configurations modulo rigid transformations and other symmetries.

Object

The mathematical object is a quotient space: many coordinate arrays represent the same molecule, and the model should operate on the equivalence class.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is molecular identity and geometry that affects chemical behavior. Coordinate frame accidents should not alter likelihood, denoising direction, or generated structure.

Allowed Movement

The sampler may move through noisy molecular states, but movement must respect equivalence under symmetry and should not learn artifacts of arbitrary orientation.

Why It Works

Quotient modeling removes nuisance variation before learning the distribution. This reduces the burden on the model: it no longer has to relearn that all rotated copies should be treated as the same object.

model distribution over configurations x modulo group G: p([x]) where [x] = {g x : g in G}

Evidence Check

Test rotation and translation invariance, molecular validity, energy or property preservation, sample diversity, and whether denoising trajectories remain consistent across equivalent coordinate frames.

Failure Boundary

The approach fails if the quotient removes information that actually matters, or if downstream tasks need orientation relative to a binding pocket, surface, or external field.

Connection

Protein hierarchy papers preserve residue and fold constraints; TideGS and RadioGS preserve physical fields; HDFlow and PACT preserve action feasibility. All start by choosing the native space where validity is defined.

EXEMPLAR 06

Alignment-Sensitive Minimax Rates

Generalization Through Spectral Placement

Problem

Two representations can have similar size or kernel complexity but different generalization because the target signal sits in different directions.

State Space

The state is a learned feature or kernel space decomposed into spectral directions with different eigenvalues and noise levels.

Object

The mathematical object is target alignment with the leading recoverable spectral span.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is target signal above noise. Generalization depends on whether the learned representation keeps the task-relevant part in directions that finite data can estimate.

Allowed Movement

Learning may reshape the representation, but useful movement increases target mass in high-signal directions instead of spreading it through noisy components.

Why It Works

Finite samples recover large-eigenvalue directions more reliably. If the target is aligned with those directions, fewer samples are needed; if the target is hidden in low-eigenvalue directions, nominal model capacity does not help much.

risk controlled by effective span: sum target_mass_j over directions where lambda_j exceeds noise threshold

Evidence Check

Measure spectral concentration of target signal, sample-size scaling, perturbation sensitivity, and pairs of representations with similar complexity but different alignment.

Failure Boundary

The theory can mislead if the learned spectrum changes during training, if the noise model is wrong, or if downstream tasks rely on nonlinear structure not captured by the spectral basis.

Connection

Single-Head Attention studies spectral recovery in attention; BBP transitions ask when spikes separate from noise; LoRA theory asks whether task movement lives in a low-rank subspace. All are signal-visibility arguments.

EXEMPLAR 07

Distributional Equivalence

Causal Discovery As An Identified Set

Problem

Observed data can be compatible with multiple causal graphs, especially with latent variables or cycles. Returning one graph can overstate what the evidence identifies.

State Space

The state is a family of causal graphs and latent structures that imply observable distributions.

Object

The mathematical object is an equivalence class: all causal structures that cannot be distinguished by the observed distribution and stated assumptions.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is epistemic honesty. The output should preserve the boundary between identified structure and plausible but unverified structure.

Allowed Movement

The algorithm may narrow the graph class using constraints such as edge ranks, independences, and distributional signatures, but cannot choose among indistinguishable alternatives without additional assumptions.

Why It Works

The method works by replacing overconfident graph recovery with identified-set recovery. A causal claim is allowed only when all structures in the equivalence class agree on it.

claim c is identified if c(G) is the same for every G with P_obs(G) = P_obs and satisfying assumptions A

Evidence Check

Report equivalence-class size, rank-constraint calibration, latent-variable stress tests, false edge and missing edge rates, and examples where the method correctly refuses to identify a unique graph.

Failure Boundary

The output is only as good as the assumptions defining the graph family and the statistical tests used to estimate distributional constraints.

Connection

Local Covariate Selection identifies adjustment for a target effect rather than a whole graph; DiCoLa scales CI evidence; CausalGame gathers interventions to separate mechanisms. All protect the same boundary: what the evidence distinguishes from what a model can narrate.

EXEMPLAR 08

Steer Like The LLM

Prompting As Activation Displacement

Problem

Prompt words are an indirect control interface. They may steer behavior, but they also consume context, interact with unrelated instructions, and make the control signal hard to isolate.

State Space

The state is the model's hidden activation trajectory across layers and tokens, plus the output distribution induced by that trajectory.

Object

The mathematical object is an activation displacement: the vector difference between the unprompted hidden state and the hidden state produced by an instruction.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is the intended behavioral change caused by the prompt, separated from the incidental surface words that produced it.

Allowed Movement

The method may move hidden states along learned steering directions, but should not disturb unrelated latent features or collapse output diversity outside the target behavior.

Why It Works

If a prompt reliably changes behavior through a stable internal direction, a learned steering model can approximate that direction directly. Control becomes a map in representation space instead of a fragile natural-language prefix.

learn delta h ~= h(prompted, x) - h(base, x); apply h' = h + delta h_target

Evidence Check

Compare prompt steering and activation steering on the same tasks, ablate layers, test off-target behavior, measure output-distribution movement, and check whether the steering direction transfers across inputs.

Failure Boundary

The approach fails if the prompt effect is context-specific, nonlinear across layers, or entangled with features that should not move.

Connection

The Tell-Tale Norm also turns hidden-state measurements into control. MINT, Causal Route Gating, and activation-oracle work use interventions to ask which hidden route actually causes the output.

EXEMPLAR 09

RLVepsR

Verifier Labels As Noisy Gradients

Problem

RL from verifier feedback can improve a model only if verifier labels are correlated with the true target. Weak verifiers can produce learning, no learning, or anti-learning depending on their error structure.

State Space

The state is a policy distribution over outputs and a verifier observation channel that maps output artifacts to noisy labels or rewards.

Object

The mathematical object is gradient alignment between the verifier-induced update and the update that would be induced by true task success.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is target-directed learning signal. The verifier may be cheap or weak, but its reward must point the policy in roughly the same direction as real correctness.

Allowed Movement

The policy may move toward higher verifier reward, but only while verifier reward remains a useful proxy for the target. When false positives dominate, movement should stop or defer.

Why It Works

Policy-gradient learning multiplies score-function gradients by a reward signal. If the reward is noisy but aligned, averaging can still move the model toward the target. If it is biased toward shortcuts, optimization amplifies the shortcut.

useful RL needs E[grad log pi(y|x) V(x,y)] aligned with E[grad log pi(y|x) T(x,y)]

Evidence Check

Sweep verifier false-positive and false-negative rates, measure learn/stall/anti-learn regimes, audit disagreement cases, and compare weak-verifier updates to stronger oracle or human judgments.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when verifier errors are systematic in the same direction the policy can exploit, or when the verifier observes the wrong artifact.

Connection

This is the training version of benchmark validity. It connects JudgeBench, WebDevJudge, CyberGym, formal proof checking, and verifier-gaming chains: the optimization target inherits the measurement channel's errors.

EXEMPLAR 10

DiReCT

Data Choice As Curvature-Aware Movement

Problem

Late-training examples can improve validation behavior or damage brittle directions. Data quality alone is not enough; the current curvature of the model decides whether an update is safe.

State Space

The state is parameter space near a trained model, equipped with gradients from candidate examples and validation curvature directions.

Object

The mathematical object is a proposed displacement decomposed into flat and stiff validation-Hessian eigenspaces.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is validation performance in high-curvature directions. The model can move, but not through directions where small changes cause large loss increases.

Allowed Movement

Select examples whose gradients align with flat useful directions and have bounded energy in stiff harmful directions.

Why It Works

A gradient is valuable only relative to the local geometry. Flat directions allow task improvement with less risk; stiff directions behave like fragile constraints around the current solution.

prefer g_x with high ||P_flat g_x|| and low sum_{lambda_j large} lambda_j <g_x, v_j>^2

Evidence Check

Measure Hessian-direction energy, validation movement, selected-versus-random data, timing sensitivity, and ablations that use gradient magnitude without curvature.

Failure Boundary

The method fails if the Hessian estimate is noisy, if validation curvature misses deployment failures, or if flat directions encode shortcuts.

Connection

Midtraining Bridges studies when data should arrive; Sequential Data Values studies order-dependent value; LoRA theory studies constrained parameter movement. DiReCT gives the local geometry version of the same idea.

EXEMPLAR 11

ReaSyn And MORetro*

Molecules As Route-Constrained Objects

Problem

A generated molecule can have attractive predicted properties while being hard or impossible to synthesize. The valid object is not only a graph; it is a graph with a route.

State Space

The state includes molecular graph, building blocks, reactions, retrosynthesis steps, conformation, target context, and property constraints.

Object

The mathematical object is a feasible set of molecule-route pairs. A candidate is valid only if the molecule and route satisfy chemical constraints together.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is chemical feasibility under optimization. Property search should not move the molecule outside the set reachable by plausible synthesis.

Allowed Movement

The generator may change structure, route, or building blocks, but movement must keep reaction constraints and target constraints consistent.

Why It Works

Route-aware generation works because it changes the optimization domain. Instead of optimizing over all strings or graphs and checking synthesis later, it searches in a space where route feasibility is part of the object.

optimize property(m) subject to exists route r with C_reaction(m,r) and C_target(m) satisfied

Evidence Check

Report route validity, building-block availability, reaction-template success, property-route tradeoffs, novelty, and whether optimizing property increases infeasible candidates.

Failure Boundary

The method fails if route templates are incomplete, if predicted reactions do not hold experimentally, or if route feasibility conflicts with biological target constraints.

Connection

Quotient-Space Diffusion handles identity under symmetry; DCFold and protein hierarchy papers handle folded structure; HDFlow and PACT handle feasible action. All make validity a constraint on the state space.

EXEMPLAR 12

Error Propagation In Quantized Diffusion Models

Compression As Accumulated Trajectory Error

Problem

Quantization can look accurate at one denoising step but drift across the full sampling trajectory. The final image or sample inherits accumulated numerical error.

State Space

The state is the diffusion sampler trajectory: noisy sample, timestep, denoiser prediction, quantized approximation, and next state.

Object

The mathematical object is error propagation through an iterative dynamical system.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is the sampler path distribution. Quantization should preserve not only local denoiser output but also the distribution reached after many steps.

Allowed Movement

The method may approximate weights or activations, but the approximation must be timestep-aware because early and late errors have different downstream effects.

Why It Works

A diffusion sampler repeatedly feeds approximation error back into the next state. Modeling accumulated sensitivity lets the quantizer spend precision where the trajectory is most vulnerable.

e_{t-1} ~= J_t e_t + q_t; final error depends on products of sampler Jacobians and per-step quantization q_t

Evidence Check

Measure per-timestep sensitivity, final sample degradation, distribution shift, long-step stability, calibration data effects, and comparisons against one-step quantization metrics.

Failure Boundary

The model fails if the linearized propagation estimate misses nonlinear sampler behavior, or if quantization changes rare modes that image metrics underweight.

Connection

LiftQuant, TetraJet, FOCUS/RePAIR, and finite-precision theory all say compression is behavioral: numerical changes can alter the function, path, or reasoning loop being preserved.

EXEMPLAR 13

VISUALSWAP And MINT

Multimodal Answers As Causal Evidence Routes

Problem

A multimodal model can answer from language priors while appearing visually grounded. The final answer does not prove which modality caused it.

State Space

The state includes image evidence, text evidence, hidden fusion state, attention or routing paths, and final answer distribution.

Object

The mathematical object is causal dependence of the answer on the intended evidence channel.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is grounding: the answer should change when relevant visual evidence changes and remain stable when irrelevant nuisance evidence changes.

Allowed Movement

The evaluator may swap visual evidence, intervene on hidden routes, or patch memory, but the intervention must isolate the channel being tested.

Why It Works

Counterfactual swaps and hidden-state interventions work because they ask a causal question, not a correlation question: would the answer have changed if the relevant evidence route were different?

grounded answer needs y(e_visual, e_text) to vary with relevant e_visual and stay stable under nuisance changes

Evidence Check

Run visual swaps, route ablations, memory patching, attribution over time, text-prior controls, and cases where visual and language priors disagree.

Failure Boundary

The method fails if swaps introduce artifacts, if the intervention changes multiple channels at once, or if attribution is measured at a layer that is not causally upstream of the answer.

Connection

MoCA separates perception and reasoning failure; Causal Route Gating suppresses prior-heavy routes; DLMR tracks memory provenance; PRISM and Mind-Omni define domain-native latent interfaces. All are modality-credit problems.

EXEMPLAR 14

Skill-Pro

Agent Experience As Reusable Procedure

Problem

An agent that solves a task once often wastes future compute rediscovering the same workflow. But blindly replaying past trajectories is unsafe because a workflow is valid only under the right preconditions.

State Space

The state is an agent trajectory with user goal, environment observations, tool calls, file or world changes, verifier outputs, and termination condition.

Object

The mathematical object is an executable skill: a partial policy with activation conditions, internal steps, and stopping rule.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is procedural correctness. Compressing an episode into a skill should preserve the dependency structure that made the original trajectory succeed.

Allowed Movement

The system may abstract away incidental details of one episode, but it must keep the variables that decide whether the skill applies and when it should stop.

Why It Works

Reusable skills work when they turn a long search problem into a verified option in a semi-Markov decision process. The agent no longer searches over every primitive action; it selects a validated macro-action whose preconditions summarize the needed state.

skill o = (I_o, pi_o, beta_o): activate in I_o, execute pi_o, terminate with beta_o

Evidence Check

Test skill reuse on shifted tasks, precondition failures, stale environment state, wrong termination, and comparison with replaying raw trajectories or solving from scratch.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when activation conditions omit hidden state, when environment dynamics change, or when the skill compresses away a dependency needed for correctness.

Connection

CyberGym and WebDevJudge verify executable artifacts; ThinKV compresses context into future-relevant state; h1 composes short dependencies into longer reasoning paths. Skill-Pro is the agent-memory version of sufficient-state compression.

EXEMPLAR 15

ParetoPO

Tool Use As A Cost-Accuracy Frontier

Problem

Tool-using agents can improve answers by calling tools, but extra calls cost latency, money, risk, and failure opportunities. A single accuracy score hides whether the agent spent action wisely.

State Space

The state is an agent policy over internal reasoning, tool calls, observations, final answers, and accumulated cost.

Object

The mathematical object is a Pareto frontier over task success and resource use.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is useful performance under cost. A policy should not be called better if it raises accuracy only by spending disproportionate tools.

Allowed Movement

Policy optimization may trade cost for accuracy, but the tradeoff should be visible as a frontier before choosing any scalar weight.

Why It Works

Pareto optimization works because many agent objectives are genuinely multi-objective. The right object is not one reward number; it is the set of policies where no objective can improve without another worsening.

pi is efficient if no pi' has accuracy(pi') >= accuracy(pi) and cost(pi') <= cost(pi), with one strict

Evidence Check

Report frontier curves, task-level tool budgets, marginal value of additional calls, robustness under cost weights, and examples where fewer tool calls produce equal correctness.

Failure Boundary

The frontier can mislead if cost omits verifier burden, security exposure, human review time, or downstream damage from wrong tool use.

Connection

VALUEFLOW and SafeDPO expose value-safety frontiers; LiftQuant and TetraJet expose accuracy-latency-precision frontiers; watermark and privacy papers expose detection-utility frontiers. ParetoPO puts agents into the same tradeoff geometry.

EXEMPLAR 16

WebDevJudge, PaperBench, And VERINA

Evaluation As Native Artifact Verification

Problem

Complex outputs such as web apps, scientific papers, code changes, and proofs can look good in text while failing in the artifact that matters. A natural-language judge may grade the explanation instead of the object.

State Space

The state is the artifact graph: files, tests, browser behavior, claims, citations, proof obligations, dependencies, and execution traces.

Object

The mathematical object is a certificate relation between a claim and a witness. The verifier should accept only when the witness supports the claim in the artifact's native medium.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is artifact correctness. The evaluation may compress rich behavior into a score, but it must preserve whether the real artifact works.

Allowed Movement

The evaluator may decompose the artifact into subchecks, but the decomposition must preserve dependencies among subclaims, tests, and runtime states.

Why It Works

Native verification works because it observes the variable where correctness lives. A web app is checked by rendering and interaction, a proof by formal obligations, a paper by claim-evidence dependencies, and code by execution.

accept claim c only if verifier V(c, witness w, artifact a) checks the dependency path supporting c

Evidence Check

Report verifier false positives, false negatives, execution logs, dependency failures, human disagreement, and cases where text-level judging disagrees with artifact-level checking.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when the verifier observes only an easy proxy, when dependency decomposition omits interactions, or when generated artifacts exploit the verifier's blind spots.

Connection

CyberGym makes agent tasks executable; RLVepsR studies what happens when verifier labels become gradients; PaperBench and RefineStat connect artifact checking to scientific claims. The common object is a checkable witness, not a fluent answer.

EXEMPLAR 17

ThinKV, STAR-KV, And EntroKV

Context Compression As Sufficient State

Problem

Long-context models cannot always retain every token at full cost. But dropping the wrong token can remove the fact that determines a later answer.

State Space

The state is a history of tokens, keys, values, attention patterns, retrieval needs, and future prediction tasks.

Object

The mathematical object is a compressed sufficient statistic of the history for future decisions.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is future-relevant information. Compression may discard redundant tokens, but it should keep evidence that can change later predictions.

Allowed Movement

The system may evict, merge, route, or quantize cache entries, but only under a scoring rule tied to future conditional behavior rather than present salience alone.

Why It Works

Cache compression works when the retained state is close to sufficient: conditioning on the compressed cache gives nearly the same next-action distribution as conditioning on the full history.

good cache C(H) satisfies P(y_future | H) ~= P(y_future | C(H)) for target task family

Evidence Check

Compare against full context on delayed facts, rare evidence, multi-hop references, adversarial distractors, long video memory, and tasks where early tokens become important late.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when importance is estimated from local attention to common tokens, when rare evidence has low immediate salience, or when future tasks differ from cache calibration tasks.

Connection

Skill-Pro compresses agent experience into procedural state; VideoKR compresses temporal evidence; Mamba Markov ICL asks when recurrent state is enough. These are all sufficient-state questions under resource limits.

EXEMPLAR 18

CSPO, SafeDPO, And SecFid

Safety As Constrained Movement

Problem

Safety methods often optimize helpfulness and then penalize unsafe outputs afterward. That leaves the policy free to search through regions that should never be reachable.

State Space

The state is a policy distribution over responses, actions, or trajectories together with safety labels, uncertainty, and distance to violation.

Object

The mathematical object is a feasible set of allowed policy movements. The policy should improve utility while remaining inside a safety region.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is constraint satisfaction near the boundary. The model can become more useful, but not by increasing probability mass on unsafe or unverified regions.

Allowed Movement

Optimization may change preference ranking, refusal behavior, or generation probability, but only under a constraint that tracks unsafe acceptances and overbroad refusals.

Why It Works

Constrained movement works because it changes the geometry of search. Instead of hoping a penalty is large enough after a violation appears, the feasible set defines which local directions are allowed before the optimizer moves.

maximize U(pi) subject to g_safety(pi) <= epsilon and distance(pi, boundary) remains controlled

Evidence Check

Run two-sided boundary audits: unsafe acceptance, false refusal, adaptive prompts near the boundary, subgroup behavior, and utility loss at different constraint levels.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when the safety set is misspecified, when constraints observe only a proxy, or when the boundary shifts under adversarial pressure.

Connection

Fair optimal transport constrains couplings; PACT constrains robot action; Copyright-Bench constrains legal agent behavior; VALUEFLOW exposes value-safety tradeoffs. All replace scalar reward with feasible movement.

EXEMPLAR 19

LiftQuant, TetraJet, And QAT Scaling

Low-Bit Deployment As Finite-Machine Approximation

Problem

Low-bit models can look close to full-precision models on ordinary metrics while changing reasoning stability, repetition behavior, calibration, or hardware-specific numerical paths.

State Space

The state is the deployed computation graph: weights, activations, quantizers, scaling rules, kernels, memory layout, and target hardware arithmetic.

Object

The mathematical object is an approximation map from the ideal model to a finite-machine model.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is task behavior under deployment inputs, not bit count alone. Approximation should preserve logits, decisions, stability, and latency within acceptable bounds.

Allowed Movement

The system may quantize, rescale, fuse kernels, or train with low precision, but movement must respect the arithmetic and layout actually used at serving time.

Why It Works

Quantization works when the induced perturbation is small in directions that affect behavior. Scaling and training can move error into less harmful coordinates, but only relative to the real machine's operations.

need loss(f_q(x), f(x)) small for deployment x, with q determined by finite arithmetic and hardware kernels

Evidence Check

Report hardware traces, precision ablations, long-output behavior, calibration drift, latency-memory curves, and tasks where low-bit errors are amplified.

Failure Boundary

The guarantee fails when simulated quantization differs from deployed kernels, when rare tokens or long sequences amplify error, or when average accuracy hides behavior drift.

Connection

Error propagation in quantized diffusion studies path-level numerical error; FOCUS and RePAIR study pruning-induced behavior loops; TileLang makes machine layout explicit. These papers all treat deployment as finite arithmetic, not ideal algebra.

EXEMPLAR 20

Safety-Guided Flow And DMPO

Reward And Safety As Distribution Transport

Problem

Guidance and reward optimization can improve samples or text, but they can also collapse diversity, miss rare modes, or push generation toward reward artifacts.

State Space

The state is a generative trajectory: source noise or base distribution, intermediate samples, guidance or reward signal, and final structured output.

Object

The mathematical object is a path between distributions, modified by safety or reward information.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is target support under useful guidance. The sampler should increase safe or rewarded mass without erasing valid modes that the base model represented.

Allowed Movement

Guidance may tilt the path toward safer or higher-reward regions, but the tilt must be compatible with the model's sampling process and the true support of the target domain.

Why It Works

Distribution guidance works when it changes local velocity or score estimates so probability mass flows away from bad regions and toward desired regions while remaining absolutely continuous with the relevant target support.

guided path changes p_t by reward or safety tilt, but must keep support(p_target) covered

Evidence Check

Audit mode recall, support coverage, reward-target disagreement, safety boundary cases, diversity under guidance strength, and rare safe outputs.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when guidance overfits a proxy reward, when the path leaves important modes unsupported, or when non-autoregressive sampling is treated like a token policy with the wrong action structure.

Connection

RLVepsR studies reward-channel alignment; Rare Event Analysis studies whether tail mass is sampled; quantized diffusion studies path error. Safety-Guided Flow and DMPO put these questions inside the sampler itself.

EXEMPLAR 21

IRNO And RealPDEBench

Physical Surrogates As Stable Operators

Problem

Physical surrogate models can predict one step or one regime well while accumulating high-frequency error, violating conservation structure, or failing under different grids and boundary conditions.

State Space

The state is a physical field, operator, grid, residual, boundary condition, and rollout trajectory.

Object

The mathematical object is a learned operator or fixed-point map over fields, with residual correction dynamics.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is physically meaningful evolution across time and resolution. The surrogate should preserve the field relationships that determine future states.

Allowed Movement

The model may iteratively correct residuals, but the correction must be stable and should reduce errors that matter to the physical dynamics.

Why It Works

Iterative neural operators work when residual correction turns a one-shot approximation into a contractive or stabilizing process. Each step reduces the part of the error the operator can represent instead of injecting new unstable modes.

seek fixed point u = F_theta(u, conditions), with residual update reducing ||R(u)|| over rollout

Evidence Check

Measure long-rollout error, residual spectra, high-frequency stability, out-of-grid generalization, boundary-condition shifts, and conservation or physics-specific metrics.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when one-step loss hides unstable dynamics, when residuals lie outside the learned correction basis, or when the benchmark does not stress the physical regime of interest.

Connection

RealPDEBench defines structure-sensitive evaluation; Stable Video Infinity also studies long trajectory coherence; diffusion transport papers control path error. The shared object is a trajectory-generating map whose local errors accumulate.

EXEMPLAR 22

Local Covariate Selection And TRECA

Local Effects As Assumption-Bounded Identification

Problem

Causal estimates can look precise while conditioning on the wrong variables. A global adjustment set may be too broad, too noisy, or invalid for the local intervention actually being claimed.

State Space

The state is a causal system with treatment, outcome, candidate covariates, local context, intervention policy, and possible hidden confounding.

Object

The mathematical object is an adjustment set for a local causal target, not a universal causal graph.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is effect meaning. The estimate should remain tied to the intervention target after selecting variables and narrowing context.

Allowed Movement

The method may include, exclude, or weight covariates, but only in ways that block confounding paths without conditioning on variables that destroy the target effect or add avoidable variance.

Why It Works

Local covariate selection works when the chosen variables make treated and control outcomes comparable for the target context. The problem is not to explain all variation; it is to isolate the variation needed for the causal contrast.

identify tau(x0) if Y(a) independent of A given selected Z near x0, with overlap P(A=a | Z)>0

Evidence Check

Report balance checks, overlap, sensitivity to hidden confounding, local versus global estimates, placebo outcomes, and whether fairness or policy conclusions change under nearby adjustment sets.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when selected covariates do not block confounding, when overlap disappears locally, or when the causal target shifts after conditioning.

Connection

Distributional Equivalence returns graph-level ambiguity; Fair Causal Bandits use causal estimates inside policy choice; robust missing-covariate papers keep uncertainty about unobserved variables. All ask what the evidence identifies under assumptions.

EXEMPLAR 23

Neural Effect Search And HypoSpace

Discovery As Validated Hypothesis Search

Problem

Automated discovery can generate many plausible hypotheses, but a search process can overfit the evidence it uses to propose them.

State Space

The state is a hypothesis space, evidence graph, candidate effect, search policy, validation set, and rejection rule.

Object

The mathematical object is a rejectable hypothesis with a validation path. A discovery is not just a generated sentence; it is a claim that can fail under evidence.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is testability under search. The system may explore many candidates, but the accepted claim must survive checks not already consumed by search.

Allowed Movement

The search may propose, refine, combine, or rank hypotheses, but acceptance must account for multiplicity and reuse of evidence.

Why It Works

Hypothesis search works when generation and validation are separated enough that the final claim has evidence beyond the search heuristic. Mathematically, search expands the candidate set; validation controls false discovery inside that expanded set.

accept h only if validation evidence rejects null after accounting for h chosen from searched set H

Evidence Check

Report held-out validation, false discovery controls, reproduction artifacts, negative examples, ablations of search breadth, and whether hypotheses survive independent data or execution.

Failure Boundary

The method fails when the same evidence proposes and validates the claim, when hypothesis language is too vague to reject, or when multiplicity is ignored.

Connection

Bitween and RefineStat make scientific artifacts checkable; PaperBench decomposes paper claims; Neural Effect Search connects causal effect estimation to hypothesis generation. The shared object is a claim whose evidence graph can defeat it.

EXEMPLAR 24

Mind-Omni And PRISM

Brain Signals As Structured Latent Interfaces

Problem

Brain and physiological signals are not ordinary text or image tokens. If they are forced too quickly into a generic representation, the model can learn scanner artifacts, task shortcuts, or reconstruction priors instead of neural structure.

State Space

The state is a physiological measurement, brain region or temporal signal, task context, latent token interface, and generated or decoded output.

Object

The mathematical object is a structured latent interface between biological signal space and foundation-model representation space.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is task-relevant neural information. Encoding should preserve signal that changes with brain or body state, not merely reconstruct familiar outputs.

Allowed Movement

The method may discretize, mask, align, or translate physiological signals, but it must keep the latent representation tied to biological variation under controls.

Why It Works

A latent interface works when it creates a sufficient bottleneck for the biological signal before joining language or image models. The bottleneck prevents the foundation model from treating the signal as decoration and forces a stable mapping from measurement structure to task variable.

learn z = E(signal, context) so output Y depends on z and z changes under controlled signal interventions

Evidence Check

Use counterfactual latent swaps, subject or session transfer, scanner artifact controls, missing-signal ablations, reconstruction-prior controls, and task labels that separate neural state from output familiarity.

Object

Preserved Quantity

Allowed Movement

The policy may abstract into subgoals or language-action tokens, but each abstract move must correspond to feasible physical transitions under the environment dynamics.

plan valid if each edge (s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}) satisfies dynamics and constraint set C_physical

Biological Generation As Hierarchy-Constrained Search

Problem

A biological sequence model can assign high likelihood to strings that are not useful proteins. Function depends on folded geometry, local chemistry, binding context, evolutionary constraints, and experimental regime, not only on the next-token statistics of amino acid sequences.

State Space

The state is a coupled object: sequence, residue identity, fold, local contacts, pocket or partner context, target property, and validation assay.

Object

The mathematical object is a constrained generative distribution over biological structures, where sequence is one coordinate of a larger feasible object.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is functional plausibility. Generation may explore new sequences, but it should preserve constraints that make the object fold, bind, remain stable, or satisfy the target biological role.

Allowed Movement

The model may mutate, complete, decode, or condition on structures, but moves should remain inside a region where biological constraints are not destroyed by token-level freedom.

Why It Works

The approach works when it factors the impossible raw search over all sequences into a hierarchy of lower-dimensional constraints. Sequence choice is guided by the structure and task variables that determine whether the candidate can survive physical and biological checks.

sample x from p(sequence | fold, context, target) while violation C_bio(x, fold, context) stays small

EXEMPLAR 27

Adam Degeneracy And Beyond Muon

Optimization As Geometry, Not Step Size Folklore

Problem

Two optimizers can have similar loss curves while moving parameters through very different geometries. A method can look stable in average loss while pushing too hard in sensitive directions or too weakly in directions where the target actually lives.

State Space

The state is parameter vector, gradient, optimizer memory, curvature, preconditioner, update direction, and task-relevant representation change.

Object

The mathematical object is the optimizer-induced metric: the rule that decides which parameter directions are cheap, expensive, amplified, or suppressed.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is useful descent in loss-relevant coordinates. Training should reduce the objective while avoiding hidden damage in stiff or semantically important directions.

Allowed Movement

The optimizer may rescale, rotate, normalize, or project updates, but those transformations must preserve descent where curvature and representation sensitivity say movement matters.

Why It Works

Preconditioning works when it turns an ill-shaped loss surface into a better-conditioned movement problem. The real claim is not that one update is larger or smaller; it is that the update is measured in coordinates that better match the local loss geometry.

choose update delta = -M_t g_t so g_t^T delta < 0 and delta^T H_t delta is controlled

Evidence Check

Measure curvature energy, update alignment, loss decrease per unit movement, sensitivity to ill-conditioned directions, optimizer-state ablations, and whether gains persist after matching compute, batch size, and learning-rate schedules.

Failure Boundary

The argument fails when the preconditioner estimates noise rather than geometry, when benchmark gains come from tuning asymmetry, or when low training loss hides representation drift that hurts later adaptation.

Connection

DiReCT chooses data by curvature-aware movement; LoRA theory asks whether task updates live in a low-rank subspace; trust-region RL constrains policy movement. All translate learning into controlled motion under a metric.

EXEMPLAR 28

Transformer Circuits And Single-Head Attention

Architecture As Explicit Computation

Problem

Architecture claims often say a model can represent a behavior, but representation is weaker than showing the computation that produces the behavior and the evidence that training actually found it.

State Space

The state is token representation, attention scores, value aggregation, feed-forward transformations, layer composition, and the data distribution that trains those pieces.

Object

The mathematical object is an algorithmic circuit inside the network: assignment, aggregation, update, comparison, memory retrieval, or spectral filtering implemented by standard layers.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is the computational relation, not the exact parameterization. Different weights may realize the same algorithm if they preserve the relevant map from inputs to intermediate states to output.

Allowed Movement

The analysis may abstract away parameter details, but it must keep enough structure to show how attention and feed-forward operations implement the claimed algorithm under finite depth, width, and data.

Why It Works

A constructive circuit works when each layer operation corresponds to a needed algorithmic step. For clustering, attention can assign points to centers, aggregation can estimate cluster summaries, and iteration can refine the assignment. For single-head attention, recovery works only when signal directions are visible above noise in the attention geometry.

mechanism claim: network map f = phi_k o ... o phi_1 approximates algorithm step A, with error bounded across iterations

Evidence Check

Show constructive parameter settings or learned mechanism probes, ablate the proposed circuit components, test out-of-distribution inputs that preserve the algorithmic rule, and separate realizability from learnability.

Failure Boundary

The claim fails when the architecture can implement the algorithm in principle but training uses a shortcut, or when the constructed mechanism works only at scale, precision, or data regimes unlike the real model.

Connection

EXEMPLAR 29

Finite Test Certification And Micro-Benchmark Reliability

Benchmarks As Finite Evidence

Problem

Benchmark conclusions are often stated as if the measured score were the true capability. In reality, every benchmark is a finite sample from some task distribution, with item noise, grader noise, model randomness, and distribution mismatch.

State Space

The state is a claim about capability, a task distribution, finite sampled items, model responses, grader outputs, score estimator, and uncertainty interval.

Object

The mathematical object is a statistical certificate: a finite-data statement about how much evidence supports a capability or ranking claim.

Preserved Quantity

The preserved quantity is the meaning of the claim under sampling. A leaderboard difference should preserve a real performance difference, not just a lucky draw of items or grader choices.

Allowed Movement

The evaluator may subsample, adapt, aggregate, or decompose tasks, but it must report uncertainty and the population to which the certificate applies.

Why It Works

Finite certification works by replacing a point score with a bound on estimation error. The mathematics does not make a small benchmark magically broad; it states exactly how much uncertainty remains after the observed evidence.

certify claim if lower_bound(score_A - score_B | n, noise, task distribution) > 0 for the stated population

Evidence Check

Report confidence intervals, bootstrap rank stability, item-level variance, grader agreement, prompt sensitivity, model stochasticity, and whether the sampled tasks cover the claimed deployment region.

Failure Boundary

The certificate fails when the task distribution is narrower than the public claim, when item leakage or grading bias breaks independence, or when reported uncertainty ignores model and evaluator randomness.

Connection

ATLAS chooses informative items adaptively; FRABench separates score components; WebDevJudge and VERINA use artifact-native checks. All improve evaluation by making the measurement channel explicit and finite.
