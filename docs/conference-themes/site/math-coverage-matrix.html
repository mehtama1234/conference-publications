SUPPORT LAYERS

How The Non-Lane Pages Fit

Paper To Object Index

Role. Paper-first lookup. It helps when the reader starts with a name and needs the shortest path to the object, reason, and deep page.

Next use. Expand toward the highest-traffic named papers that are still only present in dense atlas paragraphs.

Theme Subtheme Map

Role. Theme-first navigation layer. It maps seven lanes and 29 subthemes to problems, objects, approach families, representative papers, and cross-theme connections.

Next use. Use it to decide which subthemes deserve their own full-depth pages next.

Object Deep Dives

Role. Object-first explanation layer. It gives deeper treatment to ten recurring mathematical objects and shows why each object works across papers.

Next use. Use it as the bridge between paper exemplars and cross-paper synthesis when adding new subtheme pages.

Proof Sketches

Role. Argument-first layer. It explains twelve reusable mathematical moves that justify why methods work across different paper families.

Next use. Use it as an audit checklist: every major paper entry should name which proof sketch its evidence supports.

Equation Glossary

Role. Notation-first layer. It decodes fourteen recurring formula forms so compact equations in paper cards remain readable and tied to first principles.

Next use. Use it when adding formulas to lane pages so notation stays consistent across the atlas.

Paper Proof Audit

Role. Paper-family claim-check layer. It maps 24 representative groups to the proof sketch, assumption, evidence, and failure boundary behind their mathematical reason.

Next use. Use it to decide which named paper cards need stronger evidence checks next.

Failure Mode Atlas

Role. Cross-theme limits layer. It maps 18 recurring failure modes to broken assumptions, mathematical symptoms, representative papers, evidence checks, and repair directions.

Next use. Use it to prioritize which paper families need stronger assumptions or failure-boundary treatment next.

Approach Pattern Atlas

Role. Method-first layer. It maps 20 recurring approach families to the mathematical object they change, why the move should work, representative papers, and needed evidence.

Next use. Use it before expanding paper cards so each entry names the method move, invariant, working condition, and failure boundary.

Paper Method Matrix

Role. Paper-to-method bridge. It maps 36 representative papers and paper families to objects, approach patterns, why-it-works arguments, assumptions, failures, and neighbors.

Next use. Use it as the paper-level checklist before adding deeper prose to any lane page.

Big Picture Problems

Role. Problem-first synthesis layer. It organizes the atlas around 12 underlying mathematical problems with objects, approaches, papers, why-it-works accounts, and failure checks.

Next use. Use it to decide which broader problem a paper belongs to before writing or expanding its card.

Subtheme Proof Obligations

Role. Subtheme-level audit layer. It maps 29 subthemes to their object, approach move, proof obligation, evidence check, and next deepening target.

Next use. Use it to turn each subtheme into testable paper-level obligations instead of topic labels.

Evidence Check Atlas

Role. Evidence-first audit layer. It maps 24 checks to the mathematical objects they observe, representative papers, weak substitutes, and exposed failures.

Next use. Use it to decide what observation, ablation, interval, stress test, or verifier trace a paper card needs.

Paper Deepening Backlog

Role. Planning layer. It prioritizes 40 paper families for full nine-slot treatment with object, approach, evidence, failure, and connection targets.

Next use. Use it to select the next paper-family expansions and avoid adding disconnected summaries.

Cross-Paper Synthesis

Role. Object-first connection layer. It shows how the same mathematical object appears in different themes.

Next use. Add narrower chains for theorem-to-deployment, measurement channels, and verifier gaming.

Verifier Gaming

Role. Proxy-failure layer. It connects reward hacking, process shortcuts, instrument drift, adaptive pressure, certificate mismatch, and aggregation failure across all lanes.

Next use. Use this pattern for conference-difference synthesis: what ICML and ICLR emphasize differently as mathematical objects.

ICML vs ICLR Math

Role. Conference-difference layer. It explains ICML and ICLR as different mathematical habits: control variables versus system forms.

Next use. Add paper-specific formula and evidence boxes inside the densest lane pages.

Assumptions And Failure Boundaries

Role. Limits layer. It states what must be true for an object to help and what remains outside the method.

Next use. Add paper-specific boundary rows for the most important papers in each lane.

Theorem To Deployment

Role. Theory transfer layer. It separates mathematical, statistical, and system-level assumptions for guarantees.

Next use. Extend this pattern to safety, scientific discovery, and multimodal measurement claims.

Formula Gap Audit

Role. Cross-page compact-math repair layer. It collects named representatives that are present across the atlas but needed sharper object, formula, evidence, and connection treatment.

Next use. Use the same card format for any newly added papers before they become dense lane sections.

Paper Math Template

Role. Reusable writing and audit protocol. It states the nine slots needed for a paper entry to be first-principles rather than a surface summary.

Next use. Apply the template to any new paper added to the atlas and use it to audit older cards that still read like abstracts.

Paper Math Exemplars

Role. Full-depth demonstration layer. It applies the nine-slot template to one hundred six representative papers so future expansions have a concrete density target.

Next use. Add more exemplars for high-traffic papers, then fold the strongest patterns back into lane pages.

Main Math Atlas

Role. Entry point and concept grammar. It explains the recurring objects before sending readers to lane, index, synthesis, and boundary pages.

Next use. Continue balance passes so the page stays navigational instead of duplicating every child page.
