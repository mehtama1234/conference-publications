FIRST PRINCIPLE

Capability Has a Supply Chain

A model capability is not born only inside the architecture. It is supplied by data order, data legality, data diversity, tokenization, missingness assumptions, optimizer geometry, hardware memory, serving latency, cache policy, kernel design, and the adaptation path from pretraining to deployment. When any part of that supply chain is wrong, a method can look strong in a paper and fail in a real system.

The data and systems papers are therefore about controllability below the model interface. They ask which examples should arrive when the learning rate is still high, how to price or value reusable data, how to run long context on finite hardware, how to compress without destroying reasoning, how to adapt at inference time, and how to make deployment constraints shape the method instead of surprising it later.

FIELD SHAPE

The Capability Substrate

This lane is not a list of engineering tricks. It is the field learning that "more data" and "more compute" are too blunt. The useful question is how evidence, parameters, memory, gradients, and hardware interact over time.

Data
What examples exist, who is allowed to use them, when they arrive, how they are filtered, and whether labels encode the actual task or only surface imitation.
Training
How learning-rate schedules, curvature, checkpoint averaging, midtraining bridges, distillation gradients, and data valuation change what the model keeps.
Systems
How attention, KV caches, kernels, tensor abstractions, precision, memory placement, and serving runtimes decide which capabilities are affordable.
Adaptation
How a deployed system changes without full retraining: fast weights, model merging, task vectors, preference transfer, continual modules, and test-time updates.

SUBTHEME 01

Data Order and Curriculum

The same example can have different value at different points in training.

These papers reject the idea that a dataset is just a bag of examples. Order, mixture, repetition, and learning-rate state determine whether an example is learnable, wasted, or harmful.

ICLR 2026

LR Decay Wastes Your Best Data

Shows that ascending-quality curricula can fail when high-quality examples arrive after the learning rate has decayed. The lesson is simple and painful: good data cannot help if the optimizer has already stopped moving enough to absorb it.

ICLR 2026

Learning to See Before Seeing

Studies multimodal pretraining mixtures through controlled experiments at trillion-token scale. It treats the data mixture as the source of visual-language capability rather than an incidental recipe choice.

ICLR 2026

Pre-training under Infinite Compute

Asks what to do when compute is abundant but fresh data is scarce. Epoching, stronger weight decay, ensembling, and distillation become data-governance moves because reuse changes what the model can still learn.

ICLR 2026

WSM

Finds that the checkpoint aggregation window drives decay-free pretraining gains. The training trajectory itself becomes data infrastructure: the model should not only see examples, but preserve useful states produced along the path.

ICML 2026

Procedural Pretraining

Front-loads a small amount of algorithmic data before ordinary language, code, and math. The argument is that a compact procedural warm start can teach operators that later semantic data can reuse.

ICML 2026

Power Law Compositional Reasoning

Shows that natural power-law sampling can help compositional reasoning because frequent combinations are learned first. The paper complicates naive balancing: the long tail matters, but the head may teach reusable structure.

ICML 2026

MDA

Shows that targeted removal or augmentation of structural examples can modulate induction-head formation and in-context learning. It makes curation mechanistic: examples are chosen because they shape a specific internal capability.

ICML 2026

Alignment Pretraining

Studies AI-behavior discourse inside pretraining data and finds large downstream shifts after upsampling aligned-behavior documents. Alignment becomes partly a corpus-composition question, not only a post-training patch.

ICML 2026

PRISM

Uses compact high-quality mid-training to improve reasoning while preserving general abilities. The contribution is retention-aware specialization: move the model toward harder reasoning without wiping out broad competence.

ICML 2026

OpenThoughts

Uses public reasoning datasets and controlled recipe experiments to train competitive reasoning models. Its importance is that open data becomes an experimental substrate for discovering which reasoning recipes actually transfer.

ICML 2026

BLL-Loss

Argues exact token imitation is the wrong objective when correctness is logical or outcome-based. The label should represent successful behavior, not necessarily the literal sequence one teacher happened to write.

ICML 2026

LALP

Scores reasoning data by local premise support rather than whole-response probability. A training trace becomes a chain of teachable moves, where local validity can be useful even when the full answer is imperfect.

ICML 2026

Identity Bridge

Adds identity examples so autoregressive models can infer reverse relational knowledge. The paper is a reminder that inverse and symmetric facts do not automatically appear from one directional exposure.

ICML 2026

NS/IF Attribution

Analyzes example-removal effects under local curvature around the first Newton step. It asks when influence-style attribution can be trusted, which matters when governance wants to know which data caused a behavior.

SUBTHEME 02

Data Value and Markets

Data value is contextual, strategic, and optimizer-dependent.

A data point's value depends on what has already been learned, what other data is available, what target behavior matters, and whether contributors have incentives to reveal truth. These papers turn data selection from filtering into economics and dynamics.

ICML 2026

Sequential Data Values

Reframes data selection as dynamic programming. A data point is not valuable in isolation; its marginal value depends on what has already been selected and what future selections become possible.

ICML 2026

NASH

Decomposes utility into components where Shapley and semivalue scores become more reliable. It is a repair to noisy data valuation: credit assignment improves when the utility function exposes structure.

ICML 2026

Bayesian Truthful Valuation

Combines semivalues with hidden validation data to reward truthfulness and fairness at equilibrium. Data governance becomes mechanism design when contributors can strategically shape what they provide.

ICML 2026

Data Market Pricing

Studies reusable data sold by strategic sellers. Because data is non-rival, uniform dataset prices can produce poor equilibria; the same dataset can serve many buyers with different marginal values.

ICML 2026

OPUS

Scores documents by projecting optimizer-shaped effective updates onto a target direction. The data selector looks through the optimizer, not through metadata, so selected examples are those that actually move the model toward the target.

ICML 2026

MTS Difficulty

Explains weak training-data selection through noisy hypergradients and poor quality features. It is a useful negative result: selection systems fail when their measurement of data quality is too indirect.

ICML 2026

Source Screening

Selects informative source subsets for shared subspace estimation, even when that means discarding data. More sources are not always better if a smaller set captures the common representation with less noise.

ICML 2026

HOBIT

Reorders examples into mini-batches with hard, informative, non-contradictory negatives. In contrastive learning, batch composition is part of the label because negatives define what the model must separate.

ICML 2026

Hedging on the Frontier

Prunes and aggregates candidate models by adapting to benchmark tradeoff geometry. Selection is not limited to data points; model candidates also carry evidence about the frontier a system wants to occupy.

ICML 2026

Diffuse to Detect

Jointly optimizes detector weights, sample weights, and diffusion parameters to reduce annotation needs. It couples generation and labeling so annotation pressure lands on samples that most improve the detector.

ICML 2026

C2R

Prioritizes smallest-margin adversaries during dataset distillation. Boundary cases carry disproportionate information about robustness, so distilled data should represent decision pressure rather than average cases.

ICML 2026

GRAM

Tracks data filtering while reducing cost for multiple deployment access profiles. Filtering becomes an auditable production step, especially when different users or tiers receive different model behavior.

ICLR 2026

ATLAS

Estimates mutual benefit scores across 1,444 language pairs to guide multilingual data and compute allocation. It replaces vague balancing with a transfer matrix showing where one language helps another.

ICML 2026

Linguistic Nepotism

Shows citation behavior can prefer language over evidence relevance, especially for lower-resource languages. Data governance extends into retrieval: the evidence set can become biased before the model even reasons.

SUBTHEME 03

Governance, Provenance, and Missingness

Data constraints are part of the learning problem, not paperwork around it.

Licensing, provenance, missing modalities, and incomplete covariates change what can be trained and what uncertainty should remain visible. These papers make the constraint part of the method.

ICLR 2026

Common Corpus

Assembles permissively licensed multilingual and code data with provenance and filtering details. Its importance is that legality and reproducibility become part of the corpus design, not a note after scraping.

ICML 2026

DiReCT

Recasts late-pretraining data choice as constrained sample selection using per-sample gradients and Hessian spectral geometry. Domain targeting becomes a constrained optimization problem rather than a hand-picked mix.

ICML 2026

Midtraining Bridges Pretraining and Posttraining

Shows that midtraining helps when specialized data bridges a large distributional gap. The middle stage is not filler; it is a controlled transition between broad representations and downstream behavior.

ICLR 2026

Difficult Examples Hurt Unsupervised Contrastive Learning

Uses similarity graphs to show why difficult examples can damage contrastive learning and how removal or margin changes can help. Hard examples are not automatically useful if they distort the representation geometry.

ICML 2026

LIMSSR

Treats the training set itself as incomplete and uses prompt-guided imputation plus mask-aware uncertainty calibration. Missing modalities are not ignored; they become part of the model's uncertainty contract.

ICML 2026

Robust Contextual Optimization with Missing Covariates

Models the missingness mechanism inside downstream optimization and preserves observation uncertainty through decisions. It refuses to pretend incomplete covariates are clean features.

ICLR 2026

Synthetic Data Selection

Shows covariance shift can drive high-dimensional regression failure even when mean shift does not. Synthetic data must match structure beyond averages, or it teaches the model the wrong geometry.

ICLR 2026

CauKer

Combines Gaussian-process kernel composition and structural causal models to synthesize time-series pretraining data. The synthetic generator has an explicit causal and temporal grammar, not only surface realism.

ICLR 2026

TabStruct

Measures whether tabular synthetic data preserves structure when real causal graphs are unavailable. It targets the practical case where structure matters but ground-truth causal diagrams do not exist.

ICLR 2026

Neon

Uses sampler-induced synthetic-data bias as negative evidence without requiring new real data. Instead of treating synthetic artifacts only as defects, it turns them into diagnostic signal.

ICLR 2026

TiTok

Selects informative synthetic examples for adapter transfer without training an extra discriminator. Synthetic examples are cheap enough to flood training, so selection decides whether they help or distract.

ICML 2026

FAC Synthesis

Measures feature activation coverage in sparse-autoencoder space and generates examples for missing features. Synthetic data becomes targeted coverage repair, not just more samples.

ICML 2026

SurvDiff

Generates synthetic clinical survival data while preserving event-time distributions and censoring mechanisms. Clinical synthesis fails if it keeps visible covariates but destroys the time-to-event process.

ICLR 2026

PetaGAIL++

Uses imitation learning and differential privacy to generate mobility trajectories without exposing raw routines. The useful synthetic data must preserve movement patterns while hiding individual lives.

ICML 2026

SceneSmith

Combines text-to-3D assets, articulated object retrieval, physical-property estimation, and simulation checks. Synthetic robotics scenes are only useful if they can exist physically, so generation is tied to validity constraints.

ICML 2026

SC-MILP

Generates equivalent and perturbed mixed-integer programming instances to address scarce high-leverage branching nodes. Optimization data is hard because valuable states are rare; synthesis creates more of the states that teach decisions.

ICLR 2026

mCLM

Front-loads automated synthesis constraints in molecule generation while improving property scores and synthetic accessibility. A molecule that cannot be made is not useful data, so chemistry validity enters before scoring.

ICML 2026

COMPACT

Synthesizes complex visual questions by composing atomic capabilities. Instruction data becomes a designed skill combination space rather than a pile of isolated recognition examples.

ICLR 2026

Semantic Watermark Fingerprints

Verifies ownership after finetuning, quantization, and filtering while remaining stealthy. The governance problem is persistence: provenance marks must survive the transformations real models undergo.

ICLR 2026

LLM DNA

Defines low-dimensional behavioral representations for tracing model evolution through adaptation and distillation. Provenance becomes behavioral lineage when weights are changed, hidden, or redistributed.

ICLR 2026

Ellipse Signatures

Verifies models from logprob outputs without input access or full weights. It is output-level authentication for settings where a verifier cannot inspect the model internals.

ICML 2026

GoodDiffusion

Conditions high-quality diffusion bridge generation on valid per-input signatures. Authorization becomes sample-specific: the data right travels with the input rather than being a vague global model permission.

ICML 2026

Token Overcharging

Shows fixed per-token pricing can permit strategic overcharging while character-count-linear pricing removes the incentive. Pricing rules are governance mechanisms because they shape model-service behavior.

ICLR 2026

Hubble

Shows memorization depends on sensitive-data frequency relative to corpus size and can decline without continued exposure. Memorization is not a binary contamination flag; it is a dynamic frequency-and-training process.

ICML 2026

LM Memorization Capacity

Estimates how many bits per parameter GPT-style models can store when generalization is removed. It separates what a model can memorize in principle from what ordinary training will make it remember.

ICML 2026

AI Engram

Enables subset memory composition or erasure through linear arithmetic without iterative optimization. If memories can be localized and manipulated, deletion and provenance claims become more operational.

ICLR 2026

Empirical Privacy Protection Benchmark

Shows privacy vulnerability rises when adaptation data is close to the pretraining distribution, even without direct overlap. Privacy risk is broader than exact contamination; distributional proximity can matter.

ICML 2026

Rapid Poison

Induces false negatives by training on unsafe samples where a trigger concept is systematically absent. The poisoned signal is an omission, making governance harder than detecting obviously malicious labels.

ICLR 2026

WIMHF

Finds harmful preference features and shows targeted relabeling can improve safety without hurting general performance. Preference data becomes an object for feature-level inspection and repair.

ICLR 2026

SSPO

Uses a small amount of labeled feedback plus pseudo-labels over unpaired responses to outperform stronger labeled-only baselines. It asks how much human preference data is actually needed when unlabeled comparisons can be structured.

ICLR 2026

ScaleCUA

Combines automated agents and human experts to create scalable computer-use training data. Computer-use examples are expensive because they need screen state, action, and intent; this pipeline expands coverage while preserving human oversight.

ICML 2026

RoTS

Generates 800k recovery trajectories through tree-based discovery of GUI error modes. Agents need data for what happens after mistakes, because recovery behavior is a separate capability from clean-task demonstration.

ICML 2026

MEnvAgent

Builds verifiable Docker environments and solution trajectories for software-engineering tasks while reducing time cost. The executable environment is part of the dataset; without it, code-agent data cannot be checked reproducibly.

ICML 2026

TerminalTraj

Generates Docker-aligned tasks and validation code that improve terminal-agent training and test-time scaling. It preserves the command sequence and executable validation rather than only the final answer.

ICML 2026

daVinci-Dev

Preserves tool invocations, test executions, and information flow as mid-training data. Tool-use supervision lives in process: the final patch hides which command revealed the bug and which test guided repair.

ICML 2026

OXE-AugE

Rebalances robot-arm and gripper diversity to improve unseen-robot manipulation after fine-tuning. Embodied curation has to cover the physical variety of bodies, not only task labels.

ICML 2026

Continual VLA Forgetting

Shows that small replay buffers can preserve or recover prior vision-language-action skills while supporting new learning. What gets replayed decides whether embodied adaptation becomes learning or forgetting.

ICML 2026

VideoKR

Builds a skill-oriented video reasoning corpus from licensed expert videos with human-in-the-loop generation. The dataset is organized around capabilities, so video supervision links evidence, explanation, and procedure.

ICML 2026

Motive

Uses motion-weighted gradients to find fine-tuning clips that improve or degrade temporal dynamics. Video data should be judged by motion signal, not only frame content.

ICML 2026

Holi-Spatial

Builds millions of spatial annotations over 3D Gaussian-splatting scenes for VLM fine-tuning. Spatial capability needs layered supervision over objects, relations, depth, and scene structure.

ICLR 2026

TRACE

Improves segmentation without instance labels by using foundation-model internals as an annotation source. It turns latent model knowledge into training signal while inheriting the teacher's internal biases.

ICML 2026

Scientific Annotation BC

Shows multi-task pretraining and shared mistake representations can transfer across scientific annotation workflows. Expert curation is procedural, so workflow traces and common errors become useful data.

ICML 2026

PSAHS

Refines target graph structure using agreement between a structure-aware GNN and an attribute-only MLP under label scarcity. Agreement between two views becomes a conservative signal for repairing noisy graph data.

ICLR 2026

RULE

Treats noisy expert annotations and attribute/entity misalignments in multimodal knowledge graphs as first-class risks. The paper focuses on broken correspondences, not only wrong labels.

ICML 2026

GFD-EMVC

Detects label-noise outliers in gradient space and recalibrates them using feature prototypes. Label repair becomes a geometry problem: examples whose gradients disagree with their feature neighborhood deserve inspection.

ICLR 2026

UniImb

Uses prototype load balancing so majority samples do not dominate imbalanced graph representation learning. Dataset class structure can erase minority behavior unless the representation is balanced deliberately.

ICML 2026

MFedPBA

Aggregates decision prototypes under model heterogeneity and modality imbalance in federated learning. Governance means sharing enough decision structure to learn together without forcing every client into the same model.

ICML 2026

MV-FGAD

Shows subtle graph anomalies become harder to detect when client subgraphs are partitioned. Federated graph learning changes the anomaly signal because each client sees only part of the structure.

ICML 2026

GR-LoRA

Balances class-incremental stability and plasticity through gradient decomposition, entropy-based module selection, and OOD mismatch suppression. Continual data needs update rules that protect old classes while admitting new ones.

ICML 2026

SDFT

Learns multiple skills over time while preserving prior capabilities better than ordinary supervised fine-tuning. Demonstration-conditioned behavior acts as stabilizing supervision when new skills arrive.

ICML 2026

DiSC

Updates parametric knowledge from adaptation corpora while preserving instruction following, reasoning, and factual skills. Splitting the update corpus from protective context lets data change knowledge without eroding general behavior.

ICLR 2026

Revela

Trains specialized-domain retrieval without query-document labels. It matters because labels are often absent exactly where retrieval would help most, so the system learns relevance from language-modeling structure.

ICML 2026

OU Identifiability

Shows that one intervention per strongly connected component can identify dynamics up to scale. For causal data, the important question is which intervention exposes structure, not only how many observations are collected.

ICML 2026

Unpaired Causal IV

Proves consistency as environments grow even when observations per environment remain constant. Sparse environments can identify causal structure when variation is organized correctly.

ICML 2026

FlashOptim

Reduces checkpoint and optimizer memory enough to make large-model fine-tuning more accessible without API changes. Who can adapt models depends on memory cost, so systems work changes governance access.

ICML 2026

Self-Soupervision

Uses unlabeled data and diverse self-supervised recipes to create robust parameter-averaged models. The governed object is not only the dataset, but the set of recipes used to extract signal from it.

SUBTHEME 04

Serving and Memory Systems

A capability that cannot be served is not yet a product capability.

Long context, agent workflows, and high-throughput models depend on memory movement, KV-cache policy, scheduling, kernels, and runtime abstractions. These papers make serving constraints visible.

ICML 2026

DHSA

Runs 100K context on a single 24GB GPU by redesigning attention around memory-efficient tiled prefill. Long context becomes meaningful only when the memory layout makes it runnable.

ICLR 2026

ThinKV

Reuses evicted KV-cache slots to avoid compaction overhead and improve reasoning throughput. Cache management is now reasoning infrastructure because wasted cache motion becomes latency.

ICML 2026

STAR-KV

Combines low-rank decomposition, mixed-precision quantization, and Triton kernels for faster generation. It treats the KV cache as structured data whose internal form can be optimized.

ICML 2026

EntroKV

Keeps high-entropy attention heads while compressing low-entropy heads more aggressively. The compression policy follows information content instead of applying one uniform rule.

ICLR 2026

TileLang

Exposes memory placement, data movement, and scheduling primitives for fused neural kernels. It gives ML developers a language for performance-critical structure rather than hiding it behind opaque kernels.

ICML 2026

ThunderAgent

Schedules LLM and tool resources with workflow-level knowledge of KV caches, states, and external assets. Agent serving becomes resource-graph scheduling rather than independent model calls.

ICLR 2026

ADP

Unifies agent datasets across coding, browsing, tool use, and research benchmarks. Its infrastructure role is interoperability: traces are reusable only if actions, observations, and outcomes can be represented consistently.

ICLR 2026

Speculative Actions

Predicts likely agent actions with faster models so multiple API calls can run in parallel without changing accepted behavior. Agent latency is often wall-clock latency from tools, not token generation alone.

ICML 2026

MAP

Finds production agents mostly rely on prompting, human evaluation, and short workflows before intervention. It grounds systems work in current deployment reality rather than assuming fully autonomous long-horizon agents.

ICML 2026

AlgoVeri

Measures whether models can exploit verifier feedback rather than saturating after early attempts. Verification is not only an endpoint; it can be an infrastructure loop for test-time repair.

ICLR 2026

CRAMF

Indexes formal-math definitions with query augmentation and hybrid reranking. Specialized agent competence often requires domain infrastructure outside the model: curated libraries, retrieval, and formal handles.

ICLR 2026

Equal-Resource MoE

Trains many sparse models under fixed budgets to identify stable activation-rate regions. It asks how much sparse capacity should be active when total compute is held constant.

ICML 2026

Beyond LM

Uses sparse experts to provide language capacity while accommodating vision's data-hungry scaling. Multimodal systems have asymmetric needs, so language and vision should not be forced through identical scaling paths.

ICML 2026

Embedding Translation

Uses localized expert adaptation and confidence metrics across many pairwise model-translation settings. Embedding spaces are infrastructure boundaries; translation lets models exchange representations without full retraining.

ICML 2026

DroneDINO

Uses auxiliary task recognition to counter dominant-task overactivation in unified drone detectors. Multi-task systems need routing that notices which task is present rather than letting the easiest or most frequent task hijack capacity.

ICML 2026

FedARC

Aligns federated clients with semantic anchors while preserving local and global embedding fusion. Federated clients are heterogeneous, so shared anchors must coordinate without erasing local structure.

ICLR 2026

Global Merging

Shows late fully connected merging can improve generalization under severe data heterogeneity. Decentralized learning is partly a timing problem: local learners should specialize before global integration.

ICML 2026

mHC

Combines diversified residual connectivity with infrastructure optimization for scalable training. Architecture topology and training system are coupled; residual paths influence both representation and scaling behavior.

ICLR 2026

Differentiable MPC on GPU

Makes model predictive control practical inside RL and imitation-learning loops. A control method that is too slow or CPU-bound cannot become part of modern training, so solver placement changes the algorithm's viability.

ICLR 2026

Triple-BERT

Reuses parameters and attention over driver and order pools for dispatch. Dispatch is a relational systems problem, where served orders and pickup time depend on shared structure across both sides of the market.

ICLR 2026

Probabilistic Angle Kernels

Improves approximate nearest-neighbor search throughput over HNSW in the reported setting. Retrieval infrastructure determines whether embeddings can actually be used at scale.

ICLR 2026

FFDP

Uses non-GEMM fused kernels and distributed sharding for native-resolution brain MRI registration. Gigavoxel medical imaging needs kernels and sharding that match the workload, not transformer assumptions.

ICML 2026

Walrus

Combines harmonic-analysis stabilization, load-balanced distributed training, and compute-adaptive tokenization for scientific pretraining. Scientific domains bring geometry, numerical stability, and load balance into one system.

ICML 2026

Rare-Update Bandits

Removes expensive G-optimal design bottlenecks while preserving optimal regret in linear and generalized linear settings. It is systems-aware theory: optimal algorithms must also be cheap enough to update.

ICLR 2026

einx

Reduces tensor APIs to declarative expressions grounded in consistent vectorization rules. Tensor code often fails because shape transformations are implicit; explicit notation is infrastructure for human correctness.

ICLR 2026

FlashRNN

Trains large LSTM and GRU-style models with perplexity comparable to similarly sized Transformers and Mamba2. It shows that an architecture can become newly competitive when solver and implementation constraints are revisited.

ICML 2026

CMRU

Restores gradient flow in analog-friendly bistable recurrent units through cumulative temporal updates. Low-power recurrent hardware has different constraints from digital accelerators, so the training mechanism must match the device.

SUBTHEME 05

Compression and Efficient Models

Efficiency work asks which parts of the model actually carry function.

Compression is not just smaller weights. It is a set of hypotheses about which layers, experts, ranks, activations, heads, and kernels matter for a task under deployment constraints.

ICML 2026

MoE Compression

Combines structural pruning with 4-bit quantization for mixture-of-experts models. Conditional compute lowers active cost, but expert storage still dominates unless the inactive structure is compressed too.

ICLR 2026

Layer Pruning Reassessment

Revisits LLM pruning through large-scale benchmarks and gradient-flow analysis. It warns that compression recipes can look good on narrow tests while damaging hidden capabilities.

ICML 2026

TabSwift

Adds adaptive layer-wise early exits to tabular foundation-model inference. Serving cost becomes conditional on confidence: easy cases should not pay for the full model path.

ICML 2026

FFCC

Lowers costly activation functions into fast floating-point reinterpretation instruction sequences. It is a reminder that mathematical functions have hardware costs, and those costs shape feasible architectures.

ICML 2026

IO-Aware GNNs

Groups graph neural layers by kernel type to reduce memory traffic and improve locality. Graph learning is often bandwidth-bound, so algorithmic speed depends on data movement as much as arithmetic.

ICLR 2026

SparseRL

Treats sparse CUDA generation as a policy conditioned on nonzero matrix structure. Sparse kernels are layout-dependent, so code generation must optimize against executable runtime behavior.

ICML 2026

CONTINUUM

Adds PyTorch-level Elastic Tensors with zero-copy topological aliasing and dynamic memory behavior. Static tensor abstractions strain under dynamic models; this makes flexible memory structure a framework feature rather than a pile of copies.

ICLR 2026

Polar Express

Designs adaptive matrix-sign iterations using only matrix multiplications for high-throughput accelerators. The systems move is to reformulate an optimizer subroutine around the operation GPUs execute best.

ICML 2026

NorMuon

Implements scalable Muon-style orthogonalization under FSDP2 across devices. Orthogonalized optimization becomes credible only when sharding, synchronization, and distributed matrix operations work at training scale.

SUBTHEME 06

Adaptation and Test-Time Control

Deployment needs behavior change without rebuilding the whole model.

These papers treat adaptation as a controlled movement through model behavior: fast weights, task vectors, hyperparameter paths, graph support examples, and personalized preference transfer.

ICLR 2026

OpTI-BFM

Connects behavior foundation model task inference to upper-confidence linear bandits. It treats task identification as exploration under uncertainty, not simply prompt classification.

ICLR 2026

MF-GIA

Performs inference-time graph prediction from support examples without raw modality access or parameter updates. It matches deployment reality where a system may see examples but not retrain or inspect original modalities.

ICLR 2026

HTI

Learns conditional output-distribution paths across hyperparameter settings. Hyperparameter movement becomes a learned map through behavior space instead of a fresh training run for every setting.

ICLR 2026

In-Place TTT

Adapts final-projection fast weights at inference time using a next-token-prediction-aligned objective. The current context becomes training signal while the model is being used.

ICLR 2026

RAIN-Merging

Projects instruction-tuned task vectors away from thinking-token feature subspaces. It shows that adaptation can accidentally damage reasoning format unless the merge respects internal feature geometry.

ICLR 2026

P-GenRM

Transfers preferences from similar users and scales individual scoring schemes at test time. Personalization becomes a fast adaptation problem instead of training a new reward model per user.

SUBTHEME 07

Continual Learning

New capability should enter without erasing old structure.

Continual learning work is about the stability-plasticity bargain: a system has to absorb new concepts while preserving the representation and behavior that made it useful.

ICLR 2026

CompSLOT

Adds a concept module for continual vision learners using object-centric slots. New knowledge is localized into semantically meaningful parts instead of diffusing through the whole model.

ICLR 2026

FIRE

Formalizes stability and plasticity as measurable objectives through Frobenius-Isometry Reinitialization. It makes the tradeoff explicit: stay close enough to preserve old behavior, but move enough to learn.

ICML 2026

Skill Neologisms

Introduces trainable soft tokens while freezing the base LLM weights. A new token becomes a compact procedural handle, letting a model acquire a skill without rewriting its entire parameter space.

ICLR 2026

Huxley-Goedel Machine

Uses a clade-inspired metric to guide coding-agent development toward stronger descendants. Continual improvement becomes lineage control: pick changes that improve future search, not only current score.

ICLR 2026

GEPA

Uses trajectory reflection and Genetic-Pareto search to improve prompts with fewer rollouts than GRPO. Failures become textual search guidance, turning agent refinement into a data-efficient adaptation loop.

SUBTHEME 08

Optimization Dynamics

Training recipes work through geometry, not magic.

These papers examine the movement of parameters, gradients, ranks, teacher signals, routes, and trust regions. The common point is that optimization details decide which capability survives.

ICML 2026

Neural Thickets

Reframes pretrained weights as surrounded by many nearby task-specialist vectors. Pretraining creates a basin of reachable specialists, so adaptation is local movement through a dense neighborhood.

ICLR 2026

DMS

Replaces histogram discounting with a smooth model that distinguishes similar high-dimensional measures. Training systems need comparison tools that remain sensitive when distributions are close but behaviorally different.

ICML 2026

FlexRank

Selectively activates nested low-rank components to trade cost for capability. Rank becomes an adaptive resource: the model can spend more representational capacity when the task needs it.

ICML 2026

LoRA Gradient Descent

Reformulates stacked adapter outer products and controls step size for convergence guarantees. It gives a theoretical spine to a widely used adaptation recipe.

ICLR 2026

DTO-KD

Dynamically balances task and distillation gradients to avoid conflict and dominance. Distillation is not automatically aligned with the downstream task; the teacher can pull against the objective.

Trust-Region LLM RL

AutoEP

L2Seg

Predicts stable and unstable route segments so iterative solvers can aggregate stable portions into hypernodes. It reuses certainty: solved substructure should become compressed state for the next optimization step.

PCD

Guides samples toward novel high-performing regions beyond static training data. It addresses generator conservatism by using optimization pressure to search outside the observed sample cloud.

What This Lane Says About ICML and ICLR

The big picture is that the conference is turning infrastructure into science. Data is not a passive pile, systems are not afterthoughts, and adaptation is not a cosmetic fine-tune. The behavior of a model is shaped by the full path from corpus construction through optimization and serving.

This lane is the natural third piece of the atlas. Safety and evaluation ask what evidence should count. Agents and reasoning ask what control loop produces that evidence. Data and systems ask what substrate makes the control loop possible at all.
