Representation geometry, mapped

The central problem is understanding the shape of learned information: what features exist, how they combine, and why some transfer while others break.

Problem

Large models work, but their internals are not arbitrary. Features, embeddings, singular spectra, graph coordinates, causal latents, and activation norms often explain when a model generalizes, memorizes, or fails.

Approaches

The papers use spectral analysis, manifold views, feature decomposition, graph geometry, latent causal recovery, exchangeability, and representation-level interventions.

Connecting read

This theme is the attempt to turn model behavior from a black-box curve into a map of internal structure.
