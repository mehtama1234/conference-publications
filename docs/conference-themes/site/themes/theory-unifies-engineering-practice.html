Problem

The shared problem is that simple benchmark accuracy no longer explains what is happening. The papers in this group isolate a narrower failure mode, capability, or design constraint.

Approaches

The approaches vary by paper, but the common pattern is to define a sharper object of study, build a method around that object, and test whether it changes behavior under controlled conditions.

Connecting read

Read this page as a map of the local evidence rows, not as a claim that the theme is as central as the largest conference themes.

SUBTHEMES

What the papers are actually about

Adam hyperparameter phase diagram

ICML 2026Adam Degeneracy
Maps stable convergence, spikes, and SignGD-like oscillation regimes through second-moment decoupling.

Anti-causal bias stability criterion

ICML 2026DISCO
Derives a trainable bias-mitigation objective from a Standard Anti-Causal Model and conditional independence.

Approximate symmetry as learnable constraint

ICML 2026RECM
Recovers full equivariance on symmetric distributions while allowing non-symmetric solutions where data require flexibility.

Asymmetry improves reasoning landscapes

ICML 2026Power Law Compositional Reasoning
Proves power-law sampling reduces data requirements in a minimalist skill-composition task.

Attention as learned KDE

ICML 2026DiScoFormer
Proves self-attention can recover normalized KDE and learns multi-scale kernel-like behavior.

Automata-augmented Transformer state tracking

ICML 2026Rational Transductors
Injects WFA-derived matrix recurrence into attention to capture regular languages and NC1-complete tasks.

Bayesian view of variance reduction

ICML 2026SVRG and Beyond via Posterior Correction
Full text recovers SVRG as posterior correction over isotropic Gaussian posteriors and derives Newton-like and Adam-like extensions.

Chi-square radius of evaluation families

ICML 2026Finite Test Certification
Shows multi-environment evaluation sample complexity is governed by distribution-family chi-square radius.

Controlled origins of neural scaling laws

ICML 2026Scaling Laws Origin
Uses random-graph walks and simplified language generators to isolate scaling-law mechanisms.

Corpus statistics as semantic association mechanisms

ICLR 2026Token Association Dynamics
Shows how natural-language statistics first shape transformer token associations during training.

Correct samplers for symmetry-reduced diffusion

ICLR 2026Quotient-Space Diffusion Model
Full text contrasts quotient-space diffusion with alignment heuristics whose modified learning targets may not match valid samplers.

Dependent-feature SHAP generalization

ICML 2026Categorical ANOVA
Extends SHAP-style attribution beyond independent categorical features and rectangular support.

Dimension-improved DFM convergence

ICML 2026DFM Bounds
Derives KL discretization-error bounds for Brownian diffusion flow matching with improved dimension scaling.

Doubly exponential fictitious play barrier

ICML 2026FTRL Lower Bounds
Shows multi-player potential games can make fictitious play take doubly exponential convergence time.

Dynamical view of positional mechanisms

ICML 2026Robust Filter Attention

Dynamical-systems initialization for GNNs

ICML 2026Beyond ReLU
Derives a bifurcation-aware closed-form initialization from oversmoothing theory.

Explicit nonlinear learning dynamics

ICML 2026Gradient Flow Through Diagram Expansions
Reduces formal loss expansions to PDEs that can yield analytic gradient-flow solutions.

Feature learning as effective dimension reduction

ICML 2026Alignment-Sensitive Minimax Rates
Proves minimax risk scales like `sigma^2 * ESD` and analyzes gradient-flow regimes that reduce ESD.

Focus-dilution attention dynamics

ICML 2026Focus and Dilution
Decomposes attention learning into rank-one condensation, frequency focus, mass redistribution, and symmetry breaking stages.

Forward-derive backward-verify math solving

ICML 2026FPS

Fourier view of implicit graphs

ICML 2026SWING
Uses random-feature linearized kernels, importance sampling, and Gumbel-softmax mechanisms for accelerator-friendly graph computation.

Geometry-aware spectral diagnostics

ICML 2026Non-Euclidean EoS
Shows l_infinity, block, spectral, preconditioned, and normalized gradient methods also hover near stability thresholds.

Hamiltonian view of population flows

ICML 2026Lagrangian Action

Harmonic-analysis priors for neural solvers

Hyperparameter-controlled grokking time

Implicit-bias invariants in attention

JKO links among GANs and divergence objectives

Sound-complete evolutionary selection identification

Spectral bias mitigation by refinement

Spectral recovery scaling laws

ICML 2026Single-Head Attention in High Dimensions
Shows power-law target spectra lead to sequential recovery of spectral modes and power-law scaling of test error.

Spectral variance of generative trajectories

ICML 2026RMT Diffusion

Stability-through-reparameterization

ICML 2026POET-X

Standardized gradients through discrete operators

ICML 2026SoftJAX/SoftTorch
Packages soft Boolean, indexing, sort/rank, optimal-transport, permutahedron, and straight-through operators.

Subgrammar loss decomposition

ICML 2026Grammar Substructure

Symmetry-identified in-context algorithms
