Theme 09 - ICLR 2026: 19 / ICML 2026: 47

Alignment, preference optimization, and feedback, mapped

The central problem is turning human or verifier preference into reliable model behavior.

Problem

Feedback is noisy, sparse, inconsistent, and often changes with model capability. A reward that helps one model can mis-train another, and dense preference scores can conflict with hard correctness.

Approaches

The papers develop RL objectives, preference estimators, reward normalization, hybrid verifier/reward methods, semi-supervised feedback, clinical teaching alignment, and scaling laws for post-training.

Connecting read

This theme is about the contract between what we reward and what the model learns to do.

SUBTHEMES

What the papers are actually about

Adaptive value-difference evaluation

ICLR 2026AdAEM
Automatically generates value-probing questions to distinguish LLM inclinations beyond static HHH-style benchmarks.

Alignment pretraining

ICML 2026Alignment Pretraining
Shows AI-behavior discourse in pretraining data can causally increase or reduce downstream misalignment.

Anti-sycophancy proof robustness

ICML 2026BrokenMath
Finds state-of-the-art LLMs and agents still endorse false theorem statements despite mitigation attempts.

Base-model support barrier in post-training

ICML 2026PG Post-Training
Shows outcome-reward policy gradients can be minimax optimal yet exponentially limited outside base-model support.

Bayesian non-negative reward modeling

ICML 2026BNRM
Uses sparse non-negative latent factors inside Bradley-Terry preference modeling to reduce reward hacking.

Bounded log-likelihood reasoning SFT

ICML 2026BLL-Loss
Caps gradient influence of low-probability demonstration tokens to improve reasoning generalization.

Calibrated value-intensity steering

ICML 2026VALUEFLOW
Builds HiVES, VIDB, and anchor-based ranking evaluation to extract, steer, and score value intensity across models.

Capability-conditioned SFT objectives

ICML 2026Beyond Log Likelihood
Shows strong models can benefit from prior-leaning probability objectives while weak models still favor NLL.

Closed-form safety-constrained DPO

ICLR 2026SafeDPO
Reformulates safety alignment as a direct preference objective without reward models, cost models, or online sampling.

Co-evolving red-team agents

ICML 2026AdvGame
Produces a defender with improved robustness and an attacker usable as a general-purpose red-teaming model.

Comparative value-alignment scoring

ICLR 2026EigenBench
Aggregates model-to-model judgments under a constitution with EigenTrust to score subjective alignment.

Conditional DPO-RLHF equivalence

ICML 2026Conditional Equivalence of DPO and RLHF
Shows DPO can optimize relative advantage rather than absolute human alignment when a hidden RLHF-optimality assumption fails.

Contrastive trajectory balance for attacks

ICML 2026Stable-GFN
Avoids partition-function estimation and reduces mode collapse in reward-driven red-team generation.

DPO choice-theory foundations

ICML 2026DPO Unchained
Recasts DPO and extensions through broad normative human-choice theory beyond a single convex loss.

DPO misspecification under unrealizable rewards

ICLR 2026AuxDPO
Shows DPO can reverse preferences or lower reward when the policy class cannot represent the true reward.

Deception-probe obfuscation regimes

ICML 2026Obfuscation Atlas
Separates honest policies from obfuscated activations and detector-evading obfuscated policies under RLVR.

Dense visual reward for T2I alignment

ICLR 2026ImageDoctor
Uses localized flaws and look-think-predict reasoning as a richer reward model for preference tuning.

Diffusion post-training estimator design

ICLR 2026RLR
Targets downstream diffusion alignment by balancing bias and variance in recursive-chain optimization.

Edge-of-competence RL training

ICML 2026Pre/Mid/RL Reasoning
Finds RL reasoning gains when pre-training leaves headroom and RL data targets tasks just within reach.

Entropy discrepancy scheduling

ICML 2026Entropy Control
Switches between entropy maximization and minimization based on divergence between positive and negative samples.

Expected-maximum reward optimization

ICML 2026FIDIA
Uses a grounded gradient estimator to align RL training with the deployment selection protocol.

Fast human-aligned diffusion guidance

ICML 2026LiDAR Sampling
Matches SDXL GenEval gradient-guidance performance with a reported 9.5x speedup.

Feedback signal directionality

ICML 2026RLVepsR
Models GRPO-style reasoning modes with replicator dynamics to separate convergence slowdown from collapse.

Gradient-free instruction following for LRMs

ICLR 2026RAIN-Merging
Uses instruction-attention module scaling to improve adherence while maintaining reasoning and general capability.

Gradient-level safety buffering

ICML 2026Buffer-and-Reinforce
Merges safety and user adapters via QR decomposition without additional safety data during user fine-tuning.

Hidden-state and milestone value estimation

ICML 2026Hista/Numca
Uses numerical spans and hidden-state rollout averaging to improve process-level RL credit assignment.

Hybrid verifier and reward-model RL

ICLR 2026HERO
Combines sparse correctness checkers with dense reward-model scores for reasoning post-training.

Independent value-module alignment

ICML 2026SVGT
Maintains normative representations in a dedicated value space and steers generation through bridge tokens.

Inference-time common-ground maintenance

ICML 2026PLAINTAIN
Uses visible intermediate plans as an intervention interface while improving pass@1.

Inference-time specialist coordination

ICML 2026Divide-and-Denoise
Prevents one diffusion model from dominating composition by assigning model responsibilities under fairness constraints.

Interpretable preference-data features

ICLR 2026WIMHF
Uses sparse autoencoders to describe human-feedback datasets with natural-language preference features.

Local redundancy plasticity measure

ICML 2026Local Redundancy
Defines plasticity as information-theoretic redundancy of local gradient-direction model families.

Low-variance reward fine-tuning flows

ICML 2026Tilt Matching
Interprets velocity updates through cumulants between interpolant velocity and reward for scalable Stable Diffusion fine-tuning.

Metacognitive knowledge augmentation

ICML 2026Know More Know Clearer
Partitions LLM knowledge into mastered, confused, and missing regions using internal cognitive signals.

Mid-training as RL stabilizer

ICML 2026PRISM
Finds RL applied after PRISM mid-training gives stable monotonic gains while direct RL on base models is weaker.

Multi-objective agent alignment

ICML 2026ParetoPO
Uses hypervolume-guided scalarization and dominance-aware advantages for competing deployment objectives.

Multiplayer Nash preference optimization

ICLR 2026MNPO
Generalizes Nash learning from human feedback from two-player games to n-player policy populations.

Natural-language critique RL

ICML 2026Critique-GRPO
Combines numerical rewards with language critiques so models learn from failed attempts and guided refinements.

Omni-modal free-form reward modeling

ICLR 2026Omni-Reward
Builds reward benchmarks and data across text, image, video, audio, and 3D with free-form preferences.

On-policy self-distillation for continual learning

ICML 2026SDFT
Uses demonstration-conditioned in-context behavior as a self-teacher to reduce forgetting.

One-to-many alignment

ICML 2026ClinTutor-R1
Models individual trainee belief states and group consensus for clinical Socratic education.

Ordinal human feedback limits

ICLR 2026Ranking Feedback Online Learning
Shows ranking feedback can be insufficient for sublinear regret under instantaneous or deterministic time-average models.

Personalized generative reward modeling

ICLR 2026P-GenRM
Converts preference signals into adaptive personas and scoring rubrics for user-specific reward evaluation.

Plasticity windows for specialization

ICML 2026Midtraining Bridges Pretraining and Posttraining
Finds timing and mixture weight interact strongly, with late specialized-data introduction requiring lower weights and limited compensation.

Predictive scaling laws for LLM RL

ICLR 2026ScaleRL
Fits sigmoidal compute-performance curves and separates compute efficiency from asymptotic RL performance.

Process-level reasoning supervision

ICML 2026LALP
Selects teacher traces from diverse pools by transferable local step transitions.

Prompt correction stickiness

ICML 2026LLM Adaptability Limits
Shows most zero-shot annotation errors resist prompt rescue, especially high-confidence mistakes.

RL that teaches new reasoning paths

ICML 2026h1
Full text reports gains even at high pass@k, suggesting curriculum RL can learn reasoning paths not merely elicit base-model samples.

Regret-based preference optimization

ICML 2026RePO
Models human preferences as behavior-conditioned judgments of relative suboptimality rather than direct reward samples.

Reward-decomposed writing control

ICLR 2026LongWriter-Zero
Separates incentives for length control, writing quality, and structural formatting.

Reward-free conflicting-objective LLM alignment

ICML 2026RACO
Resolves preference-gradient conflicts with clipped conflict-averse descent toward weighted Pareto-critical points.

Reward-free text-image alignment

ICML 2026AGSM
Improves SoftREPA failure cases, including more than 35 percent counting-accuracy gain on GenEval.

Reward-hacking-resistant factuality RL

ICML 2026Binary RAR
Argues binary factuality rewards avoid generic-claim reward hacking seen with continuous factuality scores.

Reward-steered design generation

ICML 2026DiBO
Post-trains diffusion LLMs with masked-response SFT followed by rewards from label improvements.

Reward-style alignment under distribution shift

ICML 2026SCIQL
Uses gated advantage weighted regression to balance task reward and explicit behavior style.

Rubric-guided multi-domain reasoning RL

ICML 2026RGR-GRPO
Uses rubrics as dense rewards and offline guidance to improve math, physics, chemistry, and general reasoning.

Semi-supervised preference optimization

ICLR 2026SSPO
Uses reward-threshold pseudo-labeling to learn from small paired feedback and large unpaired response pools.

Social-choice RLHF regularization

ICML 2026Axiomatic Value of Regularization
Shows reference-policy regularization changes which social-choice axioms RLHF-style preference aggregation satisfies.

Soft-brake RL post-training

ICML 2026R2VPO
Improves LLM reasoning and robotic control sample efficiency by down-weighting risky updates instead of truncating them.

Solver-agnostic reward fine-tuning for image generation

ICLR 2026DiffusionNFT
Incorporates reinforcement signals into supervised forward-process objectives and removes CFG dependence.

Solver-verifier self-reward cycle

ICML 2026Agent0-VL
Aligns reasoning and verification distributions through tool-grounded critique without external reward models.

Sparse full-finetuning RL updates

ICML 2026SGD RLVR
Finds SGD updates fewer than 0.02 percent of parameters without explicit sparsity regularization.

Thinking reward models

ICML 2026Complex Reasoning/TRM
Trains reward models over reasoning quality for test-time selection and RL optimization.

Token-importance guided DPO

ICLR 2026TI-DPO
Combines gradient attribution, Gaussian-prior token weights, and triplet loss for fine-grained preference control.

Trust-aware reasoning rewards

ICLR 2026EmotionThinker
Progressively introduces reasoning-quality reward weighted by alignment between rationale and outcome.

Verification as targeted multimodal reward

ICML 2026MoCA
Uses perception verification and structured verbal checks to supervise process components rather than final answers alone.
