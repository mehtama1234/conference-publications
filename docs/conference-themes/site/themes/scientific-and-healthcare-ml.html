Problem

The shared problem is that simple benchmark accuracy no longer explains what is happening. The papers in this group isolate a narrower failure mode, capability, or design constraint.

Approaches

The approaches vary by paper, but the common pattern is to define a sharper object of study, build a method around that object, and test whether it changes behavior under controlled conditions.

SUBTHEMES

What the papers are actually about

Biophysical neural digital twins

ICML 2026Learning Biophysical Models for Neurostimulation
Full text fits Hodgkin-Huxley retinal models from extracellular MEA features to predict unseen multi-electrode stimulation responses.

Brain-signal foundation modeling

ICML 2026Mind-Omni
Tokenizes fMRI into discrete brain tokens and trains one diffusion model across seven brain-vision-language encoding/decoding tasks.

Cauchy diffusion bridges for clinical audio

ICML 2026Cauchy-Driven Diffusion Bridges
Uses heavy-tailed Cauchy bridge drivers to isolate bowel sounds from speech interference.

Centralized medical time-series modeling

ICLR 2026CoTAR
Replaces decentralized attention with core-token aggregation and redistribution for EEG/ECG channel dependencies.

Hierarchical genomic foundation models

ICML 2026dnaHNet
Uses differentiable dynamic chunking to model raw genomic sequences without fragmenting biological motifs through fixed tokenization.

Persistent feature identities for imputation

ICML 2026HELIX
Assigns learnable feature identities to stabilize cross-feature time-series imputation.

Phenotype-conditioned brain graphs

ICML 2026PhenoBrain
Injects phenotype information into long-range connectome routing and attention regulation for neuropsychiatric prediction.

Physiological signal explanations

ICML 2026Time Series Saliency Maps
Explains wearable heart-rate extraction, EEG seizure detection, and forecasting through domain-appropriate attributions.

Problem-specific AI discovery

ICML 2026TTT-Discover
Reports state-of-the-art discoveries across mathematics, GPU kernels, algorithm design, and single-cell denoising.

Research workflow automation

ICML 2026PaperBanana
Benchmarks methodology-diagram generation for AI scientists through PaperBananaBench.

Robust medical time-series annotation

ICML 2026Ranking Time Series
Targets low-agreement expert annotation, especially in medical time series, through relative judgments and elastic similarity.

Sleep-language foundation models

ICML 2026SleepLM
Aligns multimodal polysomnography with natural language using contrastive alignment, captioning, and signal reconstruction.

Time-series degenerate heads

ICML 2026Universal Redundancies in TSFMs
Identifies heads responsible for motif parroting and seasonality bias using stable-rank intrinsic ablation.

Transferable peptide generative models

ICML 2026Autoregressive Boltzmann Generators
Introduces Robin, a 132M-parameter transferable ArBG model with improved zero-shot energy-distance performance on peptide systems.

Unsupervised biosignal transfer

ICLR 2026BioX-Bridge
Uses cross-modal physiological correlations to transfer knowledge across biosignal modalities when labeled target data are scarce.
