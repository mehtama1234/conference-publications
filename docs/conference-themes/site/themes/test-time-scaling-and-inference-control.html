Problem

The shared problem is that simple benchmark accuracy no longer explains what is happening. The papers in this group isolate a narrower failure mode, capability, or design constraint.

Approaches

The approaches vary by paper, but the common pattern is to define a sharper object of study, build a method around that object, and test whether it changes behavior under controlled conditions.

Connecting read

Read this page as a map of the local evidence rows, not as a claim that the theme is as central as the largest conference themes.

SUBTHEMES

What the papers are actually about

Adaptive dLLM block scheduling

ICLR 2026AdaBlock-dLLM
Uses confidence dynamics and volatility bands to adjust semi-autoregressive diffusion decoding blocks at runtime.

Adaptive non-autoregressive decoding

ICML 2026Learning Unmasking Policies
Outperforms hand-tuned unmasking heuristics in full-diffusion language generation.

Adaptive parallel reasoning

ICML 2026ThreadWeaver
Uses parallel trajectory generation, trie rollouts, and RL to reduce CoT latency while preserving math accuracy.

Affinity-gated discrete diffusion fine-tuning

ICML 2026TD3B
Combines a target-aware Direction Oracle, soft affinity gate, and amortized fine-tuning for functional molecular control.

Attention-state adaptive reasoning stop

ICML 2026ASAG
Uses attention distributions to infer when further CoT is redundant or harmful.

Bandit proposals for coordinated search

ICML 2026NonZero
Derives NonUCT with sublinear local-regret guarantees for graph-local optima under search budgets.

Cache-friendly diffusion language decoding

ICML 2026WeDLM
Uses causal attention, topological reordering, and streaming commitment to make DLLM parallel decoding prefix-cache compatible.

Collaboration architecture as control surface

ICML 2026OMAC
Uses Semantic Initializer and Contrastive Comparator actors to optimize individual or joint MAS design dimensions.

Compute-indexed reasoning RL

ICML 2026MaxRL
Interpolates between expected-reward RL and maximum likelihood as sampling compute scales.

Compute-parameter tradeoff in latent deliberation

ICML 2026PonderLM-2
Shows latent thought steps can outperform simply doubling parameters under matched inference cost.

Convex optimal multi-draft speculative sampling

ICLR 2026Global Resolution
Reduces exponential OT verification to a convex problem in at most vocabulary-size variables.

Coverage-aware model and decoding selection

ICLR 2026Coverage Principle
Explains why coverage can predict downstream test-time scaling better than cross entropy.

Diffusion language flexibility trap

ICML 2026JustGRPO
Finds arbitrary-order dLLMs can bypass uncertain tokens and prematurely collapse reasoning coverage.

Dual-frequency reasoning-action architecture

ICML 2026LaST0
Separates low-frequency latent reasoning from high-frequency action generation through Mixture-of-Transformers.

Early-commit decoding for diffusion language models

ICLR 2026Prophet
Uses top-2 confidence gaps to decide when DLM refinement can stop and remaining tokens can decode at once.

Early-stopped internal best-of-N decoding

ICML 2026NAD
Predicts correctness within 32 generated tokens and prunes trajectories to cut token usage by 99%.

Efficient reward-aligned flow sampling

ICLR 2026GLASS Flows
Samples Markov transitions through an inner flow model recovered from a pretrained model without retraining.

Elastic speculative decoding scheduling

ICML 2026ECHO
Treats high-concurrency speculative decoding as sparse-gated batch-level super-tree scheduling.

Energy minimization as scalable thinking

ICLR 2026Energy-Based Transformers
Learns input-candidate compatibility and predicts by optimizing candidates until convergence.

Exploration through rubric guidance

ICML 2026RGR-GRPO
Reports stable entropy and improved pass@k by expanding exploration beyond purely verifiable online RL.

Grounded student-progress rewards

ICML 2026SOAR
Rewards teacher models by actual student improvement, avoiding proxy-driven instability and diversity collapse.

Hyperparameter-free distribution-aware decoding

ICLR 2026p-less
Sets token truncation thresholds from the full probability distribution at each step.

Inference-time compute for embodied world models

ICLR 2026WoW!
Finds additional planning compute can substantially improve closed-loop world-model performance.

Internalized structured reasoning patterns

ICML 2026Ctrl-R
Uses power-scaled importance weights to learn from exploratory reasoning trajectories while stabilizing RL.

Latency-quality RL for speech generation

ICML 2026Hibiki-Zero
Uses GRPO to optimize real-time translation latency while preserving translation quality, speaker identity, and naturalness.

Long-horizon reasoning curricula

ICML 2026h1
Composes short-horizon tasks into dependency chains and trains with outcome-only curriculum RL to improve long-horizon reasoning.

Lookahead reward-guided diffusion sampling

ICML 2026LiDAR Sampling
Computes expected future reward from marginal samples and guides diffusion particles without neural backpropagation.

Lossless hierarchical speculative decoding

ICLR 2026HSD
Balances excess and deficient probability mass across accessible branches to increase accepted draft tokens.

Low-entropy symbolic-token protection

ICML 2026ReQAT
Focuses QAT on digits/operators and RoPE-consistent KV cache transforms to prevent reasoning-error cascades.

Low-pass prompt-control relaxation

ICML 2026RelaxFlow
Interprets relaxed prompt control as low-pass filtering of the generative vector field.

MCMC-style base-model reasoning sampler

ICLR 2026Reasoning with Sampling
Uses the base model's own likelihoods to elicit reasoning gains without training, curated data, or verifiers.

Memory-bandwidth sparse LLM kernels

ICML 2026MACKO-SpMV
Identifies SpMV memory bandwidth as the bottleneck and reduces storage overhead for pruned LLMs.

Mixture-of-paths scientific sampling

ICML 2026Chamaileon
Optimizes one binder sequence across contexts despite scarce multi-conformational paired data.

Non-autoregressive reasoning systems

ICML 2026Enhancing Reasoning for Diffusion LLMs via DMPO
Treats diffusion LLMs as high-throughput alternatives requiring bespoke reasoning-oriented post-training algorithms.

Non-parametric agent adaptation

ICML 2026Skill-Pro
Uses semantic gradients and PPO-gated verification to improve agent behavior without model parameter updates.

On-the-fly prior adaptation

ICML 2026Distribution Transformer
Performs approximate Bayesian inference with changing priors in milliseconds instead of retraining.

Online reliability under scarce strong checks

ICML 2026Weak-Strong Verification
Controls acceptance and rejection errors while reducing exhaustive strong-verification cost.

Parallel random post-training

ICML 2026Neural Thickets
Samples, selects, and ensembles nearby parameter vectors as a competitive alternative to iterative PPO/GRPO-style adaptation.

Parallel recurrent-depth sampling

ICML 2026Recurrent Diffusion Sampler
Uses diffusion forcing to decode new tokens while refining multiple latent token states in parallel.

Plan-answer interleaved reasoning

ICML 2026PLAINTAIN
Externalizes step-by-step plans before continued reasoning to enable early feedback and reduce response latency.

Planner-aware diffusion language training

ICLR 2026PAPL
Derives a planned ELBO so DLM denoisers are trained under the non-uniform reverse paths used by inference planners.

Prefill-decode steering mismatch

ICML 2026DecodeShare
Shows decode-time steering signals are more causally relevant than prefill-derived proxies.

Program-of-layers inference

ICML 2026PoLar
Dynamically skips or repeats pretrained LLM layers as input-specific execution programs.

Protein binder test-time optimization

ICLR 2026Complexa
Unifies generative binder design with hallucination-style inference-time optimization under normalized compute budgets.

Q-value activation steering for CoT

ICML 2026Hierarchical Thinking
Uses sparse sentence-boundary steering to improve reasoning with far fewer interventions than greedy baselines.

Reasoning chains as intrinsic-dimensionality reduction

ICML 2026Reasoning Dimensionality
Shows effective CoT strategies lower the minimum model dimensions needed for task accuracy.

Reasoning mechanisms versus heuristics

ICML 2026Base Models Know How to Reason
Full text reports hybrids recover about 76% of RL-trained gaps but about 11% of SFT-distilled gaps, suggesting RL teaches when to use existing mechanisms.

Reasoning-loop failure mechanisms

ICML 2026Reasoning Loops
Explains repetitive chains through learning errors, cyclic actions, and temporally correlated Transformer mistakes.

Sample-efficient workflow tuning

ICML 2026MASPOB
Reduces multi-agent prompt search from exponential combinatorics to linear subproblems under limited evaluation budgets.

Scalable RL recipe extrapolation

ICLR 2026ScaleRL
Demonstrates predictable validation scaling up to a 100,000 GPU-hour RL run.

Selective inference-time steering

ICML 2026Thinking in Flow
Uses uncertainty-capacity and direction gates for low-intrusion trajectory-level corrections during generation.

Self-supervised objective-evaluation optimizer

ICML 2026FlowOptimizer
Trains from objective evaluations without solution labels and generalizes from low to higher-dimensional problems.

Temporal abstraction for long-horizon RL

ICML 2026SOL
Uses scalable learned options to outperform flat agents in complex long-horizon environments.

Test-time training for discovery

ICML 2026TTT-Discover
Uses reinforcement learning during test-time search to adapt an open LLM to one scientific or engineering problem.

Token-level ensemble routing

ICML 2026ME Ensemble
Recasts LLM ensembling as routing and reports 1.78x-2.68x faster generation.

Tournament-graph ranking agents

ICML 2026BlitzRank
Aggregates k-wise comparisons into preference graphs and transitive closure for certifiable top-m ranking.

Training-free VLA test-time control

ICML 2026SCALE
Avoids verifiers and multi-rollout TTS by using the model's own output distribution as an inference-control signal.

Training-free kinetic trajectory shaping

ICML 2026KPE/KTS
Boosts early motion and softens late landing to improve generation quality while reducing memorization.

Training-free reasoning improvement

ICML 2026The Tell-Tale Norm
Improves reasoning by allocating or steering computation using model-internal norm signals without extra training data.

Variable compute for horizon generalization

ICML 2026Role of Computation in RL
Shows increased policy compute improves longer-horizon generalization against larger static baselines.

Verifier-guided sequential visual refinement

ICLR 2026OmniVerifier
Uses universal verification for iterative image generation and editing improvement beyond Best-of-N.

Wasserstein geometry-aware decoding

ICML 2026Top-W
Uses token-embedding Wasserstein regularization to choose truncation sets during LLM sampling.

Zero-shot skill composition

ICML 2026Skill Neologisms
Shows independently trained skill neologisms can compose zero-shot with OOD skills and with each other.
