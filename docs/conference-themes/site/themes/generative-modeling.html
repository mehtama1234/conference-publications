Problem

Generation is no longer just images or text. It includes speech, 3D scenes, molecules, trajectories, videos, code-like structures, and decision policies.

Approaches

The papers use diffusion, flows, autoregression, latent structures, watermarking, safety guidance, few-step samplers, and hybrid physical or symbolic constraints.

Connecting read

The field is pushing generative models from content production toward controllable synthesis under real constraints.

SUBTHEMES

What the papers are actually about

Alignment-guided diffusion score matching

ICML 2026AGSM
Integrates contrastive text-image alignment directions directly into the diffusion score-matching objective.

Capacity allocation for imbalanced diffusion

ICLR 2026Capacity Manipulation
Identifies majority-class capacity monopolization as a driver of minority-class degradation in diffusion models.

Constant-memory linear-attention video diffusion

ICLR 2026SANA-Video
Uses Linear DiT and block-wise constant-memory state for high-resolution minute-long video generation.

Continuous-time diffusion forecasting and imputation

ICML 2026LLapDiff
Avoids re-gridding and sequential solvers while supporting horizon-wide forecasts and missing-value queries.

Continuous-token autoregressive image generation

ICLR 2026NextStep-1
Pairs a 14B AR model with a flow-matching head to predict continuous image tokens without VQ loss.

Cross-modal energy-based prediction

ICLR 2026Energy-Based Transformers
Applies energy-based inference across discrete and continuous modalities with strong OOD gains.

Decoder-only masked diffusion language modeling

ICML 2026Any-Order GPT
Evaluates MDMs as any-order autoregressive models inside GPT-style architectures.

Distribution-preserving watermark design

ICLR 2026Spherical Watermark
Uses spherical mapping, orthogonal rotation, and chi-square radius scaling to reconstruct multivariate Gaussian noise from watermark code.

Error-recycling long-video generation

ICLR 2026Stable Video Infinity
Trains DiTs on self-generated error trajectories to correct autoregressive drift over ultra-long videos.

Evidence-calibrated diffusion safety

ICML 2026Concept Removal Guidance
Estimates unwanted concept presence from noise predictions and adaptively calibrates negative guidance during denoising.

Fair diffusion model composition

ICML 2026Divide-and-Denoise
Coordinates multiple pretrained diffusion models by allocating noisy-sample regions through a fair division game during sampling.

Fast 3D scene generation

ICLR 2026FlashWorld
Combines dual-mode multi-view diffusion, direct 3D Gaussian generation, and cross-mode distillation for 10-100x faster scene generation.

Fisher-Rao diffusion edit distance

ICLR 2026Diffusion Spacetime
Derives geodesics over denoising distributions and applies them to molecular transition path sampling.

Flexible video generation regimes

ICML 2026Flex-Forcing
Unifies bidirectional and autoregressive video diffusion through flexible chunking over time and denoising steps.

Flow matching with graphical latent structure

ICLR 2026Structured Flow Autoencoders
Augments continuous normalizing flows with graphical models to jointly learn likelihood and posterior.

Flow-based human geometry distributions

ICLR 2026Generative Human Geometry Distribution
Encodes geometry distributions as 2D feature maps over SMPL domains for scalable human generation.

Forward-process RL for diffusion models

ICLR 2026DiffusionNFT
Uses flow matching and positive-negative generations to optimize diffusion models without likelihood estimation.

Generative Wasserstein flows

ICML 2026GWF
Unifies broad generative algorithms as parametric JKO schemes over distribution divergences.

Guidance-potential posterior initialization

ICML 2026DivIn
Samples initial noise with Langevin dynamics to steer diffusion and flow models away from collapsing modes.

Half-order diffusion fine-tuning

ICLR 2026RLR
Uses recursive likelihood-ratio gradients to fine-tune diffusion models with unbiased lower-variance estimates.

Hybrid amortized-particle sampling

ICML 2026Reinforced SMC
Uses SMC-generated off-policy data, adaptive tempering, and replay to stabilize neural samplers.

Instruction-based video editing benchmark and data

ICLR 2026EditVerse
Curates 232K video-editing samples and introduces EditVerseBench for diverse editing tasks.

Iterative denoising error dynamics

ICML 2026Error Propagation in Quantized Diffusion Models
Frames PTQ degradation as stepwise quantization errors accumulating through the denoising trajectory.

Latent Fourier controls for music generation

ICLR 2026LatentFT
Uses latent-space Fourier transforms and frequency masking to control musical structure across timescales.

Learnable generation order

ICML 2026Unifying Masked Diffusion Models
Unifies masked diffusion, autoregressive, and block diffusion generation processes and jointly learns context-dependent token order.

Locality-aware parallel AR image decoding

ICLR 2026LPD
Uses arbitrary generation ordering and position query tokens to reduce image generation steps sharply.

Mode-preserving local-to-global composition

ICLR 2026CDGS
Avoids averaging incompatible local modes in long-horizon robot plans, panoramas, and videos.

Native adaptive diffusion decoding for CO

ICLR 2026NEXCO
Makes adaptive solution expansion the intrinsic iterative unmasking process of a masked diffusion solver.

Negative extrapolation from self-training collapse

ICLR 2026Neon
Fine-tunes on self-generated data, then reverses the degraded update direction to improve image generation.

Next-token diffusion for expressive speech

ICLR 2026VibeVoice
Uses ultra-low-frame-rate continuous speech tokens and turn-taking labels for scalable podcast synthesis.

Numerical inversion for editing and sampling

ICML 2026Rex
Full text reports near-machine-precision reconstruction and benefits for image editing and Boltzmann sampling.

ODE-efficient stochastic reward adaptation

ICLR 2026GLASS Flows
Combines ODE-style efficiency with stochastic evolution for inference-time reward alignment in text-to-image generation.

Pareto-conditioned diffusion for offline MOO

ICLR 2026PCD
Treats offline multi-objective optimization as conditional sampling over desired tradeoffs.

Partition generative modeling without masks

ICLR 2026PGM
Splits tokens into noninteracting groups to retain parallel any-order generation without processing mask tokens.

Pathway-level discrete flow for chemical design

ICLR 2026ReaSyn
Refines full synthesis pathways with insertion, deletion, and substitution operations.

Perceptual reward models

ICML 2026UniPercept
Trains a perceptual MLLM baseline that can serve as a plug-and-play reward model for text-to-image generation.

Projection-free constrained diffusion

ICML 2026Efficient Diffusion via Landing
Uses a landing mechanism to enforce equality and inequality constraints on nonconvex feasible sets without costly projections.

RL-learned diffusion unmasking

ICML 2026Learning Unmasking Policies
Formalizes dLLM token unmasking as an MDP and trains a lightweight policy from token confidences.

Real-time interactive motion-controlled video

ICLR 2026MotionStream
Distills a bidirectional motion-controlled teacher into a causal streaming student with sub-second latency.

Reference-guided Live Photo restoration

ICLR 2026LiveMoments
Restores reselected video frames using structural and textural guidance from the original high-quality key photo.

Reward-finetuned 3D decodability

ICLR 2026VIST3A
Aligns generated video latents so they decode into consistent Gaussian splats or pointmaps.

Semantic confidence-guided unmasking

ICLR 2026AdaBlock-dLLM
Reduces late decoding overhead and premature token commitment in diffusion LLM inference.

State-aliasing-aware GFlowNets

ICML 2026Path-dependent DAI
Extends amortized sampler learning beyond Markov state assumptions to improve exploration and convergence.

Stein-control estimator unification

ICML 2026RFM
Shows noise- and gradient-expectation diffusion-policy objectives are instances of one estimator class.

Training-free near-lossless diffusion transformer acceleration

ICLR 2026HyCa
Speeds FLUX, HunyuanVideo, Qwen-Image, and Qwen-Image-Edit without retraining.

Training-inference path alignment for DLMs

ICLR 2026PAPL
Reports gains across protein, text, and code generation by modifying the masked discrete diffusion loss.

Unified discrete diffusion noise kernels

ICML 2026XDLM
Bridges masked and uniform-noise diffusion with a stationary kernel recovering both as special cases.

Unified safety-guided diffusion and flow

ICLR 2026SGF
Recasts Shielded Diffusion and Safe Denoiser as energy-based negative guidance against unsafe samples.

Universal inverse distillation for matching models

ICLR 2026RealUID
Distills diffusion, flow, bridge, and stochastic-interpolant matching models under one framework.

Variational insertion-order generation

ICML 2026Insertion Process
Learns where, what, and when to insert via permutation-based variational inference for variable-length sequences.

Wasserstein guarantees for diffusion flow matching

ICML 2026DFM Bounds
Extends DFM convergence analysis to 2-Wasserstein distance under score integrability and weak log-concavity.
